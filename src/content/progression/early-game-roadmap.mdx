Your first 50-100 hours as an ironman set the foundation for everything that follows. Get this right and you'll have a smooth ride into mid-game. Get it wrong and you'll be backtracking constantly.

## The Opening Moves

### Wintertodt First

Start Wintertodt early in your account. Here's why:

- **Supply crates are valuable.** Seeds, logs, ores, gems, and herbs that fund your early Farming, Construction, and Crafting
- **Firemaking has no other use.** Get it out of the way while you're still excited about the account
- **It's social and low-intensity.** Good content for learning the ironman mindset

Stop at 85 Firemaking unless you're chasing the pet. The supply crates scale with your other skills, so there's actually value in coming back later with higher stats if you want more crates.

<Callout type="tip">
Since the [October 2024 update](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Update:Wintertodt_Updates_%26_Varlamore_Drop_Rates), Wintertodt damage scales with your Hitpoints level - so the old "do it at 10 HP" advice no longer applies. You can start Wintertodt whenever it fits your progression.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Bank everything. Seriously. That includes the low-level seeds and gems that seem worthless now.
</Callout>

### The Quest Sprint

After Wintertodt, shift to questing. Quests give the best XP/hour at low levels and unlock content you'll need constantly.

**First priorities:**
1. **Fairy rings** (Fairytale I + start of II) - teleports everywhere
2. **Spirit trees** (Tree Gnome Village) - more teleports
3. **Graceful** (Rooftops to ~60 Agility) - run energy for everything

**High-value early quests:**
- Waterfall Quest (Attack/Strength)
- Witch's House (Hitpoints)
- Fight Arena + Tree Gnome Village + Grand Tree (Attack)
- The Dig Site → Bone Voyage (Fossil Island access)

<Callout type="efficiency">
The efficiency route has you complete most F2P quests, then members quests in a specific order that chains requirements together. See the Oziris guide for the exact sequence.
</Callout>

<Callout type="casual">
If you're not racing, just focus on the major unlocks first. You can circle back to quest cape later - the important thing is having fairy rings and basic teleport options.
</Callout>

## Skills to Train vs Quest

A rough guideline: if you can get a skill to 30+ through quests, do that instead of training.

| Skill | Quest XP Route | Training After |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Attack | Waterfall, Fight Arena, Tree Gnome Village | [Slayer](/skilling/slayer/) |
| Strength | Waterfall | [Slayer](/skilling/slayer/) |
| Magic | Witch's Potion, Imp Catcher, then burst/barrage | Alching, bursting |
| Prayer | Restless Ghost, Priest in Peril | Ensouled heads |
| Crafting | Sheep Herder, Observatory | Charter ships |

## Common Mistakes

- **Training combat manually.** Just quest to base 40s, then do [Slayer](/skilling/slayer/)
- **Skipping Bone Voyage.** Fossil Island access is worth the grind
- **Not getting graceful first.** Every hour without graceful costs you run energy
- **Over-staying Wintertodt.** 85 is enough; come back later with better stats

## What's Next

Once you've got:
- 85 Firemaking
