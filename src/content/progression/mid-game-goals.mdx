You've finished the [early game grind](/progression/early-game-roadmap/). You have graceful, fairy rings, and base 50s in combat. You've done the essential quests. Now what?

Mid-game is where most OSRS ironmen lose direction. Unlike early game (clear quest progression) or late game (chase specific BIS items), mid-game is a web of competing goals with no obvious order. Quest cape? Slayer grind? Corrupted Gauntlet? All valid, none clearly "first."

This guide breaks down mid-game ironman progression, gives you a framework for prioritising goals, and helps you avoid common traps that waste time.

## Defining Mid-Game

**You're in mid-game when:**
- Early game foundation is complete (graceful, fairy rings, base 40-50s)
- Quest cape is achievable (or you're close)
- You're building toward self-sufficiency in PvM

**You've reached late game when:**
- You have consistent raid KC (ToA/CoX)
- Core gear is complete (trident, bowfa/ranged alternative, prims, zenytes)
- Your goals shift to BIS chasing (mega rares) and elite diaries

The middle ground is messy by design. Mid-game is about **unlocking options**, not following a linear path.

## Best Mid-Game Goals for Ironman (Quest Cape, Slayer, Gear)

### Best Starting Goal: Quest Cape (The North Star)

Quest cape is the single best mid-game goal because it:
- **Unlocks everything.** Prif, fairy rings, spirit trees, diaries, teleports
- **Gives structure.** No decision paralysis when you're working toward a clear end state
- **Forces balanced stats.** You'll naturally hit skill thresholds needed for mid-game content

**Key quest unlocks:**
- **Song of the Elves** → Prif access, Corrupted Gauntlet, crystal gear
- **While Guthix Sleeps** → Tormented demons, demonbane weapons (for late-game bosses like Doom and Yama)
- **Dragon Slayer II** → Vorkath access, Ava's assembler
- **Recipe for Disaster** → Barrows gloves (if not done yet)

You don't need quest cape *immediately*, but it should be your default answer to "what do I work on next?" See our [Early Ironman Quests](/progression/early-quests/) guide for optimal quest order.

---

### Best Combat Progression: The Slayer Ladder (58-93 Milestones)

Slayer is the backbone of mid-game. It gives you combat XP, GP, supplies, and essential gear drops. This is where you'll spend 100-200 hours.

**Critical milestones:**

| Level | Unlock | Why It Matters |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| **58** | Black Mask | 16.67% damage and accuracy boost on-task (massive upgrade) |
| **75** | Gargoyles | Very AFK, consistent GP and alchables |
| **85** | Abyssal Demons | Abyssal whip (BIS melee weapon until late game) |
| **87** | Kraken | Trident of the seas (mandatory for Zulrah, raids, any magic PvM) |
| **91** | Cerberus | Primordial boots (BIS melee boots, pre-endgame requirement) |
| **93** | Smoke Devils | Occult necklace (BIS magic neck slot) |

**The 87 → 91 grind is non-negotiable.** Trident unlocks mid-game PvM, and prims are expected before you're considered "late game ready." See our full [Slayer guide](/skilling/slayer/) for efficient task management.

<Callout type="tip">
Don't block Black Demons if you still need zenyte shards from demonic gorillas. Skip them instead - you'll want the flexibility to kill them off-task later for zenytes.
</Callout>

### Best Gear Unlocks for Mid-Game (Fire Cape to Bowfa)

Mid-game is when you transition from quest rewards and basic gear to self-obtained upgrades.

**Essential gear:**

| Item | Source | Impact |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Fire cape** | Fight Caves | First real DPS test, BIS cape until Inferno |
| **Barrows gloves** | Recipe for Disaster | BIS gloves for most content |
| **Zenytes (4x)** | Demonic Gorillas | Anguish, Torment bracelet, Suffering, Necklace |
| **Trident** | 87 Slayer (Kraken) | Mandatory for magic PvM |
| **Abyssal whip** | 85 Slayer | BIS melee weapon until fang/rapier (see [Melee Training guide](/skilling/melee/) for progression) |
| **Primordial boots** | 91 Slayer (Cerberus) | BIS melee boots |
| **Elite Void** | Pest Control | BIS for ToB, Colosseum, many bosses |
| **Bow of Faerdhinen** | Corrupted Gauntlet (optional) | Single largest DPS upgrade in the game |

**The Bowfa Question:**

Corrupted Gauntlet for bowfa is the most divisive mid-game decision. Bowfa is a **mid-game grind that gates late-game efficiency** - you get it before raids, not during them.

**Arguments for doing CG early:**
- Bowfa is the single largest gear upgrade an ironman can get
- Makes *everything* faster: Slayer, Zulrah, GWD, Cerberus
- Unlocks content that's hard without it (some CoX rooms, efficient DKs, GWD)
- Required for efficient late-game PvM

**Arguments for skipping/delaying CG:**
- Enhanced seed is 1/400 drop (gives room to be genuinely unlucky)
- You can start entry-mode raids with trident/whip/zenytes (but it's noticeably harder)
- CG has a mechanical skill floor-takes time to learn, so some players prefer getting more stats first

<Callout type="efficiency">
**Efficiency route:** Get bowfa as soon as you complete Song of the Elves. Accept the grind. Everything after this is 20-30% faster.
</Callout>

<Callout type="casual">
**Sanity route:** Do 87 Slayer first (get trident), then decide if you want to tackle CG. If you're burning out, skip it and start raids - you can always come back later.
</Callout>

## Playstyle Decision Matrix: Which Path Suits You?

Mid-game progression is personal. Use this matrix to find the path that matches your priorities:

| Playstyle | Focus | Recommended Path | Key Decision |
|-----------|-------|------------------|--------------|
| **Efficiency-focused** | Max DPS, min backtracking | Quest Cape → CG → Slayer to 91 → Raids | Start CG early (pre-87 Slayer) for maximum impact later |
| **Balanced** | Steady progress, some efficiency | Quest Cape → Slayer to 87 → CG (optional) → Raids | Do Slayer first for gear, reassess CG based on enjoyment |
| **Casual/Enjoyment** | Varied content, no burnout | Mix activities by mood (Slayer, Quests, Diaries) → Slayer to 87 → Start Raids | Skip CG entirely if it's not fun; gear matters less than enjoyment |
| **Goal-chaser** | Clear milestones, completion | Quest Cape → 91 Slayer → 4 Zenytes → Raid-ready → Optional CG | Fast-track raid entry without CG; come back for endgame optimization |
| **Explorer** | Try everything, learn the game | Pick favorite content → Sprinkle in Slayer → Natural progression | No fixed path; follow curiosity while hitting key milestones |

**Key insight:** Quest Cape and Slayer to 87 are universal. CG and hard diaries are flexible based on your playstyle. Don't force content you hate - mid-game is long enough to find your own rhythm.

## Varlamore's Role in Mid-Game

Varlamore content (released 2024) added several new mid-game goals:

### Prayer Training (70 for Piety)
**Blessed bone shards** from Hunter's Guild are now the go-to prayer training method:
- 5-6 XP per shard (scales with Prayer level)
- Completely safe (no Wilderness risk)
- 46 Hunter to start, 72 Hunter for best rates
- Detailed breakdown in our [Prayer Training Guide](/skilling/prayer/)

Getting **Piety at 70 Prayer** is a massive melee DPS boost and should be a priority for all mid-game ironmen.

### Moons of Peril (75+ Combat)
Mid-level boss that drops **moon armour sets**:
- **Blood Moon** (melee) - stepping stone to Bandos
- **Eclipse Moon** (ranged) - good ranged DPS gear
- **Blue Moon** (magic) - magic DPS option

These armour sets are solid mid-game alternatives if you don't want to grind traditional content.

### Tormented Demons (80+ Combat)
Requires **While Guthix Sleeps** quest. Drops **tormented synapse** (1/500) which crafts:
- **Emberlight** (melee demonbane)
- **Purging Staff** (magic demonbane)
- **Scorching Bow** (ranged demonbane)

These weapons are **essential for late-game bosses** like Doom of Mokhaiotl and Yama (demonbane weapons are 80%+ more effective). Getting at least one synapse is a late-mid-game goal.

### Mixed Hide Armor (60 Ranged, 50 Defence)
Crafted from Hunter's Guild furs in [Varlamore](/guides/varlamore/) (72 Hunter + 72 Crafting for top, or pay Pellem 20k):
- Better tank armour than Barrows for most ironman content
- Provides stab attack bonuses
- Low-cost, accessible alternative to traditional melee armour

<Callout type="tip">
Varlamore content is **optional but strong**. Blessed shards for [prayer training](/skilling/prayer/) and mixed hide armour are easy wins. Moons of Peril and tormented demons can wait if you're focused on Slayer first.
</Callout>

## Best Hard Diaries for Mid-Game Ironman (Priority & Impact)

Hard diaries are natural mid-game checkpoints because their requirements align with mid-game stats (base 70s).

**Worth prioritising:**
- **Morytania** → 50% more Barrows runes + bonecrusher (do this before heavy Barrows farming)
- **Desert** → Zahur makes unfinished potions for 200gp (do this before Herblore training)
- **Ardougne** → 10% global pickpocket success (massive for Thieving)
- **Western Provinces** → Private red chinchompa area, crystal halberd access
- **Fremennik** → Noted adamantite bars from GWD aviansies, Waterbirth Island teleport

You don't need *all* hard diaries before late game, but completing 3-5 naturally as you progress is common for mid-game ironmen. See the [Achievement Diary Guide](/guides/achievement-diaries/) for a full priority ranking.

## Best Playstyle for Mid-Game: Decision Framework

With so many competing goals, how do you choose what to do next?

### 1. Prioritize by Bottlenecks

Some unlocks gate multiple other goals:
- **Quest cape** unlocks Prif (→ CG), diaries, teleports
- **Trident (87 Slayer)** unlocks Zulrah, efficient Barrows, raids
- **Piety (70 Prayer)** boosts *all* melee content

**Do bottleneck goals first.**

### 2. Prioritize by Impact

Not all upgrades are equal:
- **Piety > most gear upgrades** (universal 20%+ melee DPS boost)
- **Bowfa > any other ranged upgrade** (massive jump in capability)
- **Barrows gloves > most jewelry** (best gloves in the game)

**Do high-impact unlocks early.**

### 3. Follow Your Enjoyment

Mid-game is long. If you hate Pest Control, don't force elite void early. If CG makes you miserable, do Slayer instead.

**Burnout costs more than inefficiency.**

## Common Mid-Game Traps

**1. Grinding 99s You Don't Need**

Don't push for 99 Cooking, Fletching, or Firemaking in mid-game. Focus on **unlocks**, not levels.

**2. Over-Farming Zulrah Before Raids**

Zulrah is good for scales and GP, but don't camp it for 1,000 KC before starting raids. Get your first ToA KC, then return to Zulrah if you need more scales.

**3. Skipping Quest Cape "For Later"**

Every month you delay quest cape costs you fairy rings, teleports, and diary progress. Just do it.

**4. Burning Out on CG**

Going 500+ KC dry on enhanced seed is soul-crushing. If you're burning out, take a break. Do some Slayer. Come back later.

**5. Neglecting Construction and Herblore**

These buyables feel expensive, but **83 Construction** (jewelry box, ornate pool) and **78 Herblore** (super combat potions) are game-changers. Don't delay them forever - they unlock huge quality-of-life improvements.

## Sequencing: A Suggested Order

Everyone's path will differ, but here's a common sequence that minimizes backtracking:

### Phase 1: Foundation (First)
1. Finish any remaining RFD subquests → Barrows gloves
2. Get fire cape (if not done)
3. Push for 70 Prayer via Varlamore blessed shards
4. Start working toward quest cape (prioritise SoTE if you want CG)

### Phase 2: The Big Grinds (Order Flexible)
5. **Option A:** Do Corrupted Gauntlet for bowfa (50-500 KC)
6. **Option B:** Slayer to 87 for trident, *then* decide on CG
7. Get first zenyte shard from demonic gorillas
8. Complete quest cape (or get very close)
9. Elite void if you enjoy Pest Control

### Phase 3: Raid Prep (Final Push)
10. Slayer to 91 (Cerberus / prims)
11. Finish all 4 zenytes
12. 93 Slayer for occult necklace (optional but strong)
13. First ToA attempts (you're ready with trident, whip, zenytes)
14. Complete While Guthix Sleeps, get tormented synapse (for late-game demonbane)

## When You're Ready for Late Game

You're transitioning when:
- You have your first 5-10 ToA or CoX KC
- You're comfortable with raid mechanics
- Your goals shift from unlocks to rare drops (fang, shadow, tbow)
- Elite diaries become the focus
- You're hunting specific BIS items, not broad upgrades

**There's no hard line.** Some players start raids at 87 Slayer with budget gear. Others wait until 95 Slayer with full zenytes and bowfa. Both are valid - bowfa just makes everything significantly easier.

---

## Common Mistakes

- **Waiting too long to start raids.** You don't need BIS gear for entry-mode ToA. Get in there and learn.
- **Forcing the efficiency meta.** If CG makes you miserable, skip it. The game is long - enjoy it.
- **Ignoring Varlamore.** Blessed bone shards and mixed hide armour are low-effort, high-impact.
