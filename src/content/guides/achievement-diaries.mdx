Achievement Diaries are one of the most valuable systems for ironmen  - and one of the easiest to neglect. Each diary region offers permanent, account-wide rewards that compound over thousands of hours of play.

The timing matters more than anything else. The difference between doing Morytania Hard before vs after Barrows is hundreds of thousands of runes. Desert Hard before vs after Herblore training is millions of GP saved on unfinished potions. This guide ranks every diary by ironman impact and tells you exactly when to complete each one.

## 1. Easy Diaries  - Do All of These First

Easy diaries have minimal requirements (base 30-40 stats) and every single one gives useful rewards. Complete them all as early as possible.

| Diary | Key Reward | Why It Matters |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Varrock** | 15 daily battlestaves from Zaff (7k each) | Primary early GP source  - craft into air/fire battlestaves and alch |
| **Ardougne** | Unlimited Ardougne Monastery teleport | Closest teleport to a fairy ring + altar, used constantly |
| **Kourend & Kebos** | Hosidius kitchen access, disease-free Hosidius patches | +5% cooking success, saves time on farming runs |
| **Lumbridge & Draynor** | 30 free low alchs/day, 2 run energy restores | Free GP, saves runes early |
| **Falador** | Falador Shield 1 (25% prayer restore once/day) | Free prayer restore, useful early |
| **Kandarin** | 30 daily flax-to-bowstring exchanges, 5% more marks of grace at Seers' | Bowstrings + Agility bonus |
| **All diaries** | XP lamps (2,500 XP each at 30+) | Put on Herblore, Runecraft, or Prayer |

<Callout type="tip">
Varrock Easy is the single most important Easy diary. 15 battlestaves/day for 7k each is immediate, recurring profit. Do this as soon as you can.
</Callout>

## 2. Medium Diaries  - Natural Mid-Game Checkpoints

Complete these as requirements align (base 50-60 stats). Don't grind for them  - most complete naturally as you quest and train.

| Diary | Key Reward | Why It Matters |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **Varrock** | 30 daily battlestaves, GE teleport toggle | Double the Easy tier GP |
| **Ardougne** | 10% pickpocket success in Ardougne, 3 daily farm patch teleports | Faster Ardy Knights, convenient herb runs |
| **Fremennik** | 10% Miscellania approval boost | Less maintenance on [Kingdom](/guides/miscellania/) |
| **Kandarin** | 5% herb save at Catherby, faster spinning wheel | Every saved herb counts over thousands of runs |
| **Kourend & Kebos** | 5% chance double dense essence, 20 free dynamite/day | Runecrafting efficiency |
| **Falador** | 54 Agility shortcut in Motherlode Mine | MLM efficiency |
| **Morytania** | 5% Slayer Tower XP bonus, ghostspeak amulet on legs | Slayer QoL |

<Callout type="casual">
Don't grind medium diary requirements specifically. Most complete naturally as you quest and train. Check your diary tab every few weeks and finish any you're close to.
</Callout>

## 3. Hard Diaries  - Do These BEFORE These Grinds

This is the most important section of this guide. Hard diaries gate critical content, and completing them at the right time saves enormous amounts of resources.

| Diary | Key Reward | Do BEFORE... | Key Requirements |
|-------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Morytania** | 50% more Barrows runes, bonecrusher | Barrows grinding | 70 Prayer, 71 Agility, Desert Treasure I |
| **Desert** | Zahur makes unfinished potions (200gp), Wesley crushes items (50gp) | Herblore training | 68 Magic, 65 Thieving, 70 Agility |
| **Ardougne** | 10% global pickpocket success | Heavy Thieving (Ardy Knights, Master Farmers, Elves) | 72 Thieving, Mourning's End Part II |
| **Falador** | Noted Giant Mole drops, mole locator | Giant Mole farming (tree seeds) | 72 Slayer, 70 Prayer |
| **Varrock** | 60 daily battlestaves | Anytime  - more GP is always good | 95 Cooking (highest req) |
| **Western Provinces** | Red chinchompa private area, crystal halberd | Red chin hunting, Zulrah | 75 Thieving, Zulrah KC |
| **Kandarin** | 10% herb save Catherby, 10% enchanted bolt proc, Seers' bank teleport | Extended herb farming, PvM with bolts | 75 Smithing, 70 Prayer |
| **Fremennik** | Noted adamantite bars from GWD, Waterbirth teleport, Tan Leather spell | GWD, DKs | 75 Thieving, 72 Magic, Lunar Diplomacy |
| **Kourend & Kebos** | Ash sanctifier (half Prayer XP from ashes), slayer helm acts as Shayzien helm | Demon Slayer tasks | 70 Smithing, 74 Farming |
| **Lumbridge & Draynor** | 10% more Tears of Guthix XP | Anytime  - helps lowest skill | 70 Cooking, 70 Crafting, full RFD |
| **Karamja** | Unlimited Shilo Village mine teleport | Gem mining for Crafting | 53 Agility, 100+ Combat |
| **Wilderness** | Obelisk destination selection, noted wines of zamorak | Wilderness activities | 75 Smithing, 68 Slayer |

<Callout type="warning">
**Morytania Hard before Barrows is the most important timing on this list.** The 50% more runes from every Barrows chest is massive  - over 500 KC that's hundreds of thousands of extra chaos, death, and blood runes. Don't do extensive Barrows without it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Desert Hard is the second most important timing. Zahur turns noted grimy herbs into unfinished potions for 200gp each  - this saves enormous time during Herblore training. Complete it before any serious potion-making push.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hcim">
Hardcore ironmen should prioritise Falador Hard before any Giant Mole attempts. The mole locator from Falador Shield 3 prevents dangerous underground chasing. Without it, you risk death from misclicks or getting lost.
</Callout>

## 4. Elite Diaries  - Late-Game Goals

Elite diaries require high 80s-90s stats. Most ironmen complete these gradually as requirements come naturally through Slayer, bossing, and general progression.

**Ranked by ironman impact:**

| Diary | Key Reward | Why It Matters | Hardest Requirement |
|-------|-----------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Lumbridge & Draynor** | Fairy rings without staff, 6th Slayer block slot, 30 free high alchs/day | Frees inventory slot everywhere, better Slayer tasks | Quest cape (all quests) |
| **Morytania** | Bonecrusher grants full Prayer XP, Harmony herb patch (disease-free) | Massive passive Prayer from Slayer, extra herb patch | 96 Fishing |
| **Kourend & Kebos** | Ash sanctifier grants full Prayer XP, no boots in Karuulm dungeon | Full Prayer XP from demon ashes during Slayer | 95 Slayer |
| **Ardougne** | 84 daily buckets of sand to bank, unlimited farm patch teleports | Huge for Crafting (glassblowing), farming QoL | 91 Smithing, 94 Magic |
| **Fremennik** | Noted DK bones, 15% Miscellania approval boost | Massive Prayer XP from DK trips, Kingdom QoL | 82 RC, all GWD generals |
| **Karamja** | Unlimited Duradel teleport, noted metal dragon bars | Slayer QoL, free bars from tasks | 91 RC |
| **Kandarin** | 15% herb save at Catherby, first 200 coal auto-banked daily | Best herb save in game | 90 Smithing, 87 Magic |
| **Western Provinces** | Nieve/Steve grant same points as Duradel, free Zulrah resurrection/day | Slayer flexibility, HCIM safety | 93 Slayer, 1000 chompies |
| **Varrock** | 120 daily battlestaves | Best daily money  - diminishing returns if GP isn't a bottleneck | 89 Smithing, 95 Cooking |
| **Desert** | Unlimited Nardah/Kalphite teleports, full desert heat protection | Convenience  - KQ access, no waterskins | 95 Fletching, 85 Prayer |
| **Falador** | Disease-free Falador tree patch, larger amethyst mine | Farming QoL, amethyst arrows | 91 Farming, 88 RC |
| **Wilderness** | Noted dragon bones from wilderness dragons, free Resource Area | Alternative Prayer source, niche | 96 Magic, 90 Cooking |

<Callout type="tip">
Lumbridge & Draynor Elite is the best elite diary by far, but it requires a quest cape. If you're naturally working toward quest cape territory, prioritise finishing the remaining quests  - the fairy ring + Slayer block rewards are worth it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="casual">
Don't grind elite diary requirements. The stats (85-96) come naturally from Slayer, bossing, and general account progression. Check your diary tab periodically and complete elites when you notice you're close.
</Callout>

## 5. Diary XP Lamps  - Where to Use Them

Every diary tier gives an Antique Lamp with bonus XP. The XP scales with the diary tier:

| Tier | XP | Minimum Skill Level |
|------|-----|-------------------|
| Easy | 2,500 | 30 |
| Medium | 7,500 | 40 |
| Hard | 15,000 | 50 |
| Elite | 50,000 | 70 |

**Best skills to use lamps on:**

| Skill | Why | When |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Herblore** | Hardest ironman skill, secondary-limited, expensive | Always a good default choice |
| **Runecraft** | Slowest skill, painful diary requirements (91 for Karamja Elite) | If you're far from 77+ RC |
| **Prayer** | Bone/ash-limited for ironmen, no easy passive training | If you need Prayer levels for diary/quest reqs |
| **Construction** | Plank-limited, every level unlocks useful POH rooms | If pushing for specific POH unlocks |
| **Agility** | Slow and tedious, many diary requirements | If you need Agility for a specific diary |

<Callout type="tip">
Herblore is the default choice for most ironmen. If you're not sure, put your lamps on Herblore.
</Callout>

## 6. Combat Achievements  - The Diary-Adjacent System

Combat Achievements work on a points system and reward Ghommal's Hilt upgrades. These function similarly to diary rewards and are worth pursuing alongside diaries:

| Hilt Tier | CA Points | Key Ironman Benefits |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------|
| Tier 2 (Medium) | 161 pts | Nullifies Barrows prayer drain (with hilt equipped) |
| Tier 3 (Hard) | 416 pts | Unlimited GWD teleports, private GWD instances (150k), halved NMZ/Soul Wars imbue costs |
| Tier 4 (Elite) | 1,064 pts | GWD KC reduced to 30, superior spawn rate 1/150 |
| Tier 5 (Master) | 1,904 pts | Thralls last 50% longer, Lucky Penny (5% charge save) |

<Callout type="tip">
Ghommal's Hilt Tier 2 (Barrows prayer drain nullification) pairs perfectly with Morytania Hard. Complete both before heavy Barrows farming for maximum efficiency.
</Callout>

## 7. Requirements  - What Comes Naturally vs Needs Grinding

**Come naturally through ironman gameplay:**
- Combat stats (Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, Magic)  - from Slayer
- Cooking  - you cook everything you eat
- Farming  - herb and tree runs
- Slayer  - core ironman activity
- Thieving  - Ardy Knights and Master Farmers
- Firemaking  - Wintertodt
- Hunter  - birdhouse runs
- Fishing  - Tempoross and food gathering

**Require intentional effort:**
- **Runecraft**  - 91 for Karamja Elite is the most dreaded diary requirement in the game
- **Smithing**  - 91 for Ardougne Elite. [Giants' Foundry](/guides/giants-foundry/) helps significantly
- **Agility**  - 90 for Ardougne Elite. Pure grind, no shortcuts
- **Prayer**  - 85 for Desert Elite. Ironmen must earn all bones/ashes themselves
- **Quest cape**  - for Lumbridge Elite. Often the actual bottleneck, not the skill levels

<Callout type="tip">
Raiments of the Eye (from [Guardians of the Rift](/guides/gotr/)) significantly reduce Runecraft requirements for some diary tasks. With 4 pieces, the Falador Elite RC requirement drops from 88 to 55. Check if your RC diary tasks can be done at lower levels with Raiments.
</Callout>
