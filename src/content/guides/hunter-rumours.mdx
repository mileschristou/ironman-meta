Hunter Rumours are a game-changing Slayer-like training method for Hunter introduced with Varlamore. With optimal block lists, you can achieve 160-250k XP/hour while earning valuable supplies for prayer, herblore, and exclusive rewards like the Quetzin pet.

**At a glance:**
- **Requirements:** 46 Hunter, access to Hunter Guild (Varlamore)
- **Best XP:** Aco Expert block list (235k XP/hr, 24 tasks/hr at 99)
- **Best for ironmen:** Teco Expert (160-200k XP/hr + Herbiboar herbs + prayer supplies)

## What Are Hunter Rumours?

Hunter Rumours are a Slayer-like assignment system from the Hunter Guild in Varlamore's Avium Savannah. Players receive assignments to track down specific creatures across Gielinor, hunt them until obtaining a rare creature part, then return to the guild for Hunter XP and loot sacks containing valuable supplies.

### Requirements to Start

- **Minimum:** 46 Hunter (unboostable)
- **Location:** Hunter Guild, Avium Savannah, Varlamore
- **Access:** Children of the Sun quest completion, 30 Herblore, 27 Construction
- **Unlock:** Speak to Guild Scribe Verity in the Burrow (underneath main guild building)

### How They Work

1. Speak to a Guild Hunter in the Burrow to receive a rumour assignment
2. Travel to the creature's location across Gielinor
3. Hunt the creature using the appropriate method (box trap, falconry, deadfall, tracking, etc.)
4. Continue until you obtain the rare creature part (a jingle plays when received)
5. Return to Hunter Guild to turn in the part for XP and a loot sack

**Average completion time:** 8-10 minutes per rumour

**XP Formula:** (Hunter Level + 5) × Tier Multiplier

## Block Lists: The Key to Optimization

Block lists are the core mechanic that transforms Hunter Rumours from mediocre to exceptional training.

### How Block Lists Work

**Core Mechanic:** Guild hunters will not assign a rumour that's already assigned by another guild hunter.

**Strategy:** Accept unwanted rumours from different hunters and leave them incomplete. These "block" those tasks from being assigned by your primary hunter, narrowing their assignment pool to only high-value creatures.

### How to Set Up a Block List

1. **Start with Huntmaster Gilman (Novice tier)** - He has the largest rumour pool, giving the most flexibility
2. Accept your first unwanted rumour (e.g., Sabre-toothed Kyatt - always block this first)
3. Move to the next tier hunter and accept another unwanted rumour
4. Continue until you've blocked all poor tasks
5. Use your chosen primary hunter for active assignments

**CRITICAL:** Always start your block list with Gilman first. If you accept tasks from other hunters before Gilman, you reduce your blocking options.

### Number of Blocks Available

You can have up to **5-6 block slots** depending on your level:

| Guild Hunter | Tier | Level Req | Use |
|-------------|------|-----------|-----|
| Huntmaster Gilman | Novice | 46 | First block (largest pool) |
| Guild Hunter Cervus | Adept | 57 | Second block |
| Guild Hunter Ornus | Adept | 57 | Third block |
| Guild Hunter Aco | Expert | 72 | Fourth block |
| Guild Hunter Teco | Expert | 72 | Fifth block |
| Guild Hunter Wolf | Master | 91 | Sixth block |

## Creatures to Always Block

If you can block these, you should (prioritized):

### 1. Sabre-toothed Kyatt (55 Hunter)
- **Method:** Pitfall trapping
- **Why block:** Slowest task in the game, requires knife/logs/rope inventory, terrible XP/time
- **Always block first via Gilman**

### 2. Spotted Kebbit (43 Hunter)
- **Method:** Falconry
- **Why block:** Requires travelling to falconer, renting falcon (500 GP), slow completion, inconvenient

### 3. Pyre Fox (57 Hunter)
- **Method:** Deadfall trap
- **Why block:** Slowest Adept tier task, poor XP, annoying inventory requirements

### 4. Sunlight Antelope (72 Hunter)
- **Method:** Pitfall trapping
- **Why block:** Slowest Expert/Master tier task, only useful for hunters' sunlight crossbow upgrades (niche item)

### 5. Sabre-toothed Kebbit (51 Hunter)
- **Method:** Deadfall trap
- **Why block:** Slow completion, poor rewards

## High-Value Creatures (Don't Block)

### Red Chinchompa (63 Hunter)

- **Tiers:** All (Novice, Adept, Expert, Master)
- **Method:** Box trap
- **Why hunt:** Best XP rates, useful chinchompas for Ranged training, Baby chinchompa pet chance (1/95,898)
- **Why never block:** Fastest completion, highest XP/hour

### Herbiboar (80 Hunter)

- **Tiers:** Novice, Expert (Teco only)
- **Method:** Tracking (Fossil Island)
- **XP Rate:** 115-128k Hunter XP/hr, 3,300 Herblore XP/hr with magic secateurs
- **Why hunt:** Best herb source for ironmen, herbs scale with Herblore level (up to torstols), Herbi pet chance (1/6,500)
- **Why block:** Only if pure XP-focused using Aco Expert

### Moonlight Moth (75 Hunter)

- **Tiers:** Novice, Expert, Master
- **Method:** Butterfly net + jar (barehanded at 85)
- **Why hunt:** Restores 22 Prayer points on catch/release, moonlight moth mix ingredients (2-dose, 22 Prayer/dose)
- **Special:** Extremely valuable for ironmen - prayer restoration without prayer potions

### Tecu Salamander (79 Hunter)

- **Tiers:** Novice, Expert, Master
- **Method:** Net trap
- **XP Rate:** 150k XP/hr at 99 Hunter
- **Why hunt:** Very high XP, fast completion, rare tecu salamanders worth 200K+ GP

### Dashing Kebbit (69 Hunter)

- **Tiers:** Novice, Expert, Master
- **Method:** Tracking with hunter pouch
- **Why hunt:** Dashing kebbit meat is 3rd highest healing food (heals 16 HP), restores 10 run energy
- **Useful for:** Ironmen needing food sources

## Rewards and Loot Sacks

### Hunters' Loot Sacks

Each completed rumour rewards a tier-specific loot sack:

| Sack Tier | Average Value | Key Contents |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Basic (Novice) | 5,567 GP | Hunter meats, logs, basic herbs |
| Adept | 17,374 GP | Improved herbs, furs |
| **Expert** | **22,774 GP** | **Noted herbs (4 per roll, 1/15), blessed bone shards** |
| **Master** | **27,512 GP** | **High-tier noted herbs, sun-kissed bones, blessed bone shards** |

**Rare drops from loot sacks:**
- **Guild hunter outfit pieces** (1/50 from Adept+ sacks)
- Huntsman's kit (stores 32 different hunter equipment types)
- Enhanced/Perfected quetzal whistle blueprints
- **Quetzin pet** (Expert+ sacks only, 15x more common at 200m Hunter XP)

### Guild Hunter Outfit

**How to obtain:** 1/50 from hunters' loot sacks (Adept tier or higher)

**Pieces:** Headwear, top, legs, boots (received in order)

**Benefits:**
- **Full set:** +2.5% catch rate for all Hunter creatures
- **Full set:** +5% chance of receiving rare creature parts from rumours
- **Full set:** -5% pity threshold for rare parts

**Pity system:** Rare creature parts become guaranteed after going twice the base drop rate (e.g., 1/20 becomes guaranteed at 40). Full outfit reduces this to 38 attempts.

<Callout type="tip">
The guild hunter outfit significantly improves rumour completion speed. Prioritize earning it early by completing Adept+ rumours. Each piece improves efficiency even before completing the full set.
</Callout>

### Blessed Bone Shards

**Obtained from:** Expert and Master loot sacks

**Prayer XP:**
- 5 Prayer XP with blessed wine
- 6 Prayer XP with blessed sunfire wine

**Accumulation rate:** 20,000-30,000 banked Prayer XP per hour when combined with mining calcified rocks

**Where to use:** Libation Bowl in Civitas illa Fortis (Varlamore)

**Why valuable for ironmen:**
- Alternative to gilded altar (no dragon bones needed)
- Safer than Wilderness Chaos altar
- More AFK than traditional prayer training
- Excellent for mid-level prayer (43-70) before consistent dragon bone sources

**See:** [Prayer training guide](/skilling/prayer/) for blessed bone shards usage

## Strategic Optimization

### XP-Focused Approach (Pure Speed)

**Best Setup:** Aco Expert block list (72-91 Hunter)

**Strategy:**
- Block Teco's pool + Sunlight Antelope + slow creatures
- Available tasks: Red Chinchompa, Orange Salamander, high-XP moths
- **Expected:** 235,000 XP/hour, 24 rumour completions/hour at 99

**When to use:** Racing to 99 Hunter, don't need supplies

### Supplies-Focused Approach (Ironman Recommended)

**Best Setup:** Teco Expert block list (72-91 Hunter)

**Strategy:**
- Block Aco's pool + Sunlight Antelope + slow creatures
- Available tasks: Herbiboar (herbs), Moonlight Moth (prayer), Red Chinchompa (XP)
- **Expected:** 160-200k XP/hour + valuable herb/prayer supplies

**Why best for ironmen:**
- Herbs from Herbiboar scale with Herblore level (ranarr, snapdragon, torstol at high levels)
- Moonlight moths provide prayer restoration for PvM (better than prayer potions early-mid game)
- Still excellent XP rates (only 15-20% slower than pure XP setup)
- Expert loot sacks with noted herbs and blessed bone shards

### Balanced Approach

**Best Setup:** Wolf Master block list (91+ Hunter)

**Strategy:**
- Block Aco and Teco pools to narrow Wolf to 3 high-value tasks
- Available tasks: Red Chinchompa, Moonlight Moth, Tecu Salamander
- **Expected:** 240,000+ XP/hour + Master loot sacks

**Why balanced:**
- Highest XP rates at endgame
- Best loot sacks (noted herbs, sun-kissed bones worth 225-270 Prayer XP each)
- Access to moonlight moths for prayer restoration

## Comparisons

### Hunter Rumours vs Regular Hunting

| Method | XP/Hour | Pros | Cons |
|--------|---------|------|------|
| **Hunter Rumours (Aco Expert)** | 235,000 | Fastest safe XP, loot sacks, variety | Requires block list setup |
| **Hunter Rumours (Teco Expert)** | 160-200,000 | Herbiboar herbs, prayer supplies, good XP | Slightly lower XP than Aco |
| Red Chinchompas (regular) | 130-180,000 | Chinchompas for Ranged, safe | No loot sacks, repetitive |
| Black Chinchompas (Wilderness) | 140-250,000 | Highest XP | Dangerous (PKers), high risk |
| Herbiboar (regular) | 115-128,000 | Herbs, Herblore XP, safe | Lower XP than rumours/chins |

**Verdict:** With optimal block lists, Hunter Rumours provide the best XP rates in safe areas (only black chins in Wilderness are faster, but risky). For ironmen, Teco Expert rumours are strictly better than regular Herbiboar (same herbs + loot sacks + higher XP).

### Hunter Rumours vs Birdhouses

| Aspect | Hunter Rumours | Birdhouses |
|--------|---------------|-----------|
| XP rates | 160-250k XP/hr (active) | ~5-6k XP per run (90 seconds per 50 minutes) |
| Effort | High (active hunting) | Very low (90 seconds every 50 minutes) |
| Rewards | Loot sacks, herbs, prayer supplies, outfit | Bird nests (seeds, crushed nests for brews) |
| Best for | Active training, supplies, pet hunting | Passive XP alongside other activities |

**Verdict:** Do both. Birdhouses are passive and essential for [bird nests](/skilling/hunter/) (Saradomin brews). Hunter Rumours are the best active method at 72+.

## Integration with Other Content

### Blessed Bone Shards for Prayer Training

**The Prayer Training Loop:**
1. Complete Expert/Master Hunter Rumours (160-250k Hunter XP/hr)
2. Open loot sacks → blessed bone shards + sun-kissed bones
3. Mine calcified rocks at Libation Pool (11-20k banked Prayer XP/hr)
4. Sacrifice shards at Libation Bowl (5-6 XP/shard, 225-270 XP/sun-kissed bone)
5. Result: Efficient Hunter + Prayer training simultaneously

**Comparison to traditional prayer:**
- Gilded altar: 1.5-2m Prayer XP/hr (requires dragon bones)
- Wilderness Chaos altar: 3.5x bones (high PK risk)
- Blessed bone shards: 1.2m XP/hr (passive gathering, zero risk, AFK-friendly)

**Best for:** Mid-level prayer (43-70) on ironmen before consistent dragon bone sources

### Moonlight Moths for Prayer Restoration

**Uses:**
- Catching: 22 Prayer points restored (no jar needed)
- Releasing jarred moth: 22 Prayer points restored
- **Moonlight moth mix:** 2-dose potion, 22 Prayer/dose (made with any hunter meat + pestle/mortar)

**Ironman value:**
- Alternative to prayer potions (especially early-mid game)
- PvM trips (mix in inventory for 44 Prayer restoration per item)
- Slayer tasks (cheap prayer restoration)
- Superior to prayer potions at low Herblore levels

**Strategy:** Save moonlight moths from rumour loot sacks, combine with hunter meats for maximum efficiency

### Herbiboar for Herbs

**Doing Herbiboar via Hunter Rumours (Teco Expert):**
- Same XP rates as regular Herbiboar (115-128k Hunter XP/hr)
- Same herb gains (1-3 herbs per catch, 2-4 with magic secateurs)
- **Bonus:** Loot sacks with extra herbs, blessed bone shards, guild outfit pieces
- **Bonus:** Chance at both Herbi pet AND Quetzin pet

**Verdict:** Doing Herbiboar via Hunter Rumours is strictly better than regular Herbiboar. Only downside: requires block list setup.

### Quetzal Transport System

**Quetzal Whistle Tiers:**

| Whistle | Unlock | Capacity | Use |
|---------|--------|----------|-----|
| Basic | 10 rumours (free from Pitri) | 1 passenger | Teleport to Hunter Guild, essential |
| Enhanced | 100 rumours (blueprint) | 2 passengers | Improved transport |
| Perfected | 250 rumours (blueprint) | 3 passengers | Best transport, collection log |

**Ironman priority:** Basic whistle (10 rumours) is **ESSENTIAL**. Get this immediately - dramatically improves rumour efficiency. Enhanced/Perfected are optional.

## FAQ

### What are Hunter Rumours and how do they work?

Hunter Rumours are Slayer-like contracts from Hunter Guild (Varlamore, 46+ Hunter). Accept assignments, hunt creatures until you get rare parts, return for XP and loot sacks. Takes 8-10 minutes per rumour.

### How do I create a block list for Hunter Rumours?

Accept unwanted tasks from different guild hunters and leave incomplete. This blocks them from your primary hunter's pool. Start with Huntmaster Gilman first, block Sabre-toothed Kyatt (worst task). You can block 5-6 total.

### What's the best Hunter Rumours block list for ironmen?

Teco Expert (72-91 Hunter). Block Aco's pool + Sunlight Antelope. Gives Herbiboar (herbs), Moonlight Moth (prayer), Red Chinchompa (XP). Expected: 160-200k XP/hr + supplies. Switch to Wolf Master at 91+ for best loot sacks.

### What creatures should I always block in Hunter Rumours?

Always block: Sabre-toothed Kyatt (slowest), Spotted Kebbit (falconry), Pyre Fox (slow deadfall), Sunlight Antelope (slow pitfall). These have terrible XP rates and annoying requirements.

### Are Hunter Rumours better than regular chinchompa hunting?

Yes, with optimal block lists. Aco Expert gives 235k XP/hr (vs 140-180k from regular red chins) plus loot sacks. For ironmen, Teco Expert gives same Herbiboar herbs PLUS loot sacks and better XP.

### What are blessed bone shards and how do I get them from Hunter Rumours?

Blessed bone shards are prayer training items (5-6 Prayer XP each). Expert/Master loot sacks contain them. Accumulate 20-30k banked Prayer XP/hr combined with mining. Excellent for mid-level prayer without dragon bones.

### Should I do Hunter Rumours or regular Herbiboar?

If you have 72+ Hunter, do Herbiboar via Hunter Rumours (Teco Expert assignment). Same herbs, same XP, but PLUS loot sacks and Quetzin pet chance. Strictly better than regular Herbiboar.
