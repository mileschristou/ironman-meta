## How Death's Coffer Works

Death's Coffer is a chest inside Death's Office. You can visit Death's Office at any time through tombstone entrances around the map - the fastest way in is an amulet of glory teleport to Edgeville (10 tiles from the entrance).

**Core mechanics:**
- Sacrifice any **tradeable item worth 10,000+ GP** - receive **105% of its GE price** as locked coffer credit
- Credit cannot be withdrawn, ever - only used for death fees and private instances
- Maximum coffer balance: 2,147,483,647 coins (integer limit)
- New items released after August 2024 are **blacklisted until their GE price settles** (prevents exploits)

**Who can use it:**
- Regular Ironmen ✅
- Hardcore Ironmen ✅
- Ultimate Ironmen ❌ (different death mechanic)

**Payment priority:** When you reclaim items, fees are deducted from your coffer first. If the balance is insufficient, the remainder comes from your bank.

<Callout type="tip">
**Hardcore Ironmen:** Death's Coffer is essential for you. It funds item recovery if you die in dangerous content but survive to retrieve items from Death's Office.
</Callout>

## Gravestone vs Death's Office

This is the most important thing to understand about death costs. There are two fee tiers and they're massively different.

### Gravestone (first 15 minutes)

When you die, your items go to a gravestone at your death location. You have **15 minutes** to run back and reclaim them. The timer pauses while logged out or idle for 10+ seconds.

Gravestone fees are cheap and capped:

| Item Value | Fee Per Item | Ironman Fee (50% off) |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| Under 100k | Free | Free |
| 100k - 1M | 1,000 | 500 |
| 1M - 10M | 10,000 | 5,000 |
| 10M+ | 100,000 | 50,000 |
| **Max total fee** | **500,000** | **~250,000** |

### Death's Office (after 15 minutes)

If you don't make it back in time, items transfer to Death's Office automatically. They're stored **indefinitely** but the fees jump dramatically:

- **Mains:** 5% of each item's GE value
- **Ironmen:** 2.5% of each item's GE value

**The difference in practice:** A late-game setup worth 500M costs ~125-250k to reclaim from the gravestone but **~12.5M** from Death's Office. Always try to get back to your gravestone.

<Callout type="warning">
If you die again while you have an active gravestone, your new items are added to the existing grave and the 15-minute timer resets. You don't lose anything, but now all your items are at the new death location.
</Callout>

### Protect Item Prayer

Always use **Protect Item** (25 Prayer) during dangerous PvM. It lets you keep 4 items on death instead of 3. Since the kept item is typically your most valuable piece, this massively reduces reclaim fees. For example, keeping a scythe of vitur (~700M) on death saves you 17.5M in Death's Office fees at the ironman rate.

## The Alch vs Coffer Rule

**Rule:** `If 105% of GE price > high alch value - coffer it`

| Item | GE Price | Coffer Value | High Alch | Winner |
|------|----------|-------------|-----------|--------|
| Barrows piece (avg) | 1,000,000 | 1,050,000 | 120,000 | Coffer (+875%) |
| Ranger boots | 35,000,000 | 36,750,000 | 90,000 | Coffer (+40,733%) |
| Dragon boots | 250,000 | 262,500 | 60,000 | Coffer (+337%) |
| Rune platebody | 40,000 | 42,000 | 37,800 | Coffer (+11%) |
| Granite maul | 25,000 | 26,250 | 7,500 | Coffer (+250%) |

For ironmen, the coffer wins almost every time for items worth 10k+. The only exception is items where the high alch value somehow exceeds 105% of GE price - extremely rare, but happens when items crash and the GE guide price hasn't caught up.

<Callout type="efficiency">
Items that alch low but sell high are the best coffer candidates. Cosmetic drops (Gilded gear, 3rd Age pieces, decorative clue rewards) have massive GE prices but alch for peanuts. Coffer them immediately.
</Callout>

## How Much to Keep

| Stage | Coffer Target | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| Early game (pre-70s) | 2-5M | Deaths are cheap, gear is cheap |
| Mid game (Slayer/bossing) | 10-20M | You're wearing 10-50M in gear |
| Late game (raiding) | 30-50M | Raid gear pushes 100M+ setups |
| Endgame (tbow/scythe) | 50-100M | One bad death at Death's Office costs 10M+ |

**Quick formula:** Take your most expensive wearable loadout, multiply by 0.025 (ironman 2.5% rate) = cost of one death at Death's Office. Keep enough for 10-20 bad deaths.

<Callout type="casual">
Don't stress about coffer balance early. The 50% ironman discount makes deaths very cheap. A full Barrows set worth 3M costs only 75k to reclaim from Death's Office. Just sacrifice items passively and let it build naturally.
</Callout>

### The Trouble Brewing Method - Passive Coffer Cheese

This is the easiest way to farm Death's Coffer early game. Minimal effort, zero GP investment, consistent passive income.

**How it works:**

1. **Farm "The Stuff"** - Play one Trouble Brewing game (~20 mins, very AFK)
   - Deposit 100 buckets of water into the hopper
   - Earn 100 Pieces of Eight
   - Buy 2x "The Stuff" from Honest Jimmy (50 pieces each)

2. **Brew Mature Ales** - Set up at any brewing vat (Keldagrim, Port Phasmatys, Aldarin)
   - Ingredients per batch: 2 water buckets, 2 barley malt, 4 main ingredients (e.g., Sulliuscep mushrooms for Moonlight Mead), 1 ale yeast, 1 The Stuff
   - With The Stuff: **64% chance to mature** (vs 5% without)
   - Fermentation: 1-3 days (passive, just wait)

3. **Sacrifice to Death's Coffer** - Feed mature batches straight to Death's Office
   - Mature Moonlight Mead: ~240k+ per batch to coffer
   - Per Trouble Brewing game: 2 batches brewing = ~480k+ into coffer over time

**The math:** One 20-minute Trouble Brewing session funds multiple brewing batches that passively fill your coffer while you do other content. This is the best early-game passive coffer income with zero resource investment.

<Callout type="efficiency">
This is the coffer "cheat code." You're converting minigame time into AFK brewing into massive coffer value. Other players alch dupes for scraps; you're turning ale ingredients into 240k+ batches.
</Callout>

### Trap: Dragon Bitter(m) Glass

**Dragon bitter(m) glass is worth only ~715 GP - below the 10,000 minimum threshold.** Don't brew it for the coffer. This is the most common mature ale trap.

## Mid Game: Dupes Start Flowing

This is where your coffer fills itself naturally through PvM.

### Barrows Duplicates

After 100-200 Barrows runs, every piece has duplicates.

- **Average piece value:** 500k-2M - 525k-2.1M in coffer
- **Priority:** Keep one of each piece you actually need (Ahrim's for magic DPS, Dharok's for NMZ/training). Sacrifice everything else.
- **Typical coffer gain:** 100-200 runs = 50M-100M into coffer (if you sacrifice all non-essentials)

### Zulrah Duplicates

After you have your blowpipe, magic fang, and serpentine helm, every Zulrah unique is a coffer deposit.

| Item | GE Price | Coffer Credit |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Tanzanite fang** | ~11M | ~11.5M |
| **Serpentine visage** | ~3.8M | ~4M |
| **Magic fang** | ~3.8M | ~4M |
| Uncut onyx | ~2.6M | ~2.7M |

Tanzanite fang dupes are the big ones at ~11.5M each. Most ironmen end up with multiple fang dupes over a Zulrah grind.

### Vorkath Duplicates

Vorkath's uniques are rare but valuable coffer items.

| Item | GE Price | Coffer Credit | Drop Rate |
|------|----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Skeletal visage** | ~17M | ~17.8M | 1/5,000 |
| **Draconic visage** | ~3.9M | ~4.1M | 1/5,000 |
| Dragonbone necklace | ~85k | ~89k | 1/1,000 |

After you've made your dragonfire ward, every visage dupe is a solid coffer deposit. Dragonbone necklaces are common enough that you'll stack up multiples - they meet the 10k minimum.

### Dagannoth Kings Ring Dupes

Farming all 3 DK kings produces inevitable ring dupes.

- **Berserker ring / Archer ring:** ~4M each - ~4.2M in coffer
- **Seers ring:** Lower value (~2-3M) - still worth sacrificing
- **How long:** Farming all 3 rings consistently for 100+ KC = guaranteed dupes

### Hydra Organ Dupes (Alchemical Hydra, 95 Slayer)

Eye, Fang, Heart drop in order. After you have the Brimstone ring, subsequent organs are dupes.

- **Value per organ:** ~60k GE - ~63k coffer
- **Not huge individually** but adds up at high KC

### Cerberus Boot Dupes (91 Slayer)

Primordial, Pegasian, Eternal boots - 25-45M each.

- **Second pair of any:** 26-47M in coffer
- **Best mid-game coffer item outside Barrows**
- Prioritize farming these once you hit 91 Slayer

### God Wars Dungeon Armour Dupes

- **Bandos pieces:** 5-30M each
- **Armadyl pieces:** 5-30M each
- Once you have the set, dupes go straight to Death

### Farming Seeds - Post-99 Contracts

For ironmen who continue Farming Contracts after 99, seed dupes become a steady coffer feeder.

- **Dragonfruit tree seeds:** ~175k each - ~184k in coffer
- **Other tree seeds:** Vary widely depending on rarity
- **Not a primary source** but a nice bonus for dedicated farmers

## Trap Items - Don't Waste Your Time

### Below the 10,000 GE Minimum

- **Dragon bitter(m) glass** (~715 GP) - most common mature ale trap
- Various stackable drops that sound valuable but are under 10k per unit

### Untradeable Items (Ineligible Regardless)

These look like they should work but the coffer won't accept them:

- Imbued rings (Berserker ring (i), Seers ring (i), Archer ring (i))
- Slayer helm (i)
- God capes (charged cape)
- Any item that's been imbued at Nightmare Zone
- Tanzanite/Magma mutagens from Zulrah (untradeable cosmetics)
- Vorkath's head (untradeable)

### Blocked by Jagex

- **Bonds** - explicitly blocked
- **Leagues/Deadman/Grid Master rewards** - exploit prevention
- **Recently released items** (post-August 2024) - blacklisted until GE price settles

### Items Where High Alch Actually Beats Coffer

Extremely rare. Check: if 105% of GE price < high alch value, alch it instead. This happens when items crash and the GE guide price hasn't caught up.

<Callout type="warning">
Don't assume every high-GE item is worth coffering. Always check the alch value first using Alchemy Calculator or the formula. The answer is usually coffer, but not always.
</Callout>

Your coffer credit also pays for private boss room instances. Combat Achievements reduce GWD instance costs.

| Boss | Base Cost | With Hard CA | With Elite CA | Worth It? |
|------|----------|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| Kraken | 25,000 | - | - | Yes - no competition, safe spot freedom |
| Grotesque Guardians | 50,000 | - | - | Sometimes - if multi-combat is a problem |
| **GWD bosses** | **150,000** | **125,000** | **100,000** | **Yes once you farm consistently** |
| Corporeal Beast | 200,000 | - | - | Only if farming spirit shields |

</Callout>

## FAQ

### What's the best AFK way to fill my coffer early?

The Trouble Brewing method. One 20-minute game gets you "The Stuff" for 2 brewing batches. With The Stuff, you have a 64% chance of maturing each batch. Moonlight Mead kegs are worth ~175k each (~184k in coffer). Set up brews and collect passively while doing other content.

### Should I always coffer boss dupes instead of alching?

Almost always yes. Boss uniques have high GE values but terrible alch values. A Barrows piece worth 1M GE gives 1.05M in the coffer vs ~120k from alching. The only exception is if the GE price has crashed well below the alch value, which is extremely rare for boss uniques.

### How do gravestone fees compare to Death's Office fees?

Gravestone fees are capped at ~250k total for ironmen regardless of gear value. Death's Office charges 2.5% per item. A 500M setup costs ~125-250k at the gravestone but ~12.5M from Death's Office. Always try to reclaim from your gravestone within 15 minutes.
