Getting reliable food is one of the first challenges on an ironman. Here's what to use at each stage - and when to move on.

**At a glance:**
- **Early:** Eclipse Red wines (Varlamore), cakes (Ardy stall), jugs of wine
- **Mid:** Karambwans, monkfish, sunlight moth mixes
- **Late:** Sharks, manta rays, anglerfish, cooked antelopes, combo eating

---

## Early Game

You don't have high Fishing or Cooking yet. These options keep you alive with minimal requirements.

### Eclipse Red Wines ([Varlamore](/guides/varlamore/))
- **Heals:** 16 HP
- **Requirements:** Children of the Sun quest (5 minutes)
- **Where:** Free spawn at Hunter Guild, world hop to collect
- **Why it's good:** Best early food in the game. Also [sells for 700gp each](/supplies/early-money/) at Aldarin winery - food AND money maker.

<Callout type="tip">
Pick these up while world hopping for money making - you'll build a food stack and cash stack at the same time.
</Callout>

### Cakes (Ardougne Stall)
- **Heals:** 4 + 4 + 4 (12 total over 3 bites)
- **Requirements:** None (15 Thieving helps avoid guards)
- **Where:** Steal from Ardougne bakery stall
- **Why it's good:** Zero cost, infinite supply, no skills needed. Classic early ironman method.

### Jugs of Wine
- **Heals:** 11 HP (drains Attack by 2-3)
- **Requirements:** None
- **Where:** Buy from Fortunato in Draynor for 1gp each
- **Why it's good:** Cheap, easy to stockpile
- **Downside:** Attack drain makes it bad for melee - better for ranging/maging or skilling

### Basic Fish (Trout, Salmon, Tuna)
- **Heals:** 7 / 9 / 10
- **Requirements:** 20-35 Fishing, 15-30 Cooking
- **Where:** Barbarian Village, Shilo Village
- **Why it's good:** Trains two skills while building food supply

## Mid Game

You're doing [Slayer](/skilling/slayer/), early bossing, and need food that heals more. By this point you should have 60+ Fishing and 60+ Cooking, unlocking better food options that also train your skills.

### Karambwans
- **Heals:** 18 HP
- **Requirements:** 65 Fishing, 30 Cooking, [Tai Bwo Wannai Trio](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tai_Bwo_Wannai_Trio) quest
- **Where:** Fishing spot east of Tai Bwo Wannai
- **Why it's good:** Excellent healing AND combo food (can eat with other food in same tick)
- **Banking:** Karamja Gloves 3, or fish barrel + house teleport

<Callout type="efficiency">
Karambwans are THE ironman food. Start fishing these at 65 Fishing - they'll carry you through mid and late game.

**Pro tip:** Get Cooking Gauntlets (Family Crest quest reward) to reduce fish burn rates. If you've completed Easy Diary, use Hosidius Kitchen for even better cooking success.
</Callout>

### Monkfish
- **Heals:** 16 HP
- **Requirements:** 62 Fishing, 62 Cooking, [Swan Song](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Swan_Song) quest
- **Where:** Piscatoris Fishing Colony
- **Why it's good:** Easy to fish, bank nearby via fairy ring (AKQ)

### Sunlight Moth Mixes ([Varlamore](/guides/varlamore/))
- **Heals:** 8 HP + restores 6 + 20% of reduced stats
- **Requirements:** 65 Hunter, pestle and mortar, hunter meat
- **Where:** Hunter Guild (The Burrow)
- **Why it's good:** Works like Guthix rest - no eat delay, can heal during combat without interrupting attacks

<Callout type="tip">
Sunlight moth mixes are underrated. The no-delay healing is perfect for combat achievements where you need fast kills.
</Callout>

### Lobsters
- **Heals:** 12 HP
- **Requirements:** 40 Fishing, 40 Cooking
- **Where:** Catherby, Fishing Guild
- **Why it's good:** Easy transition between early and mid game

## Late Game

You're bossing, doing raids, and need maximum healing.

### Sharks
- **Heals:** 20 HP
- **Requirements:** 76 Fishing, 80 Cooking
- **Where:** Fishing Guild, Catherby
- **Why it's good:** Standard high-level food, reliable

### Manta Rays
- **Heals:** 22 HP
- **Requirements:** 81 Fishing, 91 Cooking
- **Where:** Fishing Trawler, Tempoross
- **Why it's good:** Highest instant healing from standard fish

### Anglerfish
- **Heals:** 22 HP (scales with HP level, can overheal)
- **Requirements:** 82 Fishing, 84 Cooking, 100% Piscarilius favour
- **Where:** Port Piscarilius
- **Why it's good:** Can heal above max HP (up to 121 HP at 99) - eat one before every boss fight
- **Note:** Overheal doesn't work in PvP

### Cooked Sunlight Antelope (Varlamore)
- **Heals:** 21 HP (12 instant + 9 delayed over 4.2s)
- **Requirements:** 72 Hunter, 68 Cooking, 50 Hunter Rumours completed
- **Where:** Hunt in Avium Savannah, cook anywhere
- **Why it's good:** High total healing, good for Slayer where damage is spread out
- **Downside:** Delayed heal makes it bad for intense bossing - eating again cancels the pending heal

### Cooked Moonlight Antelope (Varlamore)
- **Heals:** 26 HP (14 instant + 12 delayed over 4.2s) + cures poison
- **Requirements:** 91 Hunter, 92 Cooking, 50 Hunter Rumours completed
- **Where:** Hunt at night in Hunter Guild area, cook anywhere
- **Why it's good:** Highest healing food in the game. Also cures poison.
- **Downside:** Same delayed healing issue - don't use for intense damage situations
- **Note:** Burns even at 99 Cooking without the Cooking cape

## Varlamore Food Summary

Varlamore added several unique food options. Here's when to use each:

| Food | Heals | Hunter | Cooking | Best For |
|------|-------|--------|---------|----------|
| Eclipse Red | 16 | - | - | Early game, also 700gp each |
| Sunlight Moth Mix | 8 + stats | 65 | - | Speed kills, no-delay healing |
| Cooked Sunlight Antelope | 21 (delayed) | 72 | 68 | Slayer, low-damage activities |
| Cooked Moonlight Antelope | 26 (delayed) | 91 | 92 | Max healing, cures poison |

**Key difference:** Moth mixes have no eat delay (like Guthix rest). Antelopes have delayed healing that can be cancelled.

## Combo Eating

Combo eating is eating multiple foods (or food + potions) in the same game tick to heal or apply effects simultaneously. This is essential for endgame PvM.

### The Mechanics

**Same-Tick Eating (What You Can Do):**
- **Hard food + Karambwan:** YES - Click karambwan while eating shark/manta/anglerfish. Both heal in one tick.
- **Hard food + Potion:** YES - You can drink a Saradomin brew or other non-mix potions alongside food.
- **All three (food + brew + karambwan):** YES - Example: Anglerfish + Saradomin brew + Karambwan = 56 HP healed in one tick.

**Critical Rule:** Karambwan must be eaten SECOND. You can't eat two karambwans in a row, and eating karambwan first doesn't work.

**Tick Timing:**
- Standard foods (shark, manta, anglerfish): 3-tick eat delay (1.8 seconds)
- Karambwan in combos: 0-tick extra delay (doesn't add to the combo)
- Attack delay after combo: 5 ticks total (3 from food + 2 from karambwan)
- Each game tick = 0.6 seconds

### Common Combos & Healing

| Combo | Total Healing | Best For | Attack Delay |
|-------|---------------|----------|--------------|
| Shark + Karambwan | 38 HP | Sustained combat | 5 ticks |
| Manta Ray + Karambwan | 40 HP | High healing | 5 ticks |
| Anglerfish + Karambwan | 40 HP | Overheal potential | 5 ticks |
| Moonlight Antelope + Karambwan | 26 + 18 = 44 HP | Slayer, poison cure | 5 ticks |
| Anglerfish + Brew + Karambwan | 56 HP | Emergency healing | 5 ticks |

**Why This Matters:** Combo eating in one tick vs eating over multiple ticks means you survive spike damage that would kill you otherwise. In Inferno or ToB, this difference is survival vs death.

### When You NEED Combo Eating

**Essential (You Will Die Without It):**
- Inferno (TzKal-Zuk) - High burst damage phases require instant healing
- Theatre of Blood - Consistent high damage forces combo eating
- Vorkath/Zulrah - Healing before special attacks prevents one-shots

**Highly Recommended:**
- Endgame Slayer (88+ tasks) - Safety margin for unexpected damage
- High-level bossing - Marginal DPS gains from faster healing
- PvP - Reactive healing and tick-eating mechanics

**Optional:**
- Early/mid Slayer - Damage is spread out, single food healing is fine
- AFK tasks (NMZ, Splashing) - No time-critical healing needed

<Callout type="warning">
Moonlight antelope's delayed heal mechanic is compatible with karambwan. Eat moonlight antelope, wait 4.2 seconds, then combo eat with karambwan without losing the pending heal.
</Callout>

### Real DPS Impact

Combo eating doesn't just save HP-it saves time. By healing 40 HP in one tick instead of spread across multiple ticks, you avoid adding unnecessary attack delays to your rotation. At Zulrah, combo-eating before the phase start means one additional attack on the boss compared to eating after. Over a full kill, this adds up.

<Callout type="tip">
New to combo eating? Practice on low-stakes content first (Slayer tasks) before Inferno. The click timing takes practice, but the survival improvement is immediate.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

- **Ignoring Eclipse Red wines** - Best early food now, and most guides don't mention Varlamore
- **Not fishing karambwans early** - Start at 65 Fishing, they're useful forever
- **Using sharks for everything** - Combo eat with karambwans for better healing per slot
- **Forgetting anglerfish overheal** - Always eat one before a tough boss
- **Using antelopes for bossing** - The delayed heal gets cancelled if you eat again quickly
