Cooking on an ironman is about **self-sufficient food production**. You catch raw fish while [Fishing](/skilling/fishing/), cook them, and use them in PvM. It's the middle piece of the complete loop.

**At a glance:**
- **Quest speedrun (1-20):** 30 minutes, Cook's Assistant
- **Mess Hall (20-50):** Mandatory, 48-55k XP/hour, on Zeah
- **Unlock Hosidius (50):** 100% favour + Easy Diary, game-changer for burn rates
- **Mid-game (50-90):** Monkfish → Sharks at Hosidius, 150-285k XP/hour
- **Late-game (84-99):** Anglerfish AFK OR 1-Tick Karambwan speed (up to 950k XP/hour)
- **Key advantage:** Dual training with [Fishing](/skilling/fishing/) for zero material cost

## Early Game: Quest Speedrun & First Steps (Level 1-20)

### Cook's Assistant Quest

The only cooking quest with XP reward:
- **XP Reward:** 300 Cooking XP (reaches level 10)
- **Quest length:** 5 minutes
- **Unlocks:** Cook's range in Lumbridge Castle (reduced burn rate)
- **Requirements:** Access to Lumbridge

After completing, you gain access to Cook's range which has a permanent 5% burn rate reduction (equivalent to being 5 levels higher).

### Early Cooking Methods (Levels 1-20)

**Beef (Level 1):**
- 130 XP per beef
- ~30,000-50,000 XP/hour
- High burn rate early on (50%+ at level 1-10)
- Not recommended unless doing Cook's range

**Chicken (Level 1):**
- 70 XP per chicken
- Lower XP than beef, only for early questing
- Skip for efficiency

**Trout (Level 20):**
- 70 XP per trout
- Can be caught at nearby fishing spots
- Dual training opportunity with [Fishing](/skilling/fishing/)

<Callout type="tip">
Start at Cook's range for the permanent burn reduction advantage. By level 10-15, you'll want to move on to more efficient methods.
</Callout>

## Mid-Game: Mess Hall Progression (Level 20-50)

### What is Mess Hall?

Mess Hall is a **mandatory location for ironmen** (Level 20+). It's the best cooking training method for levels 20-50, with all required ingredients available on-site.

**Location:** South of Great Kourend in Hosidius
**Requirements:** 20 Cooking, access to Zeah

### How Mess Hall Works

1. **Use servery meat pie on raw meat** → Combine into pie shell
2. **Use pie shell on a range** → Cook to completion
3. **Use finished pie on the serving table** → Serve to earn XP

**XP per completed pie:** 160 Cooking XP
**Burn rate:** Extremely low (~5%) due to built-in range bonus
**XP Rate:** 48,000-55,000 XP/hour

### Why It's Essential

- **All ingredients provided on-site** - zero material gathering needed
- **Extremely low burn rate** - even at level 20, you rarely burn pies
- **Best XP rates for 20-50** - no other method compares
- **Quick progression** - level 20 to ~50 in roughly 6-8 hours

**Time at Mess Hall:** Approximately 6-8 hours to reach level 50

<Callout type="efficiency">
Every ironman must do Mess Hall 20-50. There is no faster or better alternative for this range. Accept it, get it done, then move on to Hosidius unlock.
</Callout>

## The Game-Changer: Hosidius Kitchen Unlock (Level 50)

This single unlock transforms your cooking progression.

### Requirements

**100% Hosidius House Favour**
- No Cooking requirement
- Takes 3-4 hours to grind
- Method: Make sulphurous fertiliser (0.1% favour per bucket)
  - Need: Hammer + 950 compost
  - Fastest: Buy compost, spend 3 hours making buckets

**Easy Kourend & Kebos Diary**
- Minimal requirements (mostly exploration-based tasks)
- Reward: Access to Hosidius Kitchen ranges

### Why Hosidius is Game-Changing

**Burn Rate Reduction: 5% Lower Than Standard Range**

This stacks with other bonuses:
- **Base (fire):** High burn rate
- **Standard range:** 5% reduction
- **Hosidius range:** 10% reduction (5% better than standard)
- **+ Cooking Gauntlets:** Additional +6 level equivalent
- **+ Elite Diary:** Additional 5% reduction
- **+ Cooking Cape (99):** 100% success rate

**Example Burn Stop Levels:**

| Fish | Fire | Hosidius | + Gauntlets | + Elite Diary |
|-----|------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Monkfish | 86 | 82 | 78 | 76 |
| Sharks | 94 | 90 | 86 | 82-84 |
| Anglerfish | 99 | 95+ | 90+ | 85+ |

### Banking & Convenience

- **Bank distance:** Only 6 tiles from cooking ranges
- **Accessibility:** Central on Zeah, quick teleport options
- **QoL:** Huge improvement for grinding 50-99

**Time investment:** 3-4 hours to unlock → saves 20+ hours of grinding

<Callout type="tip">
Unlock Hosidius as soon as you hit level 50. The 3-4 hour investment pays for itself immediately.
</Callout>

## Cooking Gauntlets: The Secondary Game-Changer (Level 1+)

### How to Get

- **Quest:** Family Crest (very easy, no stat requirements)
- **NPC:** Caleb in house north of archery shop in Catherby
- **Process:** Bring steel gauntlets (quest reward) to Caleb for upgrade
- **Cost:** Free

### What They Do

- **Function:** +6 invisible Cooking levels for burn rate calculations
- **Affected foods:** Lobster, Swordfish, Monkfish, Sharks, Anglerfish
- **Do NOT affect:** Other foods like beef, chicken, trout, etc.

### When to Prioritize

- Complete Family Crest before cooking Sharks (level 80)
- Use until reaching 99 Cooking
- At 99 Cooking, Cooking Cape provides 100% success (gauntlets become optional)

<Callout type="tip">
Get Cooking Gauntlets early. Family Crest has zero stat requirements and takes 15 minutes. It's mandatory for efficient Shark/Anglerfish cooking.
</Callout>

## Mid-Game Cooking (Level 50-90)

### Levels 50-65: Salmon & Trout (Optional Transition)

- **Salmon (Level 25):** 70 XP, stops burning at level 58
- **Trout (Level 20):** 70 XP, stops burning at level 49
- **Location:** Hosidius Kitchen (after unlock)
- **XP Rate:** 50,000-100,000 XP/hour
- **Recommendation:** Skip if possible - move straight to Monkfish

### Levels 62-80: Monkfish - The Sweet Spot

**Why Monkfish?**
- **Requirement:** 62 Cooking
- **Food quality:** Heals 16 HP (strong for mid-game PvM)
- **XP:** 150 XP per fish
- **Burn stop:** Level 86 (or 82 with gauntlets + Hosidius)
- **XP Rate:** 150,000-200,000 XP/hour at Hosidius
- **Dual training:** Can catch at Piscarilius, cook at Hosidius (very close)

**Duration:** 62-80 Cooking = roughly 10-12 hours

<Callout type="efficiency">
Monkfish is the best mid-game food training. You produce food you'll actually use (16 HP healing is solid for PvM), gain good XP rates, and can dual-train with Fishing if desired.
</Callout>

### Levels 80-90: Sharks - Premium Food

**Why Sharks?**
- **Requirement:** 80 Cooking
- **Food quality:** Heals 20 HP (excellent for bossing)
- **XP:** 210 XP per fish
- **Burn stop:** Level 94 (fire) → level 80-82 (Hosidius + gauntlets + elite diary)
- **XP Rate:** 250,000-285,000 XP/hour at Hosidius
- **Setup:** Hosidius Kitchen + Cooking Gauntlets + Elite Diary = almost no burning

**Duration:** 80-90 Cooking = roughly 5-7 hours

## Late-Game: Choose Your Path (Level 84-99)

### Path A: Anglerfish AFK (Recommended for Lazy)

Perfect for relaxed grinding while doing other activities.

**Setup:**
- **Requirement:** 84 Cooking
- **Location:** Piscarilius (banking convenience)
- **Equipment:** Raw anglerfish (from [Fishing](/skilling/fishing/) yourself)
- **XP:** 230 XP per fish
- **XP Rate:** 250,000-300,000 XP/hour (very AFK)
- **AFK Duration:** ~15 seconds per click
- **Burn rate:** Low with gauntlets + Hosidius

**Food Production:**
- Heals 22 HP (best healing food)
- Self-healing mechanic (restores extra 1-4 HP automatically)
- Stock 1,000+ for endgame [raiding](/supplies/food/)

**Why Anglerfish?**
- Ultra-AFK (click every 15 seconds)
- Produces best food in the game
- Overlevel cooking before reaching 99 (burn less)
- No material cost (catch yourself via Fishing)

**Duration:** 84-99 = roughly 20-25 hours

<Callout type="tip">
Anglerfish is the ironman's favorite late-game method. You're literally clicking while watching YouTube, and you're producing the exact food you need for raiding.
</Callout>

### Path B: 1-Tick Karambwan - The Speed Route

**THE FASTEST COOKING METHOD IN THE ENTIRE GAME.** Up to 950,000 XP/hour.

#### How 1-Tick Karambwan Works

**Mechanic:** Use raw karambwan on range/fire while holding SPACE or rapid-clicking to cook them instantly in one game tick.

**Setup:**
- **Requirement:** 30 Cooking (Tai Bwo Wannai Trio quest)
- **Location:** Myths' Guild (best banking)
- **Equipment:** Raw karambwan (catch yourself) + cooking gloves (optional)
- **XP:** 190 XP per fish
- **Attention:** Constant rapid-clicking (NOT AFK)

#### XP Rates: 1-Tick Karambwan

| Skill Level | XP/Hour | Difficulty |
|------------|---------|-----------|
| Learning (first 30 min) | 200,000-300,000 | Hard |
| Intermediate (after practice) | 450,000-600,000 | Medium |
| Advanced (perfect rhythm) | 600,000-800,000 | Difficult |
| Expert (near-optimal) | 800,000-950,000 | Very Difficult |

**Realistic goal:** 450,000-700,000 XP/hour for most players

#### How to Execute 1-Tick Karambwan

**Step-by-step:**
1. Open Karambwan inventory
2. Click and drag karambwan to range/fire
3. **Hold down SPACE BAR** (or rapid-click mouse button)
4. Use karambwan on range continuously
5. Karambwans cook instantly (one tick each)
6. Cooked karambwan automatically drops
7. Pick up and bank, repeat

**Pro tips:**
- **Best location:** Myths' Guild (quick bank, dedicated range)
- **Alternative:** Mor Ul Rek (same concept)
- **Hotkey setup:** Many players bind keys to streamline the process
- **Eye strain warning:** This is intense - take breaks
- **Food quality:** 18 HP healing (perfect for combo-eating strategy)

#### When to Use 1-Tick Karambwan

- **Levels 30-99:** Can do it anytime after quest
- **Best for:** Pushing 95-99 quickly (final sprint)
- **Worst for:** AFK sessions, watching Netflix
- **Ideal:** 2-3 hour sessions while focused

<Callout type="warning">
1-Tick Karambwan is mentally taxing. It requires constant attention and rapid clicking for the entire session. Many players alternate between 1-tick sessions (push hard) and Anglerfish AFK (chill sessions) to avoid burnout.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
For final levels (95-99), do 1-tick Karambwan in focused 2-3 hour sessions. You'll reach 99 in under 10 hours total, then can AFK Anglerfish for relaxation if needed.
</Callout>

## Dual Training Opportunities

The Ironman advantage: **Catch + Cook simultaneously for two-skill XP.**

### Barbarian Village (Levels 20-50)

- **Catch:** Trout/Salmon at nearby fishing spots
- **Cook:** Over perpetual fire in Barbarian Village
- **Combined XP:** ~20,000 Fishing + 20,000 Cooking XP/hour
- **Best for:** Early game (levels 1-50)
- **Advantage:** Zero banking, infinite food supply

### Monkfish Dual Training (Levels 62-80)

- **Catch:** Piscarilius Fishing Colony
- **Cook:** Hosidius Kitchen (very close, under 1 minute walk)
- **Combined XP:** Fishing rates + 150-200k Cooking XP/hour
- **Best for:** Mid-game progression
- **Result:** 16 HP healing food stockpile

### Karambwan Dual Training (Levels 30-99)

- **Catch:** Tai Bwo Wannai (requires quest)
- **Cook:** Myths' Guild or Mor Ul Rek (teleport distance)
- **Combined XP:** Fishing rates + 300-950k Cooking XP/hour
- **Best for:** Serious grinding (500+ raw karambwan cooking)
- **Result:** Thousands of 18 HP healing food for PvM

### Sacred Eel Fishing + Dissecting (Levels 72+ Cooking, 87+ Fishing)

- **Catch:** Zul-Andra (via [Fishing](/skilling/fishing/))
- **Dissect:** Requires 72 Cooking + knife
- **Combined XP:** ~21,300 Fishing + ~23,800 Cooking XP/hour
- **Bonus:** Generates **Zulrah Scales** (for prayer training)
- **Best for:** Endgame passive activity
- **Unique:** Only method that produces scales without bossing

## FAQ

**Can I AFK cook entirely?**
Yes. Anglerfish 84-99 is very AFK (~15 second intervals). But 1-tick karambwan is faster if you're willing to focus.

**When should I cook food vs store raw?**
Cook as you go while [Fishing](/skilling/fishing/). You'll naturally have thousands of cooked food by 99. Store raw only if inventory is full.

**Is cooking cape worth getting?**
Yes at 99 Cooking. Provides 100% success rate on all foods + Defence bonus. Can remove gauntlets after.

**How much total food will I have by 99 Cooking?**
If dual-training with Fishing: 3,000-5,000+ cooked fish depending on method. Enough for months of PvM.

**Can I do 1-tick for the entire 99?**
Technically yes, but burn-out risk is high. Most players do 1-tick 95-99 (final push) to stay engaged, then AFK for lower levels.

<Callout type="efficiency">
The Ironman cooking grind is 40-60 hours of solid work. But the payoff is eternal - you'll never buy food again. By 99, you'll have stockpiled 5,000+ cooked fish from natural progression.
</Callout>
