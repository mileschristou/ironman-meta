Ranged training on an ironman is straightforward if you embrace the reality: **you don't need to grind to 99**. Most of your late-game Ranged XP comes passively from Slayer and raiding.

The path is simple: Get to 40 via quests, grind to 70 via Ammonite Crabs (AFK), then decide if you want speed (chinchompas) or patience (passive progression). By 85, you're set-and raiding will naturally push you toward 95+.

---

## Early Game (1-40): Foundation via Quests & Crabs

### Quest-Based Early Levels

| Quest | XP | Level After |
|-------|-----|------------|
| Shadow of the Storm | 10,000 combat | 20 |
| Death to the Dorgeshuun + Horror from the Deep | 6,663 Ranged | 32 |

These quests get you to ~32 Ranged before active training.

### Dorgeshuun Crossbow (28-40)

**Why:** Extremely cheap, self-sufficient ammunition
- **Weapon:** Dorgeshuun crossbow (obtainable after The Lost Tribe quest)
- **Ammunition:** Bone bolts (essentially free via slayer drops or cheap purchase)
- **Location:** Ammonite Crabs or low-level Slayer tasks
- **Duration:** ~10-15 hours to reach 40

<Callout type="tip">
Bone bolts are the most forgiving ammunition for early training. They're cheap and plentiful. Don't overthink gear progression this early.
</Callout>

---

## Mid Game (40-70): Ammonite Crabs (The AFK Phase)

This is the sweet spot for ironman Ranged training. **Ammonite Crabs are the most relaxed training method in the game.**

### Why Ammonite Crabs?

- **XP Rate:** 50-100k XP/hour depending on level
- **Cost:** Near-zero (ava's accumulator recovers most ammo)
- **AFK Duration:** 10 minutes before aggression resets
- **Effort:** Minimal attention required

### Setup

| Slot | Item | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Weapon** | Toxic Blowpipe (if you have it) OR Magic Shortbow (i) OR Rune Crossbow | Speed varies; blowpipe best if available, but not required |
| **Offhand** | Blowpipe / Crossbow bolts | Depends on main weapon |
| **Ammo** | Amethyst Arrows / Broad Bolts / Blowpipe Darts | Whatever you've stockpiled |
| **Armor** | Anything with Ranged bonus | Void Knight is ideal mid-game (from Pest Control) |
| **Gloves** | None required | Slot better used for utility |

### Location Tips

- **Fossil Island:** Safe-spottable, consistent crabs
- **Aggression reset:** 10 minutes of inactivity before they stop attacking
- **Banks:** Nearby for supplies

**Result:** 40-70 Ranged in roughly 15-25 hours of mostly AFK training.

<Callout type="efficiency">
Ammonite Crabs is the most forgiving mid-game training. You can literally afk while doing other things. Don't skip this phase.
</Callout>

## Critical Unlock: Ava's Accumulator (30 Ranged)

**Quest:** Animal Magnetism

**Why it matters:**
- Recovers 60-80% of ammunition automatically
- Saves millions in ammunition costs over your account lifespan
- Makes all subsequent Ranged training dramatically cheaper
- Unlock at 30 Ranged (you'll hit this naturally during early training)

Do this quest **before** you push past 30 Ranged.

## Late Game (70-85): Choose Your Path

At 70 Ranged, you have two realistic options.

### Option A: Chinchompas for Speed (15-20 hours)

**If you want to rush 85:**
- Location: Kruk's Dungeon (Maniacal Monkeys)
- XP Rates: 500k-800k XP/hour (medium-high intensity)
- Cost: 5-10M GP in chinchompas (self-hunted via Hunter)
- Requirements: 40+ Hunter, Song of the Elves quest

**Reality Check:**
- Hunter training takes 10+ hours if you don't have it
- Chinchompas are tedious to hunt on popular worlds
- By the time you factor in prereq training, you're looking at 30+ hours total

### Option B: Slayer + Passive (30-50 hours)

**If you're patient (recommended):**
- Train Slayer with imbued Slayer Helm (makes Ranged viable)
- Do PvM content you enjoy (Vorkath, GWD, etc.)
- Let Ranged XP accumulate passively
- No time pressure, more enjoyable

**Imbued Slayer Helm (THE Game-Changer):**
At 70 Defence, you can imbue the Slayer Helm with the helm shard from Nightmare/Phosani's Nightmare.
- **Effect:** +20% damage when on-task
- **Impact:** Makes Slayer tasks with Ranged genuinely viable for training
- **This is the key unlock** that makes Slayer-based Ranged training actually reasonable

<Callout type="tip">
Imbued Slayer Helm makes Ranged training through Slayer actually viable. Without it, Slayer is inefficient for Ranged. With it, it becomes a reasonable progression path.
</Callout>

**Why Option B is Better:**
- Gain Slayer XP simultaneously (essential for mid-game progression)
- No tedious Hunter grind
- Enjoy Slayer tasks while training
- 85 Ranged comes naturally without feeling like a grind

## The Honest Truth: 85+ Comes Passively

**Here's the thing most guides don't tell you:** After 85 Ranged, you don't need to grind anymore.

### Why?

- **Raiding:** ToA, CoX, Tombs-all give massive Ranged XP
- **High-level Slayer:** Tasks give consistent Ranged gains
- **Bossing:** Vorkath, DKs, Cerberus-tons of ranged XP naturally
- **Timeline:** Most ironmen reach 95-99 Ranged passively within 100-150 hours of raiding

### The Natural Progression

By the time you've:
- Done 50 ToA KC
- Done some high-level Slayer
- Done some boss grinding for uniques

...you'll be 95+ Ranged without dedicated training.

**Don't push 99 as a goal.** Get to 85, then let raiding handle the rest.

## Slayer Integration: The Imbued Helm Strategy

Most guides say "Slayer is bad for Ranged training." **That's true without imbuing.** With an imbued Slayer Helm, it becomes reasonable.

### Slayer Helm Imbuing

**Requirements:**
- 70 Defence
- Slayer Helm shard (from Nightmare/Phosani's Nightmare)
- 250k GP

**Effect:**
- +20% accuracy on-task
- +15% damage on-task
- Makes Ranged tasks actually viable

### Which Slayer Tasks Are Ranged-Viable?

**With Imbued Helm:**
- Dragons (Dragonhunter Crossbow for +25% bonus)
- Basilisks (low Ranged defence, safespottable)
- Aviansies (Ranged weakness, weak to arrows)
- Wyverns (Ranged weakness, profitable)

**XP Rates:** 40-80k Ranged XP/hour (suboptimal vs dedicated training, but acceptable alongside Slayer)

**Verdict:** Mix imbued Helm Ranged tasks with your regular Slayer grind. You'll get decent Ranged XP while building your account naturally.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**1. Skipping Ava's Accumulator**
Problem: Wastes ammunition, increases costs dramatically
Solution: Get it at 30 Ranged. Non-negotiable.

**2. Trying to Train Ranged Through Slayer (Without Imbuing)**
Problem: Melee is 2-3x faster for Slayer; Ranged without imbue is suboptimal
Solution: Imbue the Slayer Helm first, or train Ranged separately.

**3. Forcing the Chinchompa Grind**
Problem: Tedious Hunter training, world competition, time-intensive
Solution: Unless speed is critical, skip it. Let 85+ come passively.

**4. Grinding 99 Ranged Early**
Problem: Wastes time that could go to Slayer, quests, PvM progression
Solution: Stop at 85, let raiding push you to 95+ naturally.

**5. Ignoring Void Knight**
Problem: Missing out on one of the best mid-game gear upgrades
Solution: Get Void Knight early via Pest Control (30-50 hours, worth it).

## When You're Ready to Move On

You've trained Ranged enough when:
- ✅ You're 85+ (set for most content)
- ✅ You can handle Slayer tasks efficiently (imbued Helm or Blowpipe)
- ✅ You're comfortable with boss mechanics (Vorkath, GWD, raids)

**Stop treating Ranged as a goal. Treat it as a side effect of doing fun content.**
