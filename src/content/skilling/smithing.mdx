Smithing on an ironman isn't just about XP — it's about where your bars come from and what you do with them.

**At a glance:**
- **Fastest XP:** Blast Furnace gold bars with Goldsmith Gauntlets (380-420k XP/hr)
- **Resource-efficient:** Giants' Foundry (90-250k XP/hr, uses PvM drops, often profitable)
- **Priority unlocks:** Goldsmith Gauntlets, Ice Gloves, Coal Bag, Double Ammo Mould

---

## Priority Unlocks

Get these before serious training:

| Item | Source | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| **[Goldsmith Gauntlets](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Goldsmith_gauntlets)** | Family Crest quest | 2.5x XP per gold bar (22.5 → 56.2) — essential for BF gold |
| **[Ice Gloves](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ice_gloves)** | Ice Queen (50 Mining) | Grab hot bars without bucket — huge QoL |
| **[Coal Bag](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Coal_bag)** | MLM (100 nuggets) | Carry 27 extra coal — essential for steel+ bars |
| **[Double Ammo Mould](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Double_ammo_mould)** | Giants' Foundry (2,000 rep) | Doubles cannonball speed — get this early |
| **[Smith's Uniform](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Smiths%27_Uniform)** | Giants' Foundry (15,000 rep total) | 20% faster anvil/GF actions |

<Callout type="tip">
Ice Gloves are a game-changer. Kill Ice Queen early (she's safe-spottable with fire spells) — you'll use them forever.
</Callout>

## Where Do Bars Come From?

This is the ironman-specific question most guides skip.

| Source | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **[Ordan](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Ordan) (Blast Furnace shop)** | Buy ores with GP, smelt at BF — fastest bar acquisition |
| **PvM drops** | Lots of metal items from Slayer — recycle at Giants' Foundry |
| **[Zalcano](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zalcano)** | Gold, mithril, adamant ores/bars — excellent mid-game |
| **MLM** | ~500 ores/hr — slow, but passive while AFK |
| **Mining** | Generally not worth it vs buying with GP |

**Key insight:** Most ironmen don't mine their smithing supplies anymore. It's faster to make money (thieving, agility pyramid, etc.) and buy ores from Ordan.

<Callout type="efficiency">
1 hour of Ardy Knights (~250k GP) buys more ores than 5+ hours of MLM would produce. Don't mine your bars.
</Callout>

## Gold vs Steel: The Real Comparison

You'll see some guides suggest steel bars as a "cheaper alternative." They're technically cheaper per bar, but the math tells a different story.

| Method | XP/hr | Cost Per Bar | GP Per XP | Total to 99 |
|--------|-------|--------------|-----------|------------|
| **Gold Bars (BF)** | 380-420k | ~80-100 gp | **~1.5 gp/xp** | ~12M gp |
| **Steel Bars (mined coal)** | 280-320k | ~50 gp | ~0.67 gp/xp | ~18M gp |

**The verdict:** Gold bars save you **100+ hours of gameplay time** for only ~6M additional GP. Steel is slower *and* costs more in total because of the time investment.

<Callout type="warning">
Steel bars might seem cheaper upfront, but you're trading 30+ hours of AFK training for 6-10M GP savings. That's a bad deal when you could make 250k+ GP/hr thieving or doing other content.
</Callout>

**Use gold bars.** It's not expensive, it's way faster, and you'll get paid back in time saved.

## Blast Furnace Gold (40-99)

The fastest Smithing XP in the game. Period.

### Requirements

- 40 Smithing (60 recommended for official worlds)
- Goldsmith Gauntlets ([Family Crest](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Family_Crest))
- Ice Gloves (or bucket of water)
- The Giant Dwarf started (access to Keldagrim)
- GP for gold ore (~225 gp each from Ordan)

### Method

1. Use official BF world (W358) — NPCs run the furnace for 72k GP/hr fee
2. Buy gold ore from Ordan (BF shop)
3. Equip Goldsmith Gauntlets
4. Deposit ore on conveyor belt
5. Collect bars with Ice Gloves
6. Bank and repeat

### XP Rates

- **380-420k XP/hr** with optimal setup
- ~231,000 gold ore needed for 40-99
- **Cost:** ~52M GP in ore (but you keep the gold bars)

<Callout type="casual">
This is click-intensive and expensive, but nothing else comes close for speed. Good for when you want Smithing done fast.
</Callout>

### Hybrid Method (Gold + Other Ores)

You can mix gold smelting with coal-requiring ores to reduce costs and get useful bars while maintaining high XP rates.

**How it works:**
1. Fill coal bag with 27 coal
2. Deposit 26 gold ore + coal bag contents simultaneously
3. Equip Goldsmith Gauntlets before gold bars are made
4. Every few trips, swap gold ore for mithril/adamant/rune ore

**Ore ratios (gold trips to other):**
| Ore | Ratio | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Mithril | 1:1-2 | Most frequent swaps |
| Adamantite | 1:2-3 | Good balance |
| Runite | 1:3-4 | Least frequent |

Keep ~250 coal in the furnace so higher-tier ores smelt properly.

<Callout type="tip">
This gives you ~250-350k XP/hr while producing useful bars. Great for ironmen who want both XP and resources without pure gold spam.
</Callout>

### Superheat Alternative

If you want passive Magic XP:
- Buy gold ore from Ordan
- Cast [Superheat Item](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Superheat_Item) (43 Magic)
- ~80k Smithing + 75k Magic XP/hr
- More chill than full BF runs

## Giants' Foundry

The resource-efficient alternative — and often profitable.

### Why Giants' Foundry?

- Uses bars OR metal equipment (recycle PvM drops!)
- Often profitable with lower-tier alloys
- Gives reputation for useful rewards
- More engaging than BF gold spam

### How It Works

1. Get commission from Kovac (describes sword type)
2. Select moulds matching the commission
3. Fill crucible with bars/equipment (always use 2 metals for alloys)
4. Pour metal, then work the preform (heat/cool/grind/polish)
5. Hand in for XP, GP, and reputation

### Alloy Recommendations

**Always use two metals** — alloys give massively better scores than single metals.

| Level | Alloy | XP/hr | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| 15-30 | Bronze + Iron | ~50k | Early training, slightly profitable |
| 30-50 | Iron + Steel | ~70k | Good for building reputation |
| 50-70 | Steel + Mithril | ~90-120k | **Profitable** — great for shop buying |
| 70-85 | Mithril + Adamant | ~150-190k | Best balance of XP and cost |
| 85+ | Adamant + Rune | ~200-250k | Highest XP, expensive |

<Callout type="tip">
Steel + Mithril is the sweet spot for ironmen. It's profitable, decent XP, and easy to source from shops.
</Callout>

### Priority Purchases

1. **Double Ammo Mould** (2,000 rep) — Get first, doubles cannonball speed
2. **Smith's Uniform** (15,000 rep total) — 20% faster actions
3. **Moulds** (5,400 rep total) — Better scores = more XP/sword
4. **Ore Packs** (200 rep each) — Useful for supplementing bar supply

### Recommended Shop Route (Pre-Song of the Elves)

For Steel + Mithril alloy (50-70 Smithing):

1. **Cam Torum** — Buy steel warhammers (highest stock, near bank)
2. **Mt. Karuulm** — Buy mithril claws
3. **Jatizso** — Buy steel warhammers + mithril claws

Bank a supply, then do Giants' Foundry runs. This is profitable — you'll make ~10k+ per sword.

For Mithril + Adamant alloy:

1. **Aneirin's Armour (Prifddinas)** — Buy mithril + adamant platebodies
2. Use 14 mithril + 14 adamant per sword (or 9 platebodies + 2 platelegs + 1 plateskirt each)

This is the best XP method at Giants' Foundry but not profitable.

## Using PvM Drops

Giants' Foundry gives purpose to all those alchables from Slayer:

- Rune platebodies, platelegs, med helms → Recycle for rune bars
- Adamant equipment → Mix with mithril or rune
- Mithril equipment → Mix with steel or adamant

**Note:** Equipment gives 1 fewer bar than it costs to make (platebody = 4 bars, not 5). Still worth it for drops you'd otherwise alch.

<Callout type="tip">
Don't smith bars into equipment just to recycle them — you lose a bar per item. Only recycle drops you already have.
</Callout>

## Cannonballs

If you have steel bars and need cannonballs for Slayer:

- Requires [Dwarf Cannon](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dwarf_Cannon) quest
- ~14k XP/hr (very slow)
- **Double Ammo Mould** from Giants' Foundry doubles speed
- Only make what you need for specific cannon tasks

Cannonballs are not a training method — the XP is terrible. Make them when you need them for Slayer, not as a way to train.

## FAQ

### Should I do Blast Furnace or Giants' Foundry?

Blast Furnace gold is faster (380-420k vs 90-250k XP/hr) but costs ~52M GP. Giants' Foundry is often profitable and lets you recycle PvM drops. Most ironmen do some of both — GF early for the unlocks, then BF gold if they want to rush 99.

### Is it worth mining my own ores?

No. It's much faster to make GP (thieving, agility pyramid, early money methods) and buy ores from Ordan. 1 hour of Ardy Knights buys more ore than 5+ hours of MLM.

### What should I buy from Giants' Foundry first?

Double Ammo Mould (2,000 rep). It doubles your cannonball production speed forever. Then work toward Smith's Uniform for the 20% action speed boost.

### How do I get Iron Bars for The Knight's Sword?

There's a spawn west of Graveyard of Shadows in level 18 Wilderness (2 bars spawn). For HCIM, there's a safer spawn at Sunset Coast in Varlamore after Children of the Sun.

### Is Zalcano good for Smithing supplies?

Yes, excellent mid-game. You get gold, mithril, and adamant ores/bars plus GP. Good to do once you have decent combat stats and want a break from other content.

## Common Mistakes

- **Mining your ores** — Buy from Ordan with GP. Mining is way slower than making money and buying.
- **Skipping Ice Gloves** — These save so much time at BF and GF. Get them early.
- **Using single metals at GF** — Always use alloys (two metals). Single metal swords give terrible scores.
- **Smithing bars into items for GF** — You lose 1 bar per item. Only recycle PvM drops, not bars you made.
- **Training with cannonballs** — 14k XP/hr is awful. Only make what you need for Slayer.
- **Ignoring Double Ammo Mould** — This is your first GF purchase. Doubles cannonball production forever.
