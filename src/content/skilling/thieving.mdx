Thieving on Ironman is unique: it's the primary bottleneck unlock for Herblore (herb seeds), a massive money-maker (15M+ coins), and a gear pipeline (Rogue outfit). Unlike pure skillers, Ironmen train thieving not just for XP—they train it for survival.

**At a glance:**
- **Quest speedrun (1-18):** 30 minutes, Hazeel Cult + Fight Arena
- **Fruit stalls (18-38):** ~3 hours, prepare for herbs
- **Master Farmers (38-50+):** CRITICAL - Stockpile herb seeds for Herblore
- **Rogue outfit (50 Agility + Thieving):** 1-2 hours, game-changer for profit
- **Ardy Knights (55-99):** AFK-friendly, 15M+ coins, 50-70 hours total
- **Alternatives:** Wealthy Citizens (semi-AFK, 30-50k XP/hr) or Stealing Valuables (70-105k XP/hr + coins)

## Early Game: Quest Speedrun & Stall Training (Level 1-38)

### Quest Experience Speedrun (1-18)

Complete these two quests back-to-back for instant level jump:

**Hazeel Cult Quest**
- **XP Reward:** 1,500 Thieving XP
- **Requirements:** None
- **Quest Length:** 5-10 minutes
- **Strategy:** Path 1 requires no combat (talk through dialogue)

**Fight Arena Quest**
- **XP Reward:** 2,175 Thieving XP
- **Requirements:** None (can be safespotted)
- **Quest Length:** 10-15 minutes
- **Combined XP:** 3,675 XP (reaches level 18)
- **Total Time:** 30 minutes

You now skip the first 15+ hours of grinding.

<Callout type="tip">
Do these two quests immediately on any account. They're fastest early XP in the game.
</Callout>

### Fruit Stalls (Levels 18-38)

After quest speedrun, use Fruit Stalls to reach the critical 38 Thieving unlock.

**Specifications:**
- **Location:** Hosidius region (Leaf Street Stall)
- **Level Required:** 25 (but start at 18 with decent failure rate)
- **XP per steal:** 20-40 XP
- **XP Rate:** 42,000 XP/hr (dropping all loot for pure XP)
- **Time to 38:** ~3 hours from level 18
- **Strategy:** Stand directly under the stall NPC to avoid guards
- **Tip:** Set NPC "Attack" options to "Hidden" so Pickpocket is left-click

**Why Fruit Stalls?**
- Significantly faster than earlier pickpocketing methods
- No combat required
- Prepare your account for the Master Farmer grind
- Early access to coins if you sell fruit

<Callout type="warning">
You will fail pickpockets at level 18-25. This is normal. Standing directly under the NPC reduces guard aggro, making it manageable.
</Callout>

## CRITICAL: Master Farmers (Levels 38-50+) ⭐⭐⭐

**This is the single most important section of thieving for Ironman. Master Farmers unlock Herblore training. Prioritize this over pure XP.**

### Why Master Farmers Matter

Master Farmers are the **only reliable source** of high-level herb seeds for Ironman:

**Seeds Available by Level:**
- Level 38+: Ranarr seeds (Herblore starting point)
- Level 45+: Snapdragon seeds (mid-level herbs)
- Level 60+: Torstol seeds (elite herbs)
- Bonus: Also provide low-level seeds (potato, parsnip, etc.)

**Seed Farming Rates:**
- Level 38: ~758 pickpockets/hour, 57.5% success = ~436 seeds/hour
- Level 75: ~95% success = ~720 seeds/hour
- Level 99: ~99% success = ~750 seeds/hour

**Over 12 hours of Master Farmer grinding:**
- 300-500 high-level herb seeds
- This sustains your entire Herblore grind from 30-99
- Without this: Herblore progression is severely bottlenecked

### Master Farmer Location & Mechanics

**Locations:**
- Hosidius region (most accessible)
- Ardougne (east of bank)
- Draynor Village
- Any farming location has a Master Farmer

**Pickpocketing Mechanics:**
- 60 XP per successful pickpocket
- Failure rate decreases with level (57.5% at level 38, near-zero at 75+)
- Low-intensity compared to late-game methods
- AFK-friendly (click every 3-4 seconds)

### Gear for Master Farmers

**Essential:**
- None (optional Dodgy Necklace at low levels for survivability)

**Recommended:**
- Graceful outfit (reduces weight if available)
- Food (optional, few NPCs damage you at low level)
- Stamina potions (optional, minimal running)

**Don't use:**
- Gloves of Silence (bonus too small to matter)
- Shadow Veil (costs more than benefit)

### Optimal Master Farmer Strategy

**Phase 1: Rapid Leveling (38-50, 8-10 hours)**
- Pickpocket Master Farmers continuously
- Drop all non-seed loot (prioritize XP over coin/items)
- Keep Ranarr, Snapdragon, Torstol seeds
- Drop everything else
- Expected: 400-500 herb seeds by level 50

**Phase 2: Continued Farming (50+, optional)**
- Can continue to 55-60+ if you want extra seeds
- Recommended: 12+ hours total at Master Farmers
- Expected: 500-1000+ herb seeds for entire Herblore grind

**Why Drop Loot Early?**
- Herb seeds value >>> coin/item value at this stage
- You're building a resource, not making money yet
- Money comes later from Ardy Knights (55-99)

<Callout type="efficiency">
Spend 12+ hours at Master Farmers between levels 38-55. This is not wasted time—it's investing in Herblore efficiency for the next 30+ hours of training. You'll thank yourself when you have 1000 herb seeds for Herblore.
</Callout>

## Mid Game: Rogue Outfit & Level-Up Methods (Levels 38-55)

### Getting the Rogue Outfit (Essential First)

The Rogue outfit is a **game-changer** for late-game thieving profitability.

**Specifications:**
- **Requirement:** 50 Thieving + 50 Agility
- **Location:** Rogues' Den (underground dungeon near Ardougne)
- **Obtaining Method:** Solving a platforming maze with timed obstacles
- **Time Required:** 1-2 hours at minimum stats (50/50), 30 minutes at 70+/70+
- **Pieces:** Rogue mask, rogue top, rogue trousers, rogue gloves, rogue boots
- **Effect:** Guarantees double loot from pickpocketing (when wearing full set)

**Why This Is Essential:**
- 1-2 hour investment = 2-3x profit for 40+ hours of Ardy Knights
- Early ROI is massive
- Makes late-game thieving genuinely lucrative

**Gear Setup for Rogues' Den:**
- Graceful outfit (highly recommended)
- Food for healing (traps deal minor damage)
- Stamina potions (optional, lots of running)
- Anything you don't mind losing (items drop on spikes)

**Timeline:**
- Reach 50 Thieving: ~12 hours (from quest start)
- Reach 50 Agility: Should be done by now naturally
- Spend 1-2 hours in Rogues' Den
- Emerge with full Rogue outfit = instant profit multiplier

<Callout type="tip">
Do Rogues' Den immediately after 50 Agility + Thieving. The time investment is trivial compared to the profit multiplier it provides for the rest of your account.
</Callout>

### Alternative Mid-Game Methods (Optional)

If you want variety from Master Farmers:

**Wealthy Citizens (Level 50+)**
- **XP Rate:** 30-50k XP/hr
- **Mechanism:** Street urchins distract citizens every 84-99 seconds for 100% success windows
- **Loot:** 85 coins, house keys (1 per 17 pickpockets), clues
- **Best For:** Semi-AFK alternative to Master Farmers
- **Profit:** Modest (~100 coins per pickpocket)
- **Verdict:** Valid but not better than alternatives; more about variety

**Stealing Valuables (Level 50+, requires house keys from Wealthy Citizens)**
- **XP Rate:** 70-105k XP/hr
- **Profit:** 90-150k coins/hr
- **Mechanic:** Pick house locks, steal valuables, exit through window before homeowner returns (180-190 second timer)
- **Best For:** Semi-AFK money-making while training XP
- **Verdict:** Excellent balance of XP and money, great if you want breaks from Master Farmers

<Callout type="note">
Wealthy Citizens requires house keys (rare drops). If you're just training thieving, prioritize Master Farmers for seeds. Use Wealthy Citizens/Stealing Valuables as optional variety activities.
</Callout>

## Late Game: Ardy Knights (Levels 55-99) — The Money Maker

Ardy Knights is where thieving becomes a **major money source**. This single method generates 15M+ coins while reaching 99.

### Location & Access

**Location:** East Ardougne, north of castle
- Bank nearby (fairy ring AKQ)
- Coin pouches don't degrade
- Can bank coin pouches if desired (adds 15-30 seconds per trip)

### Experience Rates by Level

| Level | XP/hr | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 55 | 86,000 | Early stage, decent success rate |
| 60 | 98,000 | Improving |
| 75 | 120,000 | Solid middle ground |
| 85 | 150,000 | Getting good |
| 94+ | 240-280,000 | Hard Diary eliminates failures |
| 99 (full setup) | 280,000+ | Peak efficiency |

**Failure Rates:**
- Level 55: ~45% fail rate
- Level 75: ~25% fail rate
- Level 94+: 0% fail rate (with Hard Ardougne Diary)

### Profit by Level (With Rogue Outfit)

| Level | GP/hr | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 55 | 60,000 | Early stage |
| 70 | 120,000 | Double pickpocket loot |
| 85 | 180,000 | Rare drops |
| 95 | 250,000+ | Peak rates |
| 99 | 280-300,000 | Maximum with diary |

**Total 55-99 Profit:** ~15,000,000 coins
- This is your early-mid game funding for everything else
- Construction, Magic supplies, PvM items, prayer training gear

### Achievement Diary Progression

**Medium Ardougne Diary (HIGHLY RECOMMENDED)**
- Effect: +10% pickpocket success for Ardougne NPCs
- Reduces failures significantly at mid-levels
- Relatively easy tasks (accessible for mid-game ironman)
- **ROI: MASSIVE** (eliminates 30% of failures)

**Hard Ardougne Diary (ESSENTIAL for endgame)**
- Effect: +10% pickpocket success for ALL pickpocketing (global)
- At level 94+: Grants 100% success rate (literally no failures)
- Harder tasks but worthwhile
- Allows true AFK at 94+ (no need to babysit for failures)

**Impact:**
- Without diary: 280-300k GP/hr at 99
- With hard diary: 280-300k GP/hr but zero failures (stress-free)

### AFK-Friendly Setup (Level 94+ with Hard Diary)

Once you reach 94 Thieving + Hard Ardougne Diary, Ardy Knights becomes **99% AFK:**

**What's AFK:**
- Click Ardy Knight once every 3-5 seconds (absolutely zero attention needed)
- No food required
- No stamina potion needed
- No failure management
- Can literally watch streams/YouTube for entire session

**What's Not AFK:**
- Banking coin pouches (optional, adds 15-30 seconds per trip)
- Equipment swaps (once per session)

**Realistic Setup:**
- Wear Rogue outfit (full set for double loot)
- Ardy Cloak 4 (with hard diary)
- No other gear needed
- Coin pouch fills, deposit, repeat

### Failure Avoidance Before Level 94

**Before Hard Diary:**
- Use Dodgy Necklace (level 55+): 25% chance to negate stun/damage on fail
- Wear Gloves of Silence (Hunter 54): +5% success (becomes redundant with diary)
- Use Shadow Veil spell (Magic 47): 15% avoid chance

**Best Strategy:**
- Complete Medium Diary ASAP (eliminates 30% of failures)
- Grind to 94 Thieving + Hard Diary
- Then true AFK is achieved

### Money vs Loot Decision

**Early Ardy Knights (55-70):** Collect coin pouches
- Small amounts of money, value building stage
- Don't need to manage loot inventory

**Mid Ardy Knights (70-85):** Continue collecting
- Profit ramping up (120-180k/hr)
- Rogue outfit drops becoming valuable

**Late Ardy Knights (85-94):** Full collection
- High profit (180-250k/hr)
- All loot valuable

**Endgame (94-99):** Bank regularly
- Maximum profit (280-300k/hr)
- Can afford banktrips (no time cost relative to XP)

**Total Expected Loot:**
- Coins: 15M+
- Rogue equipment drops: 0-5 extra pieces (sellable)
- Herb/Seed drops: Varies
- Total sellable: 15-20M GP equivalent

## Wealthy Citizens & Stealing Valuables (Level 50+ Alternatives)

### Wealthy Citizens (March 2024 Varlamore Content)

**Location:** Bazaar at Civitas illa Fortis
**Requirements:** Level 50 Thieving, Children of the Sun quest

**How It Works:**
- Every 84-99 seconds, three street urchins distract nearby wealthy citizens
- During distraction: **100% success rate** (guaranteed pickpocket)
- Outside distraction: Success based on level (46% at 50, 79% at 99)
- 96 XP per successful pickpocket

**XP Rate:** 30-50k XP/hr (depends on engagement)

**Loot:**
- 85 coins per pickpocket (almost always)
- House keys (1 per ~17 pickpockets, needed for Stealing Valuables)
- Easy clue scrolls (rare)

**Is It Worth Doing?**

Verdict: **Optional, not essential**
- Lower XP than Ardy Knights (30-50k vs 86-280k)
- Lower profit than Ardy Knights
- Advantage: Very semi-AFK due to distraction mechanic
- Best Use: Alternative to Master Farmers if you want variety
- Profit is mostly from house keys → Stealing Valuables

<Callout type="tip">
Use Wealthy Citizens as a change-of-pace if you're grinding Master Farmers for 12+ hours. Semi-AFK nature is perfect for relaxing while still making progress.
</Callout>

### Stealing Valuables (Level 50+, Requires House Keys)

**Requirements:** 50 Thieving, Children of the Sun quest, house keys

**How It Works:**
- Obtain house keys from pickpocketing Wealthy Citizens (1 key per 17 pickpockets)
- Break into house, search chests/wardrobes/jewelry boxes
- 45 XP per item stolen
- Must exit through window before homeowner returns (180-190 second timer)
- Fail to exit: Lose ~90% of stolen goods

**Specifications:**
- **XP Rate:** 70-105k XP/hr
- **Profit:** 90-150k coins/hr (valuables sell to Oriana)
- **Average Haul:** 1,600-2,300 valuables from 18-19 houses/hour
- **Per Valuble:** 55 coins (65 with Fortis Colosseum Gladiator rank)
- **Effort:** Low-intensity, semi-AFK (timers are forgiving)

**Is It Worth Doing?**

Verdict: **YES, great alternative to Ardy Knights for variety**
- Better XP than Wealthy Citizens (70-105k vs 30-50k)
- Decent profit (90-150k coins/hr)
- Much lower intensity than pickpocketing
- Great for training while doing other things
- No risk (if you miss timer, you just lose items, not your life)

**Best Use:** Levels 50-65 as alternative to Master Farmers if you want variety and profit simultaneously

- Ardy Knights with zero failures (true AFK)

- **Outcome:** 99 Thieving, 15M+ coins, no more failures

## FAQ

**Q: How long does thieving take to 99?**

A: 50-70 hours depending on route. Master Farmers (38-50) takes 12+ hours because you're farming seeds, not pure XP. Ardy Knights (55-99) takes 30-40 hours. Total: roughly mid-game investment.

**Q: Do I have to do Master Farmers?**

A: No, but you'll regret it. Herblore will be bottlenecked for 30+ hours without 500+ herb seeds. Spend 12 hours farming now, save 30 hours of Herblore grind later.

**Q: Is Ardy Knights really AFK?**

A: At 94+ with Hard Diary: 99% AFK. Click once every 3-5 seconds, zero attention needed. You can literally watch YouTube for entire sessions.

**Q: How much does Rogue outfit matter?**

A: Doubles your profit. 60k GP/hr → 120k GP/hr. Worth 1-2 hours of Rogues' Den effort.

**Q: Should I do Wealthy Citizens or Stealing Valuables?**

A: Stealing Valuables (70-105k XP/hr + 90-150k coins/hr) is better value than Wealthy Citizens (30-50k XP/hr). Use either as variety from Master Farmers if you want a break.

**Q: When do I complete Achievement Diaries?**

A: Medium Diary: By level 50-60 (relatively easy, huge ROI). Hard Diary: By level 85-90 (hard tasks, but worth it for true AFK at 94+).

**Q: Can I skip Graceful outfit?**

A: Yes, but you'll waste stamina potions. Graceful is quality-of-life. Get it from [Agility](/skilling/agility/) (13-16 hours) if you're doing Agility anyway.

**Q: What's the difference between coin pouches and banking?**

A: Coin pouches are AFK (auto-fill inventory). Banking is faster profit/hour but requires 15-30 second trips. At 55-70, skip banking. At 85+, bank for efficiency.
