Thieving is the bottleneck unlock for Ironman progression. Master Farmers provide the herb seeds you need for Herblore (1000+ seeds possible), while Ardy Knights generate 15M+ coins for your entire mid-game. This guide covers the fastest path, the most profitable methods, and the critical Master Farmer unlock.

**At a glance:**
- **Quest speedrun (1-18):** 30 minutes, Hazeel Cult + Fight Arena
- **Early stall training (18-38):** 3 hours (Tea or Fruit stalls)
- **CRITICAL: Master Farmers (38-50+):** 12+ hours for 1000+ herb seeds (Herblore foundation)
- **Rogue outfit (50 Agility + Thieving):** 1-2 hours, doubles all pickpocket loot
- **Ardy Knights (55-99):** AFK-friendly, 15M+ coins, best balanced method
- **Fastest XP:** Blackjacking (45-91, 260k+/hr) or Pyramid Plunder (91-99, 270-300k/hr)
- **Best Profit:** Vyres (82+, 2.5M/hr) or Elves (85+, 3M/hr)

## Early Game Thieving: Quest Speedrun & Stall Training (Level 1-38)

### Quest Experience Speedrun (1-18)

Two quick quests unlock 3,675 XP instantly:

**Hazeel Cult Quest**
- **XP Reward:** 1,500 Thieving XP
- **Requirements:** None
- **Quest Length:** 5-10 minutes
- **Strategy:** Choose Path 1 (no combat required)

**Fight Arena Quest**
- **XP Reward:** 2,175 Thieving XP
- **Requirements:** None (can be safespotted if needed)
- **Quest Length:** 10-15 minutes
- **Combined:** 3,675 XP = Level 18
- **Total Time:** 30 minutes

You skip the first 15+ hours of tedious grinding.

<Callout type="tip">
Do these two quests immediately on any account. They're among the fastest early XP in OSRS for any skill.
</Callout>

### Best Early Thieving Methods for Ironman (Tea Stalls vs Fruit Stalls)

After quest speedrun, reach 38 Thieving using one of two stall methods:

#### Tea Stalls (Level 5+)

**Specifications:**
- **Location:** Various (Taverley, Rimmington, Ardougne)
- **XP Rate:** 40,000+ XP/hr
- **Failure Rate:** Moderate at early levels
- **Guards:** None nearby - No guard mechanics
- **Best For:** Pure speed, safety
- **Reputation:** Fastest early stall option

**Why Tea Stalls:**
- No guards to manage
- Consistent high XP rates (40k+/hr)
- Available at level 5
- Zero complexity

#### Fruit Stalls - Hosidius (Level 25+)

**Specifications:**
- **Location:** Leaf Street (Great Kourend)
- **Level:** 25 (but accessible from 18 with decent success rate)
- **XP Rate:** 42,750 XP/hr
- **Time to 38:** ~3 hours from level 18
- **Requirements:** Optional 15% Hosidius favour
- **Best For:** Slightly better XP, Varlamore location synergy

**Why Fruit Stalls:**
- Slightly higher XP than tea (42.75k vs 40k)
- Stand under NPC to avoid guards
- Access to Galanova fruits (valuable resource)

### Comparison: Tea vs Fruit Stalls

| Factor | Tea Stalls | Fruit Stalls |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **Speed to 38** | ~4 hours | ~3 hours |
| **XP/hr** | 40k | 42.75k |
| **Guard Mechanics** | None | Avoidable |
| **Difficulty** | Very Easy | Easy |
| **Galanova Fruits** | No | Yes (valuable) |
| **Favour Needed** | None | Optional 15% |
| **Level** | 5+ | 25+ |

**Recommendation:** Fruit stalls if you're committed to Varlamore content. Tea stalls if you want maximum safety and simplicity. Both reach 38 in under 4 hours.

<Callout type="efficiency">
Galanova fruits from fruit stalls are valuable (used for Botanical Pies, +4 Herblore boost). If choosing fruit stalls, keep all Galanova fruits obtained.
</Callout>

## CRITICAL: Master Farmers - Best Herb Seed Source for Ironman (1000+ Seeds/Hour at High Level)

**This is the single most important section for Ironman. Master Farmers unlock Herblore progression. Everything else is supplementary.**

### Why Master Farmers Are Essential

Master Farmers are the PRIMARY and most efficient source of high-level herb seeds:

**Herb Seed Drop Rates:**
- **Ranarr Seeds:** 1 per 302 pickpockets (0.33%)
- **Snapdragon Seeds:** 1 per 2,083 pickpockets (0.048%)
- **Torstol Seeds:** Extremely rare, scales with Farming level

**Seed Farming Potential:**
- **At Level 38:** ~436 seeds/hour
- **At Level 75:** ~720 seeds/hour
- **At Level 99:** ~750 seeds/hour (with 100% success rate)

**Over 12 Hours of Master Farmer Grinding:**
- 5,000-9,000 total herb seeds
- 300-500 Ranarr + Snapdragon seeds specifically
- **This sustains your entire Herblore grind from 30-99**

Without Master Farmers, Herblore becomes severely bottlenecked. You'll spend 30+ extra hours grinding alternative seed sources.

### Master Farmer Locations & Mechanics

**Optimal Locations:**
- **Hosidius** (best for early game)
- **Ardougne** (east of bank)
- **Draynor Village**
- **Any farming location**

**Success Rates by Level:**
- Level 38: 57.5% success rate
- Level 75: ~95% success
- Level 99: 94.1% success rate

**Failure Mechanics:**
- Failed pickpockets deal 1-2 damage and stun briefly
- Dodgy Necklace (25% chance) helps early levels
- Gloves of Silence (+5% success) minimal impact
- Hard Ardougne Diary (+10% success) eventually eliminates failures at 94+

### Optimal Master Farmer Strategy

**Phase 1: Rapid Level Gain (38-50, 8-10 Hours)**
- Pickpocket Master Farmers continuously
- **Drop all non-seed loot** (prioritize XP over coin/items)
- **Keep all Ranarr, Snapdragon, Torstol seeds**
- Drop common seeds (potato, parsnip, etc.)
- Expected yield: 400-500 high-level seeds by level 50

**Phase 2: Extended Farming (50-55+, 8-12 Hours)**
- Continue Master Farmer pickpocketing
- Build seed reserve for entire Herblore progression
- Expected total: 1000-1500 herb seeds by level 55
- Money from loot becomes secondary benefit

**Why Drop Loot Early?**
- Herb seeds value >>> coin/item value at this stage
- You're building a resource foundation, not making money yet
- Money comes later from Ardy Knights (55-99)
- Building seed redundancy prevents future bottlenecks

<Callout type="warning">
Commit to 12+ hours at Master Farmers between levels 38-55. This feels long, but it saves 30+ hours of Herblore bottleneck later. Invest early for exponential long-term value.
</Callout>

### Master Farmers vs Other Seed Sources

**Master Farmers alone are NOT the only source, but they're the most efficient:**

| Source | Seeds/Hour | Time Investment | Best For |
|--------|-----------|---|---|
| **Master Farmers** | 400-750 | Active grinding | Volume (4000+ seeds) |
| Farming Contracts | 50-200/day | Daily 5-10 mins | Passive supplement |
| Kingdom Management | 20-50/day | Passive | Long-term passive |
| Slayer Drops | 100-500/task | Passive during slaying | Secondary |
| Bird Houses | 30-100/day | Passive Fossil Island | Long-term passive |

**Optimal Strategy:** Master Farmers for active grinding + Kingdom + Contracts for passive gains = unlimited seeds long-term.

## Mid Game: Rogue Outfit & Progression (Levels 38-55)

### Obtaining the Rogue Outfit - Game-Changing Gear

The **Rogue outfit is the single biggest profitability multiplier** in thieving for Ironman.

**Specifications:**
- **Requirement:** 50 Thieving + 50 Agility
- **Location:** Rogues' Den (platforming maze)
- **Time Required:** 1-2 hours at minimum stats (50/50), 30 minutes at 70+/70+
- **Full Set Effect:** **100% guaranteed double loot from pickpocketing**
- **Set Pieces:** Rogue mask, rogue top, rogue trousers, rogue gloves, rogue boots

**Why It Matters:**
- **Without Rogue outfit:** Ardy Knights = 60-120k GP/hr
- **With full Rogue outfit:** Ardy Knights = 200-300k GP/hr
- **Profit multiplier:** 2-3x for 40+ hours of endgame grinding
- **ROI:** 1-2 hours of platforming = 50-100M additional GP

This is non-negotiable for competitive thieving progression.

**Rogues' Den Platforming:**
- Navigate timed obstacles and jumps
- Fall = Start from beginning (no actual penalty)
- Expect: 20-30 attempts at low Agility/Thieving
- Expect: 5-10 attempts at 70+ stats
- Brings full rogue outfit upon completion (one-time reward)

<Callout type="efficiency">
Complete Rogues' Den immediately after reaching 50 Thieving + 50 Agility. The time investment is trivial compared to the profit multiplier for the rest of your account.
</Callout>

## Mid Game: Alternative Methods for Variety (Levels 50-55)

If you want breaks from Master Farmer grinding:

### Wealthy Citizens - Semi-AFK with Distraction Mechanic (Level 50+)

**Specifications:**
- **XP Rate:** 30-50k XP/hr
- **Mechanism:** Street urchins automatically distract citizens every 84-99 seconds (100% success during window)
- **Loot:** 85 coins per pickpocket, house keys (1 per 17 pickpockets)
- **Effort:** Very low - mostly automatic

**Unlocks:** Stealing Valuables activity (70-105k XP/hr)

### Stealing Valuables - Semi-AFK Money + XP (Level 50+)

**Specifications:**
- **XP Rate:** 70-105k XP/hr
- **Profit:** 90-150k coins/hr
- **Requirements:** House keys (from Wealthy Citizens pickpocketing)
- **Mechanic:** Break into houses, steal valuables, exit within 180-190 seconds
- **Best For:** Players wanting lower intensity while still earning money and XP

**Verdict:** Both optional. Use as breaks from Master Farmers if desired, but Master Farmers priority is higher for seed acquisition.

## Best Ardy Knights Setup for Ironman (AFK-Friendly, 300k GP/Hr)

Ardy Knights is the gold standard AFK thieving method for Ironman.

### Why Ardy Knights Dominate

**Experience Rates by Level:**
- Level 55: 86,000 XP/hr
- Level 75: 120,000 XP/hr
- Level 94+: 240-280,000 XP/hr (with Hard Ardougne Diary)

**Profit with Rogue Outfit:**
- Level 55: 60,000 GP/hr
- Level 75: 150,000 GP/hr
- Level 95+: 280,300,000 GP/hr

**Total 55-99 Profit:** 15M+ coins

**Why It's Best for Ironman:**
- AFK from level 94+ with Hard Diary (literally no failures)
- Generates 15M coins for entire mid-game (Construction, Magic, Prayer)
- No special requirements (just pickpocket)
- Best XP/money/AFK balance

### Achievement Diary Progression

**Medium Ardougne Diary (HIGHLY RECOMMENDED)**
- Effect: +10% pickpocket success for Ardougne NPCs
- Eliminates ~30% of failures
- Relatively easy tasks
- **Do this by level 60-70**

**Hard Ardougne Diary (ESSENTIAL for endgame)**
- Effect: +10% pickpocket success for ALL pickpocketing
- At 94+ Thieving: 100% success rate (literally zero failures)
- Enables true AFK training
- **Do this by level 85-90**
- Result: 99% afk from 94-99 (click every 3-5 seconds)

### Optimal Ardy Knights Gear Setup

**Essential:**
- Full Rogue outfit (doubles loot)
- Graceful outfit (optional, weight reduction helps)

**Recommended:**
- Dodgy Necklace (early levels, 55-70)
- Medium/Hard Ardougne Diary
- Stamina potions (optional, minimal energy drain)

**Advanced:**
- Ardy Cloak 4 (with hard diary)
- Gloves of Silence (early only, diary makes irrelevant)

|---|---|---|---|
| 55-75 | 20 | 2-3M | Before Medium |
| 75-94 | 25 | 5-8M | Medium complete by 70 |

## Best High-Profit Thieving Methods for Ironman (Vyres & Elves 82+)

After reaching endgame, maximize profit with these methods:

### Pickpocketing Vyres (82+ Thieving)

**Specifications:**
- **XP Rate:** 306.9 XP per successful pickpocket
- **Profit:** 1.5M-2.5M GP/hr with Rogue outfit
- **Loot:** Blood shards (rare), coins, unique items
- **Effort:** Click-intensive (not AFK)
- **Best For:** Profit-focused grind

### Pickpocketing Elves (85+ Thieving)

**Specifications:**
- **XP Rate:** 353.3 XP per pickpocket
- **Profit:** 1.5M-2M GP/hr base, **3M GP/hr at 99**
- **Loot:** Crystal shards (common), coins, gems
- **Effort:** Click-intensive (not AFK)
- **Gear Bonus:** 50% profit boost with full Rogue outfit at 99
- **Best For:** Maximum endgame profit

### Comparative Analysis: Profit Methods

| Method | Level | Profit/hr | XP/hr | Best For |
|--------|-------|---|---|---|
| Vyres | 82+ | 1.5-2.5M | High | Early endgame profit |
| Elves | 85+ | 1.5-3M | High | Peak profit (99 = 3M/hr) |
| Ardy Knights | 55+ | 60-300k | 86-280k | AFK + balance |

**Strategy:** Many Ironmen do Ardy Knights 55-99 (AFK + money), then occasionally grind Vyres/Elves for additional profit when they need bulk money.
