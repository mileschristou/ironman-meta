Runecraft is the gatekeeper to endgame PvM. God spells, Sinister Offering, Demonic Offering, and wrath runes for high-level magic content all require Runecrafting levels. The challenge for ironmen is pure essence — you can't buy it from the GE, so most training methods become bottlenecked by supply.

The Guardians of the Rift (GOTR) minigame changes everything. It produces essence without consuming it, meaning you can train runecrafting at your own pace without grinding essence specifically. Most ironmen spend 100-200 hours here and get rewarded with the Colossal Pouch and Raiments of the Eye — items that make all subsequent runecrafting better forever.

## Key Milestones

| Level | Unlock | Why It Matters |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| 27 | GOTR access | Best early ironman RC method |
| 44 | Adept catalysts in GOTR | XP rate increase |
| 50 | Lava runes | Fast XP if you have essence stockpiled |
| 59 | Expert catalysts | Further GOTR boost |
| 75 | Master catalysts | Best GOTR XP rate (60k+/hr) |
| 77 | Blood runes (Arceuus) | AFK income, passive rune supply |
| 85 | Colossal Pouch | 40 essence per trip, massive efficiency |
| 90 | Soul runes | Better income than blood runes |
| 95 | Wrath runes | Required for God spells + Demonic Offering |
| 99 | Max cape | Runecrafting complete |

After 95, most ironmen get 99 passively through Blood/Soul rune training, which produces both runes and XP simultaneously.

## The Core Ironman Problem: Pure Essence

Pure essence is required for almost every runecraft method above level 1 runes. Unlike mains who can buy essence from the GE, ironmen must source it passively. This means most traditional runecraft methods become impractical — you'd spend more time grinding essence than training runecrafting.

Here's where ironmen get their essence:

| Source | Rate | Notes |
|--------|------|-------|
| Ardougne Diary daily | 100-250/day | Easy, scales with diary tier |
| Slayer drops | Passive | Wyrms, gargoyles, bloodvelds — main passive source |
| Temple Trekking | Passive bonus | Random chance during trekking |
| Soul Wars | ~0-60/game | Kills drop bones and essence, secondary benefit |
| Essence mining (Rune Essence Mine) | Low | Not recommended — slow and inefficient |

<Callout type="tip">
Don't grind essence specifically. Let Slayer and diary tasks drip-feed your supply. Use GOTR in the meantime — it produces essence without consuming it.
</Callout>

For most ironmen, the decision is simple: **use GOTR until 77+, then switch to Blood/Soul runes.**

## GOTR: The Ironman Default

Guardians of the Rift is the single best runecrafting method for ironmen. Full details in the [dedicated GOTR guide](/guides/gotr/), but here's the summary:

- Located at Temple of the Eye (accessed via Apprentice Tamara east of Salve Graveyard)
- Mine guardian fragments → craft into essence → imbue at altars
- **Zero essence consumed** — runs on fragments you mine during the minigame
- Earn Abyssal Pearls to buy Colossal Pouch, Raiments of the Eye, and other upgrades
- Passive Mining (10k/hr), Crafting (6k/hr), and Prayer XP gains on top of RC training

### XP Rates by Level

| RC Level | XP/hr | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| 27-44 | ~20-30k | Early levels, weak essence only |
| 44-59 | ~35-45k | Adept catalysts available |
| 59-75 | ~50-60k | Expert catalysts unlock |
| 75+ | ~60-70k | Master catalysts, full efficiency |

With Colossal Pouch at 85+, you gain another 5-10k XP/hr because you carry more essence and bank less.

<Callout type="efficiency">
Do GOTR until you have:
1. Colossal Pouch (250 pearls, huge efficiency spike)
2. Raiments of the Eye (full set, 2,500 pearls, +60% rune production)

After that, you can stay for the passive XP or transition to other methods. Most ironmen finish around 77 RC and move to Blood runes, returning to GOTR later.
</Callout>

## Lava Runes: Fastest XP (For Essence Hoarders)

Lava runes are the fastest runecrafting method in OSRS — 50-102k XP/hr depending on your level. However, they consume essence rapidly, making them impractical for most ironmen unless you've stockpiled a large supply.

### Setup

- **Level 23 Runecrafting** (hard requirement)
- **Earth talisman or Ring of the Elements** (talisman is free, ring costs 300 Abyssal Pearls)
- **Binding necklace** (1 charge per essence, need spares)
- **Pure essence** (lots of it)
- **Magic Imbue spell** (82 Magic, or bring earth runes if you don't have magic)

### Method

1. Fill inventory with pure essence and binding necklace
2. Cast Magic Imbue (or use earth runes + binding necklace)
3. Teleport to Lava Maze (if you have the talisman) or navigate there manually
4. Use essence on the lava rifts to craft lava runes
5. Return to Ferox Enclave, teleport back, repeat

### XP Rates

- **50 RC:** ~50k XP/hr
- **60 RC:** ~65k XP/hr
- **75 RC:** ~85k XP/hr
- **85 RC:** ~95k XP/hr
- **99 RC:** ~102k XP/hr

<Callout type="warning">
Lava runes burn through essence fast — expect to use 1,000+ essence per hour. For reference, you'll get roughly 300-500 essence per day from Slayer tasks. Don't use this method unless you have at least 10,000 essence stockpiled, and even then, only use it if you have a comfortable buffer.
</Callout>

### When to Use Lava Runes

- **Post-Slayer:** After hundreds of hours of Slayer, you've accumulated a large essence stockpile and want to finish 99 quickly
- **As a bridge:** 50-77 RC between GOTR and Blood runes, if you prefer faster XP
- **For variety:** Some players find GOTR repetitive and prefer lava runes despite the essence cost

For most ironmen, GOTR is more realistic because it doesn't require pre-existing essence.

## Blood Runes: AFK Income (77+ RC)

At 77 Runecrafting, you unlock Arceuus favour (Zeah), which gives you access to blood runes. This is where runecrafting becomes self-sustaining for ironmen.

### Why Blood Runes Matter

- **37-45k XP/hr** of completely AFK training
- Blood runes are useful for ironmen: Sanguinesti Staff charges, scythe charges, high-alch value (10,500 GP each)
- No talisman needed — just pure essence and walking distance
- 100% Arceuus favour required (simple, passive grind)

### Method

1. Travel to Zeah (via boat or teleport)
2. Go north of Arceuus to the Dark Altar
3. Run essence to the altar, craft blood runes
4. Bank at nearest bank (Arceuus Library if you're 100% favour)
5. Repeat

### Speed Improvements

- **NPC Contact** (73 Magic, Lunar spellbook): Teleport to Arceuus house directly, skipping the walk back to bank
- **Ring of the Elements:** Slightly faster than walking (if you have pearls to spare)
- **Stamina potions:** Only worth it if you're speed-running; usually not necessary for AFK

<Callout type="tip">
Blood runes are one of the best passive runecraft training methods. You're AFK, producing runes you'll actually use, and getting paid in XP. This is the sweet spot for 77-99.
</Callout>

## Soul Runes: 90+ AFK

Soul runes are similar to blood runes but require 90 RC and access to the Catacomb of Souls (beneath Port Phasmatys).

- **35-50k XP/hr** of AFK training (slightly lower than blood)
- Better GP value per rune (~12,500 GP each) than blood runes
- Requires 90 RC and completion of Morytania Diary for efficient banking
- Otherwise identical to blood rune method

Most ironmen skip soul runes and go straight to wrath runes at 95, but if you need the GP equivalent, souls are slightly better.

## ZMI / Ourania Altar: Situational Variety

The Ourania Altar (also called ZMI Altar) crafts random runes based on your runecrafting level. It's located at the Ourania Altar in the wilderness-adjacent area.

- **71 RC required**
- **20-50k XP/hr** depending on level and essence type
- No talisman needed
- Produces mixed runes (random based on your level)

**When to use:** Only if you specifically need rune variety. For pure XP or income, GOTR, Lava, and Blood runes are all superior.

## Wrath Runes: Endgame Unlock (95+ RC)

Wrath runes are the endgame unlock for runecrafting. They're not a grinding method, but rather a milestone unlock that enables key spells.

### Requirements

- **95 Runecrafting** (hard requirement)
- **Dragon Slayer II** (to access the Wrath altar in Myth's Guild basement)
- **Pure essence** (as with all rune crafting)

### Why Wrath Runes Matter

Wrath runes unlock three critical spell categories:

1. **God Spells**
   - Saradomin Strike (magic attack)
   - Flames of Zamorak (magic attack)
   - Claws of Guthix (attack+magic)

2. **Sinister Offering** (70 Magic, Arceuus)
   - Cast on bones, 3x prayer XP per bone
   - Enables passive prayer training during Slayer

3. **Demonic Offering** (84 Magic, Arceuus)
   - Cast on ashes, 3x prayer XP per ash
   - Alternative to Sinister for ash-heavy Slayer tasks

<Callout type="tip">
Once you have 95 RC, you should have wrath rune access for life. Craft them when you have essence available, not as a grinding method.
</Callout>

## Recommended Progression

### Fresh Account → 27 RC

Use quest XP and low-level runecraft training to reach 27. This is purely to unlock GOTR.

**Key quests for RC XP:**
- Rune Mysteries (Aubury's) — 50 XP
- Client of Kourend (Zeah) — 500 XP
- Enter the Abyss (Runecrafting Guild) — 1,000 XP

After these quests, you're close to 27. Finish with a few air/mind rune runs if needed.

### 27 → 77 RC: GOTR Phase

Spend 100-200 hours in GOTR. Your goals:

1. **First:** Get Colossal Pouch (250 Abyssal Pearls)
2. **Second:** Get Raiments of the Eye full set (2,500 pearls total)
3. **Third:** Decide if you want to continue for Ring of the Elements or move on

At 77, you unlock Blood runes and can transition.

### 77 → 99: Blood/Soul Runes + Optional Boosts

Train exclusively via Blood runes (77-90) and Soul runes (90-99), or:

- **Use Lava runes 50-77** if you have a large essence stockpile and want faster XP
- **Mix in ZMI** if you need mixed rune types for various tasks
- **Get Wrath runes 95+** when you hit 95 and have Dragon Slayer II done

The passive nature of Blood runes makes this phase completely painless — you can do it while doing other content.
