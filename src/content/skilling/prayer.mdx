Prayer is one of the most impactful skills for ironmen. Protection prayers at 43 unlock safe-spottable content, and Piety at 70 is a massive DPS increase. The challenge isn't training - it's getting the bones.

## Key Milestones

| Level | Unlock | Why It Matters |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| 43 | Protection prayers | Safe-spot bosses, survive dangerous content |
| 44 | Eagle Eye | Ranged DPS boost |
| 45 | Mystic Might | Magic DPS boost |
| 70 | Piety | Massive melee DPS boost, unlocks serious bossing |
| 74 | Rigour (scroll) | BIS ranged prayer |
| 77 | Augury (scroll) | BIS magic prayer |

For most ironmen, **43 is the first goal** and **70 is the second**. Don't rush 99 - you'll get there naturally through Slayer and bossing.

## Early Game: Quest to 43

Questing is by far the most efficient way to get early prayer levels. These quests have low requirements and stack nicely:

| Quest | Prayer XP | Requirements |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| The Restless Ghost | 1,125 | None |
| Priest in Peril | 1,406 | None |
| Recruitment Drive | 1,000 | Black Knights' Fortress, Druidic Ritual |
| Holy Grail | 11,000 | Merlin's Crystal, 20 Attack |
| The Great Brain Robbery | 6,000 | Various (mid-game) |
| His Faithful Servants | 20,000 | 30 Construction, Children of the Sun |

<Callout type="tip">
The Restless Ghost and Priest in Peril alone get you to level 9. Add Holy Grail and you're at 30+. His Faithful Servants (Varlamore) jumps you significantly higher.
</Callout>

If you complete most prayer-granting quests, you can reach the low 40s without training at all.

## Training Methods Compared

| Method | XP Multiplier | Risk | Requirements | Best For |
|--------|--------------|------|--------------|----------|
| Blessed bone shards | 5-6x | None | 30 Prayer, Varlamore access | Everyone - safe and efficient |
| Chaos Altar | 7x average | High (Wilderness) | None | Fast XP if you don't mind dying |
| Gilded Altar | 3.5x | None | 75 Construction | Legacy method - rarely worth it now |
| Ectofuntus | 4x | None | Priest in Peril | Legacy method - rarely worth it now |
| Offering spells | 3x | None | 70 Magic, wrath runes | Passive during Slayer |

<Callout type="tip">
**Realistically, you're choosing between blessed bone shards (safe, good XP) or Chaos Altar (risky, best XP).** Gilded Altar and Ectofuntus exist but are outclassed - only consider them if you're a HCIM without Varlamore access or need ecto-tokens specifically.
</Callout>

## Blessed Bone Shards (Recommended)

The Varlamore method has changed ironman prayer training. It's safe, efficient, and you can collect shards passively.

### How It Works

1. **Get blessed bone shards** (see sources below)
2. **Get jugs of wine** (buy from wine sellers or make with 35 Cooking)
3. **Bless the wine** at the exposed altar south of the Teomat (30 Prayer)
4. **Sacrifice shards** at the libation bowl north of the altar

### XP Rates

- **5 XP per shard** with blessed wine
- **6 XP per shard** with blessed sunfire wine (add sunfire splinters before blessing)
- One jug of blessed wine covers 400 shards
- Sacrifice up to 100 shards per action

### Where to Get Shards

| Source | Requirements | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Hunter rumours | 46 Hunter | Passive while training Hunter |
| Calcified deposits | 41 Mining | AFK in Cam Torum, crush on anvil for 5-15 shards each |
| Breaking down bones | Chisel | Bless bones first, then chisel into shards |

The number of shards from bones scales with bone tier:

| Bone Type | Shards |
|-----------|--------|
| Regular bones | 1 |
| Big bones | 2 |
| Dragon bones | 9 |
| Superior dragon bones | 12 |

<Callout type="efficiency">
Collect shards passively through Hunter rumours and calcified deposits. When you're ready to train, bless and sacrifice in batches. No need to grind specifically.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hcim">
This is the recommended method for Hardcore Ironmen. Zero risk, decent XP rates, and you can collect supplies without dangerous content.
</Callout>

### Sunfire Splinters

Adding sunfire splinters to wine before blessing gives 6 XP per shard instead of 5 - a 20% boost.

**Where to get splinters:**
- Colosseum (Wave 1 farming is fastest)
- Various Varlamore content
- Sunlight antelope hunting

Worth doing if you have splinters, but don't grind specifically for them unless you're pushing high prayer levels.

## Chaos Altar (Fastest)

The Chaos Altar in level 38 Wilderness offers the best XP rates but comes with PKer risk.

### XP Mechanics

- **3.5x base XP** per bone
- **50% chance** the bone isn't consumed
- **Effective multiplier: ~7x** on average

This makes it roughly 40% faster than the gilded altar, but you will die occasionally.

### Strategy

- Use a 3-4 item setup (bone carrier + 3 protected items)
- Bank at Ferox Enclave (Ring of dueling)
- Accept that deaths happen - budget for losing some bones
- Go during off-peak hours for fewer PKers
- Bring cheap bones first to learn the route

<Callout type="warning">
Don't bring your entire bone stack. Bring what you're willing to lose per trip. Deaths are part of the method.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hcim">
Avoid this method entirely on a Hardcore. The XP boost isn't worth the status.
</Callout>

## Gilded Altar (Legacy Method)

Once you have 75 Construction, you can build a gilded altar in your POH chapel. However, blessed bone shards are better in almost every way.

### Setup

- Gilded altar (75 Construction)
- Two incense burners (61 Construction each)
- Both burners lit = 3.5x XP

### Why You Probably Shouldn't Use This

- 3.5x multiplier vs 5-6x for shards - significantly worse
- Requires 75 Construction investment
- Only real advantage is using bones directly without processing

The gilded altar made sense before Varlamore. Now it's a legacy method that's hard to justify unless you're a HCIM who somehow can't access Varlamore content.

## Ectofuntus (Legacy Method)

The Ectofuntus in Port Phasmatys offers 4x XP per bone with zero risk. Like the gilded altar, it's been outclassed by blessed bone shards.

### Process

1. Grind bones in the bone grinder (top floor)
2. Collect ecto-tokens from slime (basement)
3. Worship at the Ectofuntus with bonemeal + slime

### Why You Probably Shouldn't Use This

- 4x multiplier vs 5-6x for shards
- Tedious multi-step process
- Only advantage: earns ecto-tokens for Morytania diary

The only reason to use Ectofuntus is if you specifically need ecto-tokens. Otherwise, use shards.

## Offering Spells (Passive)

At 70 Magic with wrath runes, you can cast offering spells during Slayer tasks.

- **Sinister Offering** - bones (3x XP)
- **Demonic Offering** - ashes (3x XP)

### Requirements

- 70 Magic (Sinister) or 84 Magic (Demonic)
- Wrath runes (95 Runecrafting or Wrath altar access)
- Arceuus spellbook

This isn't a primary training method - it's passive XP while doing Slayer. Cast when you have a full inventory of bones/ashes.

## Where to Get Bones

Don't grind dragons specifically for prayer unless you urgently need levels. Most bones come passively.

### Passive Sources (Recommended)

| Source | Bones | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Slayer | Various | Dragon tasks, boss tasks - let them accumulate |
| Vorkath | Superior dragon | 2 noted per kill |
| Zulrah | Dragon | Noted drops |
| Dagannoth Kings | Dagannoth | 77 XP per bone |
| Alchemical Hydra | Hydra | 110 XP per bone |
| Moons of Peril | Various | Generous bone drops |

### Active Grinding (If Needed)

| Location | Bones | Rate | Notes |
|----------|-------|------|-------|
| Wilderness green dragons | Dragon | 300-330/hr | Risky, but fast with diary |
| Myths' Guild dragons | Dragon | ~200/hr | Safe, requires DS2 |

<Callout type="tip">
The Myths' Guild basement has both green and blue dragons. Kill blues while waiting for green respawns. Safest dragon bone farming for HCIM.
</Callout>

## Ensouled Heads

Ensouled heads from Slayer can be reanimated for prayer XP using the Arceuus spellbook.

| Head Tier | Prayer XP | Magic Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Basic (goblins, etc.) | 130-520 | 16 |
| Dragon | 1,560 | 93 |

**Honest assessment:** The XP rates are slow and it's not worth going out of your way to farm heads. They're a minor bonus from Slayer, nothing more.

<Callout type="tip">
At higher levels, only bother keeping high-tier ensouled heads (dragon, demon, etc.). The low-tier ones aren't worth the bank space or time to reanimate.
</Callout>

## Recommended Progression

### Fresh Account → 43 Prayer

1. Complete The Restless Ghost and Priest in Peril
2. Do Holy Grail when you have 20 Attack
3. Complete His Faithful Servants (Varlamore) when accessible
4. Use any method to finish off remaining XP to 43

### 43 → 70 Prayer

1. Collect bones and shards passively through Slayer and Hunter
2. When ready, use blessed bone shards (safe) or Chaos Altar (fast)
3. Don't rush - you'll get there naturally

### 70+ Prayer

At this point you have Piety and can do most content. Continue collecting bones/shards through PvM and train when you have large stacks.
