## Block Lists

You get up to **7 block slots total** (1 per 50 quest points, max at 350 QP). You can now have **different block lists for each slayer master** — set up each one independently via the "View list" interface.

All metal dragons (bronze through rune) are now **one combined task with 14 weight** — the highest in the game. Block this on every master.

**Block list philosophy:**
1. **Block HIGH weight tasks you don't want** — costs points but saves many future skips
2. **Skip LOW weight tasks** — cheaper than blocking, won't appear often
3. **Never block tasks you might want for bosses** — skip those instead

### Duradel Block List — Maximum XP

**Block (7 slots):**
1. **Metal dragons** (14 weight) — ALWAYS block, highest weight in game
2. **Hellhounds** (9 weight) — unless doing Cerberus pet
3. **Greater demons** (9 weight) — unless doing Skotizo/K'ril
4. **Black demons** (9 weight) — unless farming zenytes
5. **Suqahs** (8 weight) — bad XP, remote location
6. **Drakes** (8 weight) — slow, bad XP
7. **Wyrms** (8 weight) — slow, underwhelming drops

**Skip (low weight, not worth blocking):**
- Fire giants (7) — bad XP
- Spiritual creatures (6) — skip unless farming dragon boots
- Trolls (6) — tedious
- Blue dragons (4) — skip (or do Vorkath)
- Cave horrors (4), Elves (4), Kurasks (3), Waterfiends (2)

**Extend:**
- Dust devils (barrage in Catacombs)
- Nechryael (barrage in Catacombs)
- Bloodvelds (barrage, optional)

**Don't unlock:**
- TzHaar (use Steve if you want these)
- Red dragons, Fossil island wyverns

<Callout type="efficiency">
**XP focus = block everything 8+ weight that's slow.** Dust devils and nechryael extended are 200-300k magic XP/hour. These are your best tasks — never skip them.
</Callout>

### Duradel Block List — Maximum Money

**Block (7 slots):**
1. **Metal dragons** (14 weight) — unless farming rune dragons
2. **Abyssal demons** (9 weight) — good XP but not great GP
3. **Hellhounds** (9 weight) — unless doing Cerberus
4. **Nechryael** (9 weight) — XP focus, not money
5. **Fire giants** (7 weight) — bad GP
6. **Suqahs** (8 weight) — bad GP
7. **Wyrms** (8 weight) — mediocre GP

**Extend:**
- Cave kraken (AFK money)
- Dust devils (good GP + XP)
- Gargoyles (AFK money)
- Greater demons (if doing tormented demons)
- Black demons (if doing demonic gorillas for zenytes)
- Basilisks (if doing basilisk knights for jaw)
- Vampires (if doing vyrewatch for blood shards)

### Duradel Block List — Pet Hunting

**Block (7 slots):**
1. **Metal dragons** (14 weight)
2. **Greater demons** (9 weight) — unless hunting K'ril
3. **Fire giants** (7 weight)
4. **Nechryael** (9 weight)
5. **Drakes** (8 weight)
6. **Wyrms** (8 weight)
7. **Aberrant spectres** (6 weight)

**Extend based on pets you want:**
- Abyssal demons (Sire pet), Cave kraken (Kraken pet), Greater demons (K'ril pet), Black dragons (KBD pet), Gargoyles (Grotesque Guardians pet), Arachites (Araxyte pet, 92+ slayer)

<Callout type="tip">
Block high-weight tasks that don't have boss variants. Extend tasks with the pets you're actively hunting. Use task storage (500 points) to save boss tasks for when you want to grind them.
</Callout>

### Turael Block List — Point Boosting

**9 Turael tasks → 10th with Duradel = ~100-150 points/hour.**

**Block (7 slots):**
1. **Lizards** — very slow, need ice coolers or sulphur lizards
2. **Bats** — spread apart, slow
3. **Dogs** — few spawns, high HP
4. **Dwarves** — slow at White Wolf Mountain
5. **Skeletons** — high HP, tedious
6. **Wolves** — high HP
7. **Kalphites** — critical for task cancelling (see below)

**Why block kalphites on Turael:** If you get kalphites from Duradel and ask Turael to cancel it, he'll say "that's my task too." But if you **block kalphites on Turael**, he can cancel it for you. This applies to any task both masters share.

<Callout type="efficiency">
Point boosting gets you ~100-150 points per hour early game. Stop once you have superiors, slayer helm, and 3-4 blocks (~1,200 points spent). After that, normal tasks are more efficient.
</Callout>

### Konar Block List — Hydra Farming

Only use Konar if you're 95+ slayer and specifically want hydra tasks.

**Block (7 slots):**
1. **Metal dragons** (14 weight)
2. **Drakes** (8), **Wyrms** (8)
3. **Abyssal demons** (9), **Black demons** (9)
4. **Bloodvelds** (9), **Cave kraken** (9)

Skip everything except hydras.

## Best Slayer Master

### Duradel (100 Combat, 50 Slayer) — Your Default

**Use for:** General slayer training, XP, most efficient progression. Highest weight on good tasks (bursting, cannoning). Better XP rates than every other master.

### Konar quo Maten (75 Combat) — Milestones Only

Konar forces you to kill monsters in **specific locations**, which hurts XP significantly.

**Only use for:**
- Every 10th/50th/100th task (milestone bonus points)
- Hydra farming at 95+ slayer

### Nieve/Steve (85 Combat) — TzHaar Tasks Only

Highest TzHaar weight of any master. Use if grinding Jad or Zuk on-task. Duradel is better for everything else.

### Turael (3 Combat) — Point Boosting & Task Cancelling

Fastest tasks (birds, cows, rats). Use for 9 Turael → 10th Duradel point boosting early game. Also use to cancel terrible tasks from Duradel — but this resets your streak to 0, so only do it for truly awful tasks. Skipping with points (30 points) is usually better.

### Krystilia (Wilderness) — Optional

Separate task streak. High points, Larran's keys (prayer XP, seeds, runes), good money from revenants. But PK risk makes this unsuitable for HCIM. Situational.

### Master Comparison

| Master | Level Req | XP Rate (avg) | Points/Task | Best For |
|--------|-----------|---|---|---|
| Turael | 3 | 20-30k | 4 | Point boosting |
| Nieve/Steve | 85 Combat | 40-60k | 12 | TzHaar tasks |
| Konar | 75 Combat | 30-50k | 18 | Milestones, hydra farming |
| **Duradel** | 100 Combat, 50 Slayer | **50-100k+** | 12 | **XP grinding — best overall** |
| Krystilia | 3 (Wilderness) | 30-50k | 20+ | Wilderness profit |

## Black Mask & Slayer Helm

### Black Mask — Get This at 58 Slayer

At 58 slayer, cave horrors drop the [black mask](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Black_mask). This is your single biggest early priority.

- **16.67% damage AND accuracy boost** on task
- Works immediately — no slayer points needed
- 1/512 drop rate — expect 1-2 hours at cave horrors in Mos Le'Harmless (Cabin Fever quest required)
- Bring a light source and antipoison

<Callout type="efficiency">
The black mask boost is so significant that rushing 58 slayer should be a priority. Every task after getting it becomes noticeably faster.
</Callout>

### Slayer Helm — Upgrade from Black Mask

The slayer helm combines the black mask with nosepeg, earmuffs, facemask, and spiny helmet. Same 16.67% melee boost, but works against all slayer monsters that require headgear.

**Imbuing** adds **15% ranged AND magic boost** — essential for bursting and ranged tasks.

| Method | Cost | Time | Notes |
|--------|------|------|-------|
| **[Emir's Arena](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Emir%27s_Arena)** | 200 points | 30-40 mins | Fastest. Points for losing too. |
| Soul Wars | 500 zeal tokens | 2-4 hours | Team-based minigame |
| Nightmare Zone | 1.25M points (625k with Hard CA) | 4-8 hours | AFK but slow |

**Recommendation:** Emir's Arena. Even if you lose every match, ~18 matches takes 30-40 minutes. No supplies needed.

<Callout type="tip">
The [scroll of imbuing](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Scroll_of_imbuing) is reusable if you uncharge the item. You can swap it between slayer helm and other items (berserker ring, etc.) as needed.
</Callout>

**Imbue priority:** Slayer helm first → berserker ring → archers ring.

### Bigger and Badder (Superiors)

**Get this first.** Superior slayer monsters have a 1/200 chance to spawn when you kill their regular version.

| Drop | Rarity | Why You Want It |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| **[Imbued heart](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Imbued_heart)** | 1/32 from superiors | +10% magic level boost, recharges — BIS for magic |
| Eternal gem | 1/25 from superiors | Infinite slayer gem |
| Dust battlestaff | 1/128 from superiors | Unlimited air + earth runes |
| Mist battlestaff | 1/128 from superiors | Unlimited air + water runes |

The **imbued heart** is the golden drop. Getting superiors unlocked early maximises your chances across the entire slayer grind.

<Callout type="efficiency">
Superiors give 3x the XP of regular monsters too. Even without the drops, you're getting bonus XP every ~200 kills.
</Callout>

## Best Bursting Tasks

Dust devils and nechryaels are the premier bursting tasks. This is the fastest way to train magic XP through slayer.

**Why burst:**
- 200-300k+ magic XP/hr
- Great drops (herbs, seeds, runes)
- Passive prayer XP with bonecrusher

**Setup:**
- Proselyte armour (prayer bonus) + magic attack items
- Defensive casting (for defence XP)
- Bonecrusher + dragonbone necklace (prayer sustain)
- Rune pouch with blood/death/water runes

**Method:** Stack monsters in a corner (run through them) → cast Ice Burst/Barrage → collect loot → repeat.

| Task | Method | Slayer XP/hr | Magic XP/hr | Level Req |
|------|--------|---|---|---|
| Dust Devils | Ice Burst | 150-180k | 200-250k | 65 Slayer, 74 Magic |
| Dust Devils | Ice Barrage | 160-200k | 250-300k | 65 Slayer, 94 Magic |
| Nechryaels | Ice Burst | 180-220k | 250-290k | 80 Slayer, 74 Magic |
| Nechryaels | Ice Barrage | 200-260k | 280-330k | 80 Slayer, 94 Magic |

<Callout type="efficiency">
Barrage at 94+ magic is a massive jump. The upgrade from burst to barrage (250k → 330k magic XP/hr) is worth the levels. Never skip burst tasks — extend them with a bracelet of slaughter.
</Callout>

## Best Cannon Tasks

Use cannon when you have cannonballs to spare. Best for mid-level tasks where you can't burst.

| Task | Location | Slayer XP/hr | Notes |
|------|----------|---|---|
| Dagannoths | Lighthouse | 80-120k | Fast task, good for DK prep |
| Kalphites | Kalphite Lair | 60-90k | If not blocked |
| Bloodvelds | Catacombs | 70-100k | Good XP with cannon |
| Fire Giants | Waterfall | 40-60k | If not skipping |

<Callout type="casual">
Cannon speeds up tasks significantly but burns through cannonballs. Save them for tasks you can't burst and want done fast. Get the Double Ammo Mould from [Giants' Foundry](/guides/giants-foundry/) first.
</Callout>

### Expeditious & Slaughter Bracelets

- **[Expeditious bracelet](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Expeditious_bracelet)** — 25% chance to not count a kill (speeds up bad tasks)
- **[Bracelet of slaughter](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bracelet_of_slaughter)** — 25% chance to not count a kill but you still get XP (extends good tasks)

Use expeditious on tasks you want done fast. Use slaughter on burst tasks to extend them.

<Callout type="tip">
These bracelets are a great use for opals and jades — gems that otherwise just pile up in your bank. Craft them at 23 and 29 Crafting respectively.
</Callout>

## Optimisation Tools

### Skilling Discord Slayer Spreadsheet

The most powerful tool for customising your block list is the **[Skilling Discord Slayer Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14-rahdjUzFiWe5STNV87GBekIc45FtXwYmH58JPUsVc/)**.

- Calculates optimal blocks based on YOUR goals (XP, money, pets)
- Shows **points per task** — aim for ~0 for perfect efficiency
- Customisable for your unlocks, gear, and playstyle

**How to use:**
1. **File → Make a Copy**
2. Toggle your settings (gear, alts, unlocks)
3. Mark tasks as "Do", "Block", "Skip", or "Don't unlock"
4. Watch the "Points per task" stat — get it close to 0

<Callout type="tip">
The spreadsheet defaults to sweaty setups (alt accounts, scythe, etc.). Turn these off if you don't have them. It's fully customisable to your actual gear.
</Callout>

### In-Game Block Interface

The new slayer interface lets you view and block tasks without getting them first:
1. Talk to any slayer master → "View list"
2. See exact task weights and quantities
3. Block/unblock directly — no need to get the task first

## Wilderness Slayer

Krystilia in Edgeville assigns wilderness-only tasks on a **separate streak** from your main assignments.

**Worth doing for:**
- [Larran's keys](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Larran%27s_key) — prayer XP (ensouled heads), runes, seeds
- Extra points without touching your main streak
- Some fast tasks (green dragons, ice giants)

**Skip entirely on Hardcore.** PK risk isn't worth it.

## Common Mistakes

- **Not blocking metal dragons** — 14 weight means 1 in 7 tasks. Block on every master.
- **Not rushing black mask** — the 16.67% boost is massive. Get 58 slayer and camp cave horrors.
- **Rushing slayer helm before superiors** — superiors cost 50 points and drop imbued heart. Get them first.
- **Not imbuing slayer helm via Emir's Arena** — it takes 30 mins, not hours.
- **Blocking black demons when you need zenytes** — skip instead to keep Demonic Gorillas open.
- **Blocking hellhounds when you want Cerberus** — skip instead until you're done with the boss.
- **Skipping burst tasks** — dust devils and nechryaels are 200-300k magic XP/hour. Never skip these.
- **Using Konar as main master** — forced locations kill XP rates. Milestones only.
