Crafting on an ironman is all about resource management. Here's how to train it efficiently without burning your entire bank.

**At a glance:**
- **Best early method:** Quests (1-40 instant) → Superglass Make with giant seaweed
- **Fast XP:** Dhide bodies (green at 63, black at 84 for 425k XP/hr)
- **Efficient method:** Dorgesh-Kaan light orb lamps (87-99, cheap + bonus Firemaking XP)
- **Daily routine:** Buy battlestaves from Zaff (unlock with Varrock diaries)

---

## Why Crafting Matters

Crafting unlocks essential ironman progression:

**Combat gear:**
- Dragonhide armour (40-70 Ranged gear)
- Glory amulets (80 Crafting — teleports + combat bonuses)
- Zenyte jewellery (89-98 — BiS jewellery)

**Diary requirements:**
- 70 Crafting for Lumbridge & Draynor Hard (power amulet from scratch)
- 85+ for various Elite diaries

**Money making:**
- Superglass Make: 700k+ GP/hr profit
- Battlestaves: 300k+ daily profit (Elite Varrock diary)

---

## When Should You Train Crafting?

Crafting has specific breakpoints that matter for progression:

**Rush to 80 first:**
- Glory amulets (80 Crafting + 68 Magic) save hundreds of hours
- Prioritise this over many other skills early-game

**Train passively to 87:**
- Daily battlestaves from Zaff (unlock Varrock diaries)
- Superglass Make during downtime
- No need to grind hard until 87

**Power grind 87-99:**
- Dorgesh-Kaan lamps are worth focused training
- Do this when you need a break from combat

## Early Game (1-40)

Skip the grind entirely with quests.

### Quest XP (1-40 Instant)

| Quest | Crafting XP | Requirements |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| [Elemental Workshop I](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Elemental_Workshop_I) | 5,000 | None |
| [Elemental Workshop II](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Elemental_Workshop_II) | 7,500 | Elemental Workshop I |
| [Murder Mystery](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Murder_Mystery) | 1,406 | None |
| [The Giant Dwarf](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Giant_Dwarf) | 2,500 | None |

**Total:** 16,406 XP (level 1 → 35 instantly)

**XP Lamps (use on Crafting):**
- [Temple of the Eye](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Temple_of_the_Eye): 9,210 XP lamp → Pushes you past 40
- [Lunar Diplomacy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Lunar_Diplomacy): 5,000 XP lamp (also unlocks Superglass Make)

<Callout type="efficiency">
Do Elemental Workshop I & II first thing. 12,500 free XP in 20 minutes. Then add Temple of the Eye lamp and you're at 40 Crafting before training a single level manually.
</Callout>

### Alternative: Leather (1-20)

If you're training before quests:

1. Kill cows in Lumbridge for cowhides
2. Tan at Al Kharid (5 gp each)
3. Craft leather gloves (1+) → leather bodies (14+)
4. 217 leather needed to reach 20 (~70k cost)

Not recommended — quests are faster.

### Gold Jewellery (20-40)

If you still need levels after quests:

- **20-40:** Sapphire amulets (55 XP) → Emerald amulets (70 XP)
- **XP rate:** 50-70k/hr at Edgeville furnace
- **Cost:** Moderate (buy gold ore from Blast Furnace or mine it)

## Mid Game (40-77)

This is where ironmen diverge from mains. Superglass Make is your best friend.

### Superglass Make — The Ironman Staple

The [Superglass Make](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Superglass_Make) spell transforms buckets of sand + seaweed into molten glass instantly.

**Why it's essential:**
- **XP rates:** 108-153k Crafting XP/hr (600-850 casts/hr)
- **Profit:** 700k+ GP/hr
- **Materials:** Self-sustainable via giant seaweed farming
- **Bonus:** Builds glass stockpile for Construction and more Crafting later

**Requirements:**
- [Lunar Diplomacy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Lunar_Diplomacy) quest
- 77 Magic (or boost from 72 with Wizard's mind bomb)
- Giant seaweed farming (23 Farming recommended)

**How it works:**
1. Use 3 giant seaweed + 18 buckets of sand per cast
2. Cast Superglass Make → Creates ~27-30 molten glass (1.6x multiplier)
3. Each cast gives ~180 Crafting XP + 78 Magic XP
4. Bank and repeat

<Callout type="tip">
One giant seaweed = 6 soda ash when cooked. Using Superglass Make with giant seaweed is 6x more efficient than manually using soda ash.
</Callout>

### Giant Seaweed Farming (Fossil Island)

**Setup:**
1. Complete [Bone Voyage](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bone_Voyage) quest (unlock Fossil Island)
2. Get seaweed spores (buy from Mernia near seaweed patches, or gather underwater)
3. Plant in underwater seaweed patches (2 patches available)
4. Use ultracompost for best yield
5. Wait 40 minutes → Harvest 15-34 giant seaweed per patch

**Banking giant seaweed:**
- Use [Fish Barrel](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fish_barrel) (40 Fishing) to store 28 seaweed per slot
- Or bank normally at Fossil Island

**Sustainability:**
- 2 seaweed runs per day = ~60-120 giant seaweed
- That's 20-40 Superglass Make casts = 3,600-7,200 Crafting XP per run
- Fully sustainable with regular farming

### Buckets of Sand — Three Methods

**Option 1: Sandstone Grinding (Best)**

- **Location:** Bandit Camp Quarry (Desert)
- **Requirements:** 35 Mining
- **Method:**
  1. Mine sandstone at quarry
  2. Deposit in Sandstorm grinder (west of quarry)
  3. Collect buckets of sand (50 coins per bucket from Drew)
- **Rates:** 2,000-3,500 buckets/hr (up to 6,000/hr with tick manipulation)

**Option 2: Charter Ships (Easy)**

- Buy from Trader Crewmembers at charter ship docks
- 10 buckets of sand per world stock
- World hop to buy in bulk
- Port Khazard closest to bank

**Option 3: Yanille (Low Effort)**

- Buy from Trader Crewmember in Yanille (house portal)
- 40 buckets of sand per stock
- Slower than sandstone but requires no mining

<Callout type="efficiency">
Mine sandstone during Farming runs. One hour of sandstone grinding = enough buckets for several seaweed cycles. You'll build a huge stockpile passively.
</Callout>

### Glassbowing (After Superglass Make)

Once you have molten glass, blow it into items:

| Item | Level | XP | Best For |
|------|-------|-----|----------|
| Beer glass | 1 | 17.5 | Very early |
| Lantern lens | 49 | 55 | Mid-game |
| Empty light orb | 87 | 70 | Dorgesh-Kaan lamps |
| Dorgeshuun light sphere | 87 | 104 | Dorgesh-Kaan lamps |

**XP rates:** Up to 120k/hr glassbowing

### Guardians of the Rift (Passive Alternative)

An AFK-friendly minigame that provides passive Crafting XP alongside Runecrafting training.

**Requirements:** None (accessible from level 1)

**Why consider it:**
- 5,000-6,000 Crafting XP/hr (passive while training Runecrafting)
- No material costs
- Useful rewards for ironmen (Raiments of the Eye outfit, abyssal needle, pearls)
- Good for early-game accounts without Lunar Diplomacy

**How it works:**
- Create guardian essence and power up the Great Guardian
- XP rewards scale with your Crafting level
- Can be combined with Runecrafting training

**Verdict:** Not the fastest Crafting method, but excellent passive XP if you're training Runecrafting anyway. Don't grind this solely for Crafting XP.

<Callout type="casual">
If you're planning to train Runecrafting early (for blood/soul runes later), Guardians of the Rift gives you free Crafting XP on the side. Efficient accounts do this before grinding Crafting hard.
</Callout>

## Dragonhide Bodies (63-84)

Fast XP but requires gathering hides. Here's the progression:

### Green D'hide Bodies (63-71)

- **XP:** 186 per body
- **Rate:** ~300k XP/hr
- **Materials:** 3 green dragon leather + needle & thread
- **To wear:** 40 Ranged, 40 Defence, Dragon Slayer I

**Gathering green dhides:**
- Kill green dragons in Wilderness (various locations)
- 100% drop rate on green dragonhide + dragon bones
- Low HP, easy to kill

### Blue D'hide Bodies (71-77)

- **XP:** 210 per body
- **Rate:** ~350k XP/hr
- **Materials:** 3 blue dragon leather + needle & thread
- **To wear:** 50 Ranged, 40 Defence

**Gathering blue dhides:**
- **Taverley Dungeon** (most popular — safespot available)
- **Catacombs of Kourend** (good for Slayer tasks)
- **Baby blue dragons** (lower combat level, same drops)

<Callout type="tip">
Safespot blue dragons in Taverley Dungeon for minimal supply usage. Bring antifire shield or potion and range them. 100% blue dragonhide drop rate.
</Callout>

### Red D'hide Bodies (77-84)

- **XP:** 234 per body
- **Rate:** ~400k XP/hr
- **Materials:** 3 red dragon leather + needle & thread
- **To wear:** 60 Ranged, 40 Defence
- **Cost:** 5-7 GP/XP (moderate loss)

### Black D'hide Bodies (84-99)

- **XP:** 258 per body
- **Rate:** 425-500k XP/hr
- **Materials:** 3 black dragon leather + needle & thread
- **To wear:** 70 Ranged, 40 Defence
- **Cost:** 200M+ to 99 (huge loss)

**Verdict:** Black dhide bodies are the fastest XP in the game, but extremely expensive. Only do this if you're GP-rich or rushing 99.

## Dorgesh-Kaan Light Orb Lamps (87-99)

**The most efficient method for 87-99.**

### What Are They?

Fix broken light orbs throughout [Dorgesh-Kaan](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dorgesh-Kaan) (goblin city) for massive XP.

**Requirements:**
- 87 Crafting
- 44 Thieving
- 52 Firemaking
- [The Lost Tribe](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Lost_Tribe) quest

**Method:**
1. Blow molten glass into empty light orbs (70 Crafting XP each)
2. Add cave goblin wire (104 Crafting XP per orb)
3. Fix broken lamps throughout Dorgesh-Kaan (500 Crafting + 500 Firemaking XP per lamp)
4. **Bonus:** +2,500 Crafting + 2,500 Firemaking XP per 50 lamps fixed

**XP rates:**
- Beginners: 90k Crafting XP/hr
- Experienced: 130k Crafting XP/hr
- Also gives: 90k Firemaking XP/hr + 4k Thieving XP/hr

**Why it's the best:**
- Cheap (only molten glass from Superglass Make)
- Bonus Firemaking and Thieving XP
- No major GP loss
- Sustainable

<Callout type="efficiency">
Dorgesh-Kaan lamps are the ironman meta for 87-99. Costs almost nothing compared to dhide bodies, and you get free Firemaking levels too.
</Callout>

## Battlestaves (Daily Routine)

[Zaff](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zaff) in Varrock sells battlestaves daily. Buy them every day and craft for passive XP + profit.

**Daily stock (unlocked via Varrock Diary):**
- No diary: 5 battlestaves (7,000 gp each)
- Easy diary: 15 battlestaves
- Medium diary: 30 battlestaves
- Hard diary: 60 battlestaves
- **Elite diary: 120 battlestaves**

**Stock resets:** 00:00 UTC daily

**Crafting battlestaves:**
- 137.5 XP per staff
- Air battlestaves: 330k XP/hr
- Water battlestaves: 250k XP/hr

**Profit:**
- Elite diary = 120 battlestaves/day
- Sell back to Grand Exchange or high alch for 300k+ daily profit

<Callout type="casual">
Elite Varrock diary is a long-term goal but absolutely worth it. 120 battlestaves per day = 16,500 Crafting XP + 300k profit daily for 2 minutes of work.
</Callout>

## Zenyte Jewellery (89-98)

Endgame jewellery crafting for BiS gear.

### What is Zenyte?

[Zenyte](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zenyte) is obtained from [Demonic Gorillas](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Demonic_gorilla) (1/300 drop rate). You need 4 zenytes total for the full set.

**Crafting levels:**
- **Cut zenyte:** 89 Crafting (50 XP per cut)
- **Zenyte ring:** 89 Crafting (150 XP)
- **Zenyte necklace:** 92 Crafting (165 XP)
- **Zenyte amulet:** 98 Crafting (200 XP)
- **Zenyte bracelet:** 95 Crafting (180 XP)

**XP rates:** ~180k/hr at 1,200 crafts/hr

**Verdict:** Only craft the zenytes you need for gear. Don't use them for training — they're too rare and valuable.

## Glory Amulets (80 Crafting)

[Amulet of Glory](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Amulet_of_glory) is essential for ironmen — teleports to Edgeville, Karamja, Draynor, and Al Kharid.

**How to make:**
1. Mine dragonstones (rare from gem rocks, or buy from Tzhaar)
2. Cut dragonstone (55 Crafting)
3. String into amulet of glory (unstrung) at 80 Crafting
4. Enchant with Lvl-5 Enchant (68 Magic)

**Alternative sources:**
- Prifddinas gem rocks (high chance)
- Tzhaar gem shop (300k coins per dragonstone)
- Chambers of Xeric rewards

<Callout type="tip">
Your first glory is a huge milestone. Rush 80 Crafting and 68 Magic to make one. The teleports save hundreds of hours.
</Callout>

## Efficient vs Profitable Methods

### Fastest XP (Cost Not Considered)

1. **Black d'hide bodies (84+):** 425-500k XP/hr — Costs 200M+ to 99
2. **Red d'hide bodies (77+):** 400k XP/hr — 5-7 GP/XP
3. **Blue d'hide bodies (71+):** 350k XP/hr — Moderate cost
4. **Air battlestaves (54+):** 330k XP/hr — Moderate loss

### Most Efficient (XP + Cost Balance)

1. **Dorgesh-Kaan lamps (87-99):** 90-130k XP/hr — Cheap, bonus Firemaking XP
2. **Superglass Make (40-77):** 108-153k XP/hr — Profitable!
3. **Green d'hide bodies (63-71):** 300k XP/hr — Moderate cost, easy to gather

### Profitable Methods

1. **Superglass Make:** 700k+ GP/hr + 108-153k Crafting XP/hr
2. **Daily battlestaves (Elite Varrock):** 16,500 XP + 300k profit for 2 mins
3. **Gem cutting:** Varies by gem type (slow XP but cheap)

### XP vs Cost Comparison

Full breakdown from 1-99:

| Method | XP/hr | GP/XP | Total Cost to 99 | Time to 99 |
|--------|-------|-------|------------------|------------|
| Black dhide bodies (84-99) | 425k | -15 GP/XP | 200M+ loss | ~30 hours |
| Red dhide bodies (77-99) | 400k | -6 GP/XP | 80M+ loss | ~32 hours |
| Dorgesh-Kaan lamps (87-99) | 130k | ~0 GP/XP | Near zero | ~100 hours |

| Guardians of the Rift | 5-6k | 0 GP/XP | Free | ~2,200 hours (passive) |
| Daily battlestaves only | 16.5k/day | Profitable | 300k profit/day | ~800 days |

## FAQ

### What's the fastest way to 99 Crafting?

Black d'hide bodies (84-99) at 425-500k XP/hr. But it costs 200M+ GP. For ironmen, Dorgesh-Kaan lamps (87-99) are faster considering gathering time.

### Is Superglass Make worth it?

Absolutely. It's profitable, sustainable, and builds a glass stockpile you'll use for Construction and more Crafting later. Every ironman should use this method.

### How much does 99 Crafting cost?

Depends on method:
- **Superglass Make (40-99):** Profit!
- **Dorgesh-Kaan lamps (87-99):** Near zero cost
- **Mix of methods:** 20-50M GP
- **Black d'hide bodies only:** 200M+ GP loss

### When should I start farming giant seaweed?

As soon as you complete Bone Voyage (unlock Fossil Island). Giant seaweed is essential for Superglass Make and grows in 40 minutes. Start early and build a stockpile.

### Should I do Varrock diaries for battlestaves?

Yes. Even Easy diary (15 battlestaves/day) is worth it. Elite diary (120/day) is a long-term goal but gives 16.5k daily Crafting XP for 2 minutes of work.

### What's the cheapest method to 99?

Superglass Make from 40-99. It's actually profitable (700k+ GP/hr) while giving decent XP (108-153k/hr). Or use Dorgesh-Kaan lamps from 87-99 for near-zero cost.

### When should I make my first glory amulet?

As soon as you hit 80 Crafting and 68 Magic. Glory teleports save hundreds of hours. Get dragonstones from Tzhaar shop or gem rocks.
