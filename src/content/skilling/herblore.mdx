## Mastering Mixology (2024 Update) - THE GAME-CHANGER

The biggest herblore update for ironmen ever. This Varlamore minigame completely changes the meta.

### What Is It?

**Herblore minigame in Aldarin, Varlamore**
- **105-110k XP/hr** at efficient levels
- **3-4x more XP per herb** than traditional potion-making
- **No secondaries required** - the minigame provides everything
- Rewards are essential for all ironmen

**Requirements:**
- 60 Herblore (not boostable)
- Completion of [Children of the Sun](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Children_of_the_Sun) quest
- All recipes unlocked at 81 Herblore

**Location:**
- Aldarin island, Varlamore
- Fairy ring code: **CKQ**
- Or charter ship from Varlamore

### How It Works

You fill potion orders using three reagents: **Mox** (purple), **Aga** (red), and **Lye** (green).

**Process:**
1. Add clean herbs or unfinished potions to the refiner → Get paste (Mox/Aga/Lye)
2. Deposit up to 3,000 of each paste type in the hopper
3. Check potion orders (top-left corner) - shows which potions to make
4. Pull three levers to select paste combination (order doesn't matter)
5. Click beaker to create potion
6. Process potions at machines (agitator/crystallizer/concentrator)
7. Submit completed potions on conveyor belt → Big XP drop!

**Machine types:**
- **Agitator (V icon):** Click once, click again when sparkles appear
- **Crystallizer (beaker icon):** Click, wait for 5 pumps, click again (listen for sound)
- **Concentrator (cauldron icon):** Spam click - no timing needed

**The Digweed:**
- Purple plant spawns randomly in corners
- Pick it immediately (despawns after ~10 seconds)
- Add to any potion before/after processing
- **Doubles XP and points** for that potion

<Callout type="efficiency">
Use the **"Mastering Mixology" RuneLite plugin** (Plugin Hub). It highlights machines, tells you what to make, and shows Digweed spawns. Essential for efficiency.
</Callout>

### Which Herbs to Use (Strategic Guide)

**The golden rule:** Use herbs where you have **excess compared to secondaries**. Don't waste valuable herbs you need for important potions.

#### Mox (Purple Paste)

**Best herbs:**
- **Harralander** (recommended) - You'll get tons from Moons of Peril
- Guam, Marrentill, Tarromin - Any low-level herbs you have excess of

**Why:** Low-level herbs have limited uses and you'll accumulate far more than you need. Harralander is perfect because Moons of Peril gives you hundreds.

#### Aga (Red Paste)

**Best herbs:**
- **Irit** (recommended) - Excess from Moons of Peril, but eyes of newt are free so keep some for potions
- **Dwarf weed** (excellent choice) - Zammy wines are dreadful to get until later content
- Cadantine, Lantadyme - If you have excess compared to secondaries

**Why:** Dwarf weed is the standout here because Zammy wines require either Zamorak GWD or slow wine-making. Most ironmen have far more dwarf weed than wines. Irit is good if you have excess from Moons.

#### Lye (Green Paste)

**Best herbs:**
- **Avantoe** (top choice) - Only used for stamina potions, which are limited by amylase crystals, not avantoes
- **Kwuarm** - Good secondary choice if you have excess

**Avoid for Lye:**
- ❌ Ranarr - Need for prayer potions (essential!)
- ❌ Snapdragon - Need for super restores (expensive!)
- ❌ Toadflax - Need for Saradomin brews (raids essential!)
- ❌ Torstol - Need for super combats (very expensive!)

**Why:** Avantoe is the ironman meta for Lye. You'll always have more avantoes than amylase crystals, making them perfect for Mixology. Kwuarm is decent if you have extras beyond what you need for super strengths.

<Callout type="warning">
**Never use Ranarr, Snapdragon, or Toadflax for Mixology!** These are essential for prayer potions, super restores, and Sara brews respectively. You'll regret wasting them.
</Callout>

**Full refiner chart:** See [OSRS Wiki - Refiner](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Refiner) for complete herb-to-paste mappings.

**Strategic summary:**
1. **Mox:** Harralander (from Moons)
2. **Aga:** Dwarf weed (Zammy wines are painful)
3. **Lye:** Avantoe (limited by amylase, not herbs)

This keeps your valuable herbs (Ranarr, Snapdragon, Toadflax, Torstol) for actual potion-making where they're irreplaceable.

### When to Use Mixology

**Use Mixology when:**
- You have lots of herbs but no secondaries
- You want pure XP for level goals
- You need Mixology reward currency
- Training 60-81 for recipe unlocks

**Use traditional potion-making when:**
- You need specific potions for PvM (prayer pots, staminas, brews)
- You have both herbs and secondaries available
- You're making immediately useful potions

**Ironman strategy:**
- Get Mixology rewards first (goggles + amulet)
- Use Mixology for herbs where secondaries are hard to obtain
- Make traditional potions for useful ones (prayer, stamina, combat)

## Herb Gathering (Critical for Ironmen)

Herblore is only as fast as your herb supply. Here's how to get them:

### 1. Farming (Primary Method) - DO HERB RUNS EVERY 80 MINUTES

**This is your main herb source.** Consistent runs = consistent XP.

**Disease-Free Patches (prioritise these!):**
1. **Troll Stronghold** - [My Arm's Big Adventure](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/My_Arm%27s_Big_Adventure) quest
2. **Weiss** - [Making Friends with My Arm](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Making_Friends_with_My_Arm) quest
3. **Hosidius** - 75% Hosidius favour
4. **Varlamore** - NEW! 16,000 Colosseum glory (Champion rank)

**Other patches:**
- Falador Park
- Ardougne (north of zoo)
- Catherby (near bank)
- Port Phasmatys
- Farming Guild (85 Farming)
- Harmony Island (elite Morytania diary)
- Troll Stronghold protected

**Total: 10+ patches available**

**Efficient Herb Run Strategy:**

**Inventory:**
- Magic secateurs (equipped or inventory) - **+10% herb yield**
- Ultracompost (apply to every patch)
- Herb seeds (highest level you can afford)
- Teleports (Ectophial, Ardougne cloak, Explorer's ring, etc.)
- Seed dibber, spade
- Herb sack (if you have it from Tithe Farm)

**Ultracompost creation:**
- Buy pineapples from charter ships → Compost bin
- OR harvest watermelons → Compost bin
- Add 25 volcanic ash (from Fossil Island) to turn super → ultra
- Alternative: Use Compost potion (22 Herblore) on compost bin

**What herbs to plant:**

**Early game (low Farming):**
- Whatever seeds you get from contracts/master farmers
- Focus on **Ranarr** for prayer potions

**Mid game (65+ Farming):**
- **Ranarr** (prayer potions - priority #1!)
- **Toadflax** (Sara brews later, cheap)
- **Avantoe** (super energy → stamina potions)

**Late game (75+ Farming):**
- **Ranarr** (always useful)
- **Snapdragon** (super restores, expensive)
- **Torstol** (super combat, highest level)

**Time investment:**
- 5-10 minutes per run
- Every 80 minutes (herb growth time)
- ~6-8 runs per day if efficient

<Callout type="tip">
Set RuneLite timers for herb runs. Even 3 runs per day (morning/afternoon/evening) gives consistent herb income. This is how you sustain Herblore long-term.
</Callout>

### 2. Farming Contracts (Best Seed Source!)

**Available at Farming Guild (45+ Farming required)**

Currently the **best way to obtain herb and tree seeds** for ironmen.

**Contract tiers:**
- Easy (45 Farming)
- Medium (65 Farming)
- Hard (85 Farming)

**Strategy:**
- Complete contracts for seed packs containing herb seeds
- Pre-plant certain crops to complete multiple contracts quickly
- Don't harvest pre-grown crops - wait for contract assignment
- Replace contracts as you complete them for maximum efficiency

**This is how you get Ranarr, Snapdragon, and Torstol seeds consistently.**

### 3. Kingdom of Miscellania (Passive Income)

**Essential for ironmen** - set this up ASAP!

**Requirements:**
- Completion of [Throne of Miscellania](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Throne_of_Miscellania)
- [Royal Trouble](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Royal_Trouble) quest unlocks more workers

**Optimal setup:**
- Maximum GP in coffer (750k+ recommended)
- 10 workers on Maple Logs
- **5 workers on Herbs** (for endgame potion supply)
- Keep approval rating at 100% (check every few days)

**Maintaining approval:**
- Talk to Advisor Ghrim to check rating
- Help citizens with skilling tasks until they say "Thanks!"
- Rating falls 2.5% daily (1% with Royal Trouble)

**Yields:**
- 50+ herbs daily at high approval
- Passive, efficient long-term strategy
- Set it and forget it (check weekly)

### 4. Master Farmer Pickpocketing (Ranarr Seeds)

**Fastest method for Ranarr seeds** specifically.

**Requirements:**
- 38 Thieving minimum
- 85 Thieving for better seed rates (Snapdragon, Torstol)
- **94 Thieving + Hard Ardougne Diary** = stop failing
- **99 Thieving + Thieving cape** = never fail

**Strategy:**
- Focus on this specifically for Ranarr seeds
- Yields various herb, allotment, and flower seeds
- Can be tedious but very effective
- Master Farmers in Draynor Village, Hosidius, Farming Guild

### 5. Slayer Drops (Passive Collection)

**Best monsters for herb drops:**
- **Aberrant Spectres** - Good herb drops
- **Nechryaels** - Bulk herb/seed drops when bursting
- **Kurasks** - Consistent herb drops
- **Chaos Druids** (early game) - Level 13 combat, common herbs
  - Locations: Edgeville Dungeon, Taverley Dungeon
  - Drop expensive herbs: Lantadyme, Avantoe, Dwarf Weed, Ranarr
- **Spitting Wyverns** - 4-dose prayer potions, ranarr seeds/weeds

### 6. Herbiboar Hunting (Hybrid Method)

**Requirements:**
- 80 Hunter
- 31 Herblore minimum (higher = better herbs)
- Stamina potions + Herb sack recommended

**XP rates:**
- 115-130k Hunter XP/hr
- Passive Herblore resource gathering

**Herb quality:**
- Determined by your Herblore level
- Low-level herbs disappear from drop table at certain levels
- **Recommended: 80+ Herblore** for high-quality herbs only

**Is it worth it?**
- **YES** if you need herbs and can support it with staminas
- Excellent alternative to chinchompas
- Good for alching/making broad arrows while hunting
- Equip or carry Magic Secateurs for extra herb per harvest

### 7. Other Sources

- **Zulrah** - Snape grass, herbs, scales (all useful!)
- **Vorkath** - Blue dragon scales
- **Chambers of Xeric** - Herb drops from rooms

## Advanced Methods (Faster XP)

### 1-Tick Method (Stackable Ingredients)

Certain potions have **stackable secondaries**, letting you abuse game mechanics for insane XP rates.

**How it works:**
1. Put a large stack of secondary in inventory (e.g., 10,000 amylase crystals)
2. Take 27 potions needed (e.g., super energy for staminas)
3. Hold **SPACE BAR** on keyboard
4. Click secondary on each potion rapidly
5. You'll make 1 potion per tick = insane XP/hr

**Stackable ingredient potions:**
- **Stamina potions (77):** ~530k XP/hr
- **Anti-venom (87):** ~600k XP/hr
- **Extended super antifire (98):** ~800k XP/hr

**Downsides:**
- Extremely click-intensive
- Only works for specific potions
- Can be expensive

<Callout type="warning">
The 1-tick method is exhausting. Only use for final push to 99 or when you want Herblore done ASAP. Most ironmen mix methods instead.
</Callout>

### Cleaning Herbs (AFK Profit)

**Auto-cleaning (very AFK):**
- Put grimy herbs in inventory
- Click one herb
- Character auto-cleans slowly
- 60-70k XP/hr

**Manual cleaning:**
- Click each herb as fast as you can
- 80-88k XP/hr
- More profit than auto-clean

**"Grime" Spell (Arceuus Spellbook):**
- 70 Magic required
- [A Kingdom Divided](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/A_Kingdom_Divided) quest
- Full inventory of grimy herbs
- Cast spell → cleans all at once
- **Best profit per hour** for cleaning
- Very click-intensive

**Is cleaning worth it?**
- Good for ironmen with excess herbs and no secondaries
- Profitable (clean herbs > grimy herbs)
- Can do while AFKing other activities

## FAQ

### What's the fastest way to 99 Herblore?

Extended super antifire 1-ticking (98-99) at 800k XP/hr. But realistically: mix of super combats (90+) and Mixology. For ironmen, 80-120 hours total depending on herb supply.

### Is Mastering Mixology worth it?

**Absolutely YES** for ironmen. Get the rewards first (especially goggles + amulet), then decide how to train. 3-4x more XP per herb than traditional methods, and rewards save millions long-term.

### How many herb runs should I do per day?

Minimum 2-3 per day (morning/evening). Ideal is every 80 minutes during active play time. Consistency > quantity. Even casual runs add up to thousands of herbs over weeks.

### What herbs should I prioritise farming?

**Ranarr** (prayer potions - priority #1), **Toadflax** (Sara brews, cheap), **Snapdragon** (super restores, expensive), **Torstol** (super combat, highest level). Focus on what you need most.

### Should I clean herbs or leave them grimy?

Clean immediately to save bank space. Grimy herbs take twice as much space and cleaning is fast (auto-clean update). Exception: if you want AFK XP later, stockpile grimy.

### When should I start making stamina potions?

As soon as you hit 77 Herblore and have amylase crystals from Rooftop Agility. Don't wait - you'll use staminas forever. Make them as you get amylase.

### Are surge potions worth making?

**YES** - essential for PvM meta. 25% spec attack restore is huge for bossing. Demonic tallow is rare though, so stockpile it and make surge potions in batches.
