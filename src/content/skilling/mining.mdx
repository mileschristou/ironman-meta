**At a glance:**
- **Supplies needed:** Prospector kit, coal bag, gem bag, celestial ring, expert mining gloves
- **Best AFK:** Shooting Stars (gem bags for Crafting)
- **Best for Prayer:** Calcified Rocks (pre-70 Prayer)
- **Best resources:** Zalcano (bulk ores, bars, gems, crystal shards)
- **Endgame:** Amethyst 92-99 (blowpipe/tbow ammo)

## Priority Unlocks

Get these before focusing on XP:

| Item | Source | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| **[Celestial Ring](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Celestial_ring)** | 2,000 stardust (Shooting Stars) | +4 invisible Mining boost, works uncharged |
| **[Prospector Kit](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Prospector_kit)** | 180 nuggets (MLM) | 2.5% XP bonus, needed for Varrock Elite diary |
| **[Coal Bag](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Coal_bag)** | 100 nuggets (MLM) | Essential for Blast Furnace |
| **[Gem Bag](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Gem_bag)** | 100 nuggets (MLM) | Holds gems while mining |
| **[Expert Mining Gloves](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Expert_mining_gloves)** | Unidentified minerals (Mining Guild) | Prevents ore depletion, huge for amethyst |

<Callout type="tip">
Get the Celestial Ring first - it takes under 2 hours and the +4 boost helps everything else.
</Callout>

## Mining Guild

The [Mining Guild](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mining_Guild) (60 Mining, boostable) is where you'll spend a lot of time. It's worth understanding what it offers:

- **Invisible +7 Mining boost** - stacks with celestial ring (+4) and visible boosts. This means at 60 Mining you mine as if you're 71
- **50% faster rock respawns** - every rock in the guild respawns twice as fast as the same rock elsewhere
- **Unidentified minerals** - earned passively while mining. Used to buy mining gloves (40 minerals), superior mining gloves (100), and expert mining gloves (180 total). Also buys gem bags (20 each)
- **Bank chest** inside the members' area
- **Amethyst rocks** at 92 Mining (expanded area requires Falador Elite diary)

<Callout type="tip">
You need 180 unidentified minerals total for expert mining gloves. Mine iron ore in the guild (1/100 mineral chance per ore) while working toward them. At higher Mining levels, coal (1/60), mithril (1/40), and adamantite (1/30) give minerals faster.
</Callout>

## Early Game (1-45)

### Quests (1-37)

Skip the early grind entirely:
- **[Doric's Quest](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Doric%27s_Quest)** - Level 10
- **[Plague City](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Plague_City)** - Level 18
- **[The Dig Site](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Dig_Site)** - Level 37

Total: 27,525 XP for minimal effort.

### Power Mining Iron (37-45)

- **Where:** Mining Guild (60 Mining) or any iron spot
- **Why:** Fast XP, work towards expert mining gloves
- **Tip:** Mine in the Mining Guild once unlocked - faster respawns and you earn unidentified minerals

## Mid Game (45-70)

You have choices here based on what you need:

### Option 1: Motherlode Mine

- **XP/hr:** 30-50k
- **Effort:** Low (but not truly AFK)
- **Why do it:** Prospector kit, coal bag, gem bag, ores for Blast Furnace
- **When to stop:** Once you have the unlocks (around 180 nuggets for prospector + 200 for bags)

<Callout type="casual">
MLM is comfortable but ores are less valuable than you'd think. You'll buy most smithing supplies with GP later.
</Callout>

### Option 2: Sandstone (for Crafting)

- **XP/hr:** 50-70k (with tick manipulation)
- **Effort:** Medium-High
- **Why do it:** Buckets of sand for giant seaweed - 99 Crafting
- **How much:** Mining to ~80 gives enough sand for 99 Crafting

<Callout type="efficiency">
Sandstone is incredible value. You're effectively training Mining AND banking 99 Crafting at the same time.
</Callout>

### Option 3: Shooting Stars

- **XP/hr:** 20-35k
- **Effort:** Very Low (7+ min AFK per layer)
- **Why do it:** Celestial ring (2,000 dust), then gem bags (300 dust each) for passive Crafting XP
- **When:** Anytime you want true AFK - working, watching TV, etc.

### Option 4: Calcified Rocks (41+)

- **XP/hr:** 30-50k (up to 94k with tick manipulation)
- **Effort:** Low-Medium (~70 sec per vein)
- **Why do it:** Passive Prayer XP (10-15k/hr) via blessed bone shards
- **Best for:** Pre-70 Prayer push. Less valuable once you have Piety.

<Callout type="tip">
Calcified rocks are amazing if you're struggling to hit 70 Prayer for Piety. After that, the Prayer XP becomes less impactful.
</Callout>

### Option 5: Blast Mine (43+)

- **XP/hr:** 55-88k (scales with level)
- **Effort:** Medium (chisel, dynamite, tinderbox required)
- **Why do it:** Ores 10 levels above your Mining level - runite ore at 75 instead of 85
- **How it works:** Chisel a cavity in a hard rock, place dynamite, light it, move away, collect blasted ore

| Your Level | Ores You Get |
|-----------|-------------|
| 43-54 | Coal, gold |
| 55-59 | + Mithril |
| 60-69 | + Adamantite |
| 75+ | + Runite |

<Callout type="casual">
Blast Mine isn't popular - most ironmen skip it entirely in favour of MLM, sandstone, or shooting stars. The main appeal is getting runite ore 10 levels early, but if you don't specifically need runite ore, there's no reason to go here. Dynamite costs 575gp from Thirus's shop in Lovakengj, or you can claim 20-80 free daily from the Kourend diary.
</Callout>

## Late Game (70-92)

### Volcanic Mine (85+)

- **XP/hr:** 68-79k
- **Effort:** Medium (requires teams)
- **Why do it:** Best XP rates, chance at broken dragon pickaxe from ore packs
- **Tip:** Get prospector kit first

### Gem Rocks (with Karamja Hard diary)

- **XP/hr:** 50-70k
- **Effort:** Medium-High
- **Why do it:** Gems for jewellery (slayer bracelets, teleport jewellery, dragonstones)
- **Where:** Underground Shilo Village mine (requires diary for deposit box)

### Blast Mine (75+)

Still an option here if you specifically need runite ore early. At 75+ Mining you're getting runite regularly, and XP rates hit 65-80k/hr. Most players prefer other methods though.

### Continued Shooting Stars / Calcified

Both remain viable to 92 if you prefer the AFK lifestyle or want the secondary rewards.

## Endgame (92-99)

There are faster methods to 99 - Volcanic Mine, shooting stars, even gem rocks. But here's the thing: once you hit 99 Mining, you're probably never mining again. Amethyst is the one resource you'll want a massive stockpile of for endgame PvM.

### Amethyst

- **XP/hr:** ~20k
- **Effort:** Very Low (75 sec respawn)
- **Why do it:** Amethyst darts for blowpipe, amethyst arrows for tbow
- **How much:** 92-99 gives ~27k amethyst

<Callout type="efficiency">
You'll burn through amethyst darts at Zulrah, hydra, and general slayer. Amethyst arrows get used at every tbow boss. Getting 99 here means you're set for thousands of hours of PvM - rush 99 another way and you'll eventually have to come back anyway.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Bring a chisel and gem bag. Cut amethyst into dart tips as you mine - saves banking and gives Crafting XP.
</Callout>

### Expert Mining Gloves & Varrock Armour

These two items stack for massive amethyst efficiency:

- **Expert Mining Gloves** - Prevent ore depletion 1-3 times per session. Get them from Mining Guild before parking at amethyst.
- **[Varrock Armour 4](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Varrock_armour_4)** - Reward from the Elite Varrock Diary. Gives a 10% chance of mining 2 ores at once (granting XP for both). Only tier 4 works on amethyst - lower tiers cap at gold (tier 1), lovakite (tier 2), or adamantite (tier 3). Note: doesn't work at Motherlode Mine, on gem rocks, or while Blast Mining.

Combine both and you're getting significantly more amethyst per hour with minimal extra effort. Varrock Armour 4 adds roughly 10% more ore yield over time - meaningful across a long 92-99 grind.

## Zalcano

[Zalcano](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zalcano) is a skilling boss unlocked after [Song of the Elves](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Song_of_the_Elves) (requires 70 Mining, Smithing, and five other skills at 70). It's not a traditional mining training method, but it's one of the most valuable resource-generating activities in the game for ironmen.

### Why Ironmen Do Zalcano

The Mining XP itself is modest (~13.5k/hr with resources on), but the drops are huge:

| Drop Category | Examples | Why It Matters |
|--------------|---------|---------------|
| **Noted ores** | Gold (86-756), mithril, adamantite, runite | Bulk ores for Smithing/Blast Furnace |
| **Noted bars** | Mithril, adamantite, runite bars | Skip smelting, use at Giants' Foundry |
| **Gems** | Uncut diamonds, dragonstones | Crafting, jewellery, bolt tips |
| **Onyx bolt tips** | 1-45 per drop (2/36 chance) | Extremely hard to get otherwise |
| **Runes** | Blood, death, nature, law, soul, cosmic | Keeps you stocked for PvM and alchs |
| **Crystal shards** | 1-3 guaranteed per kill | Crystal tools, divine potions, crystal armour |

### Crystal Tool Seed (1/200)

Zalcano is the only source of the [crystal tool seed](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Crystal_tool_seed) for ironmen. You need this to make the crystal pickaxe, crystal axe, or crystal harpoon - all best-in-slot skilling tools. Most ironmen get one within 100-200 KC.

### How the Fight Works

1. Mine tephra from glowing rocks in the corners
2. Refine it at the furnace (Smithing XP)
3. Imbue it at the altar (Runecraft XP)
4. Throw imbued tephra at Zalcano to break her 300 HP stone armour
5. Once armour breaks, mine Zalcano directly with your pickaxe for ~12-16 seconds
6. She rises with a shockwave, armour resets, repeat until dead

**Avoid:** Falling boulders (move 1 tile), orange symbols (escalating damage). **Stand on:** Blue symbols (50% more damage to her armour). Kill golems if they spawn - they heal her armour.

### Optimal Setup

- **Group size:** 3-5 players is ideal. Smaller groups mean more contribution per person and better loot. Large themed-world groups (~15 players) give faster kills per hour but lower individual loot.
- **Gear:** Dragon or crystal pickaxe, graceful, celestial ring, stamina potions, some food
- **KC target:** 100-300 KC covers most resource needs and gives a good shot at the crystal tool seed

<Callout type="tip">
You can toggle resources off by speaking to Rhiannon in Prifddinas. This boosts Mining XP from ~13.5k/hr to 50-90k/hr but you lose all the valuable resource drops. Most ironmen keep resources on - the drops are too good to skip.
</Callout>

<Callout type="hcim">
Zalcano is one of the safest bosses in the game. Attacks are telegraphed and avoidable. The only real danger is not paying attention to falling boulders. Bring food just in case but deaths here are rare.
</Callout>

## Pickaxe Upgrades

### Dragon Pickaxe

Getting a d-pick is optional but nice (~5-6% faster than rune):

| Source | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Wilderness bosses (Callisto, Venenatis, etc.) | Best drop rate but risky |
| Kalphite Queen | Safer but longer kills |
| King Black Dragon | Very rare (1/1500) |
| Volcanic Mine ore packs | 1% chance per 4,000 point pack |

### Crystal Pickaxe

The [crystal pickaxe](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Crystal_pickaxe) is the best pickaxe in the game - about 3% faster than dragon. To make one:

1. **Crystal tool seed** - 1/200 from Zalcano
2. **Dragon pickaxe** - from sources above
3. **120 crystal shards** - from Zalcano, Gauntlet, pickpocketing elves, etc.
4. **Singing bowl** in Prifddinas - requires 76 Smithing and 76 Crafting (boostable)

The crystal pickaxe degrades (10,000 charges, 1 per ore mined) but reverts to dragon pickaxe speed at 0 charges rather than breaking. Recharge with crystal shards - 100 charges per shard. It also gives +2 max hit when mining Zalcano vs +1 from dragon pickaxe.

<Callout type="casual">
Don't stress about getting a crystal pickaxe early. The 3% speed increase is marginal. If you're doing Zalcano for the resources anyway, you'll likely get the seed naturally. Treat it as a bonus, not a goal.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
You can boost from 90 to 92 Mining with a celestial ring + dwarven stout(m) to start amethyst earlier.
</Callout>

## Common Mistakes

- **Grinding MLM for ores** - You'll buy bars with GP from Blast Furnace shop. MLM is for the unlocks, not the ores.
- **Skipping Celestial Ring** - +4 invisible boost is too good. Get it early.
- **Ignoring Sandstone** - Mining sandstone effectively banks 99 Crafting. Don't skip it.
- **Calcified Rocks after 70 Prayer** - The Prayer XP is most valuable pre-Piety. After that, shooting stars or amethyst give better value.
- **Not getting Expert Mining Gloves** - These make amethyst way better. Worth the iron grind.
- **Skipping Zalcano** - If you've done Song of the Elves, do at least 100-200 KC. The noted ores, bars, gems, and crystal shards are too valuable to ignore.
- **Toggling Zalcano resources off** - Unless you're purely after XP, keep resources on. The drops are worth far more than the extra Mining XP.
- **Not knowing about Blast Mine** - It's niche and most players skip it, but if you specifically need runite ore before 85 Mining, Blast Mine gives it 10 levels early. Worth knowing about even if you never use it.

## FAQ

### What's the best AFK mining method for ironman?

Shooting stars. You can AFK for 7+ minutes per layer, and you get stardust for the celestial ring and gem bags. Amethyst is also extremely AFK (75 second respawn) but requires 92 Mining.

### Should I do MLM or shooting stars?

Both, but for different reasons. Do MLM until you have prospector kit, coal bag, and gem bag (~280 nuggets total). After that, shooting stars are better - more AFK, and stardust rewards are more useful long-term.

### When should I start amethyst?

At 92 Mining, or you can boost from 90 with celestial ring + dwarven stout(m). Don't rush to 92 with other methods just to start amethyst earlier - the XP rates are similar and you'll want the full 92-99 grind to build your stockpile.

### Is the dragon pickaxe worth getting?

It's a ~5-6% speed increase over rune. Nice to have, not essential. If you're doing Wilderness bosses anyway, great. Don't grind specifically for it - you'll eventually get one from Volcanic Mine ore packs or bossing.

### Is 3-tick granite worth it?

Only if you want fast XP and don't care about secondary rewards. Most ironmen prefer methods that give useful resources (sandstone for Crafting, calcified rocks for Prayer, amethyst for ammo). Pure XP grinding is rarely the ironman way.

### Should I do Zalcano for Mining XP?

Not primarily. The Mining XP with resources on is only ~13.5k/hr. You do Zalcano for the drops - bulk ores, bars, gems, onyx bolt tips, and the crystal tool seed. If you want Mining XP, Volcanic Mine (68-79k/hr) or Blast Mine (55-88k/hr) are much faster. Zalcano is a resource activity that happens to give Mining XP.

### How do I get a crystal pickaxe?

Crystal tool seed (1/200 from Zalcano) + dragon pickaxe + 120 crystal shards at a singing bowl in Prifddinas. Needs 76 Smithing and 76 Crafting (boostable). The 3% speed boost over dragon is marginal - treat it as a natural upgrade from Zalcano farming rather than something to grind for.
