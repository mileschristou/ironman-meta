Getting the herbs is one thing - finding the secondaries is often the real bottleneck. Here's where to get everything you need for herblore training.

**At a glance:**
- **Easy:** Eye of newt, limpwurt, chocolate dust, unicorn horn dust, goat horn dust
- **Medium:** Snape grass, white berries, mort myre fungus, red spiders' eggs, blue dragon scales
- **Annoying:** Wine of zamorak, crushed nests, amylase crystals
- **Varlamore:** Aldarium, squid paste, crab paste, yellow fin, haddock eye

---

## Easy (Buy or Minimal Effort)

### Eye of Newt
- **Used for:** Attack potions, Super attacks
- **Where:** Buy from magic shops (Port Sarim, etc.) - stock of 10+ per world
- **Cost:** 3 GP each

### Limpwurt Root
- **Used for:** Strength potions, Super strengths
- **Where:** [Farm them](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Limpwurt_root) (26 Farming), or kill Hobgoblins/Hill Giants
- **Tip:** Do herb runs with limpwurt - they stack up fast

### Chocolate Dust
- **Used for:** Energy potions
- **Where:** Buy chocolate bars from cooking shops, use pestle and mortar
- **Cost:** ~10 GP each

### Unicorn Horn Dust
- **Used for:** Antipoisons, Super antipoisons
- **Where:** Kill unicorns, grind horns
- **Tip:** [Tower of Life](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tower_of_Life) method if you need bulk

### Goat Horn Dust
- **Used for:** Combat potions
- **Where:** Kill goats near Nardah or Keldagrim
- **Tip:** Easy to stockpile, often overlooked

### Volcanic Ash
- **Used for:** Ultracompost
- **Where:** [Mine on Fossil Island](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Volcanic_ash)
- **Tip:** You'll need a lot - grab some whenever you're on the island

## Medium (Some Setup Required)

### Snape Grass
- **Used for:** Prayer potions
- **Where:** [Farm them](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Snape_grass) (61 Farming) or pick spawns on Waterbirth Island
- **Tip:** Start farming these early - you'll need hundreds for prayer pots

<Callout type="warning">
This is the big one. Prayer potions are essential and snape grass is the bottleneck. Get 61 Farming and start farming them as soon as possible.
</Callout>

### Red Spiders' Eggs
- **Used for:** Restore potions, Super restores
- **Where:** [Tower of Life](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tower_of_Life) (spawn them with raw sardine + red spiders' eggs), or Nightmare Zone
- **Tip:** Tower of Life is the ironman standard

### White Berries
- **Used for:** Super defence potions
- **Where:** [Farm them](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/White_berries) (59 Farming for bush, 83 for the better patch), or pick in Isafdar/Red Dragon Isle
- **Tip:** Farming is the long-term solution

### Mort Myre Fungus
- **Used for:** Super energy potions
- **Where:** Cast Bloom with silver sickle (b) in Mort Myre Swamp
- **Requirements:** Nature Spirit quest, prayer points to spam Bloom
- **Tip:** Bring prayer pots or use Morytania Legs 3 for prayer restore

### Blue Dragon Scales
- **Used for:** Antifire potions
- **Where:** Kill blue dragons (Taverley Dungeon, Myths Guild), or pick spawns and grind
- **Tip:** Myths Guild basement is best for killing - blue and green dragons together

### Potato Cactus
- **Used for:** Magic potions
- **Where:** [Farm them](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Potato_cactus) (64 Farming) or Kalphite Lair spawns
- **Tip:** Farming is much better than running to Kalphites

## Annoying (But Essential)

### Wine of Zamorak
- **Used for:** Ranging potions, Super ranging potions
- **Where:** Telegrab at Chaos Temple (low level), or [Chaos Fanatic](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chaos_Fanatic) drops (higher level)
- **Tip:** Telegrab is slow but safe. Chaos Fanatic is faster but risky.

<Callout type="tip">
Don't grind this early - wait until you actually need ranging pots. You'll make yourself miserable telegrabing wines at level 30.
</Callout>

### Crushed Nests
- **Used for:** Saradomin brews
- **Where:** [Bird house runs](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bird_house_trapping) (passive), [Kingdom of Miscellania](/guides/miscellania/) with workers on maple logs (~8-9 nests/day), [Giant Mole](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Giant_Mole) (active), Wyson the Gardener (trade mole parts)
- **Tip:** Start bird houses early - nests stack up passively over time

<Callout type="efficiency">
Bird houses are one of the best passive activities in the game. 5 minutes every 50 minutes for Hunter XP + nests. Start these immediately after Bone Voyage.
</Callout>

### Amylase Crystals
- **Used for:** Stamina potions
- **Where:** Marks of grace from rooftop courses, or [Hallowed Sepulchre](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hallowed_Sepulchre)
- **Tip:** Hallowed Sepulchre is more efficient if you're 72+ Agility. Otherwise, collect marks during normal Agility training

### Lava Scale Shards
- **Used for:** Extended antifire potions
- **Where:** Kill Lava dragons (Wilderness)
- **Tip:** Wilderness only. Bring minimal risk gear, expect to die occasionally

## Varlamore Secondaries

### Aldarium
- **Used for:** Goading potion, Prayer regeneration potion
- **Where:** Buy from [Mastering Mixology](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mastering_Mixology) shop in Aldarin
- **Cost:** Mox/Aga/Lye points from the minigame
- **Tip:** Do some Mixology anyway for the rewards - Aldarium is a bonus

### Squid Paste / Crab Paste / Rainbow Crab Paste
- **Used for:** Haemostatic items, Super hunter potion, Armadyl brew
- **Where:** Hunter Guild creatures, process with pestle and mortar
- **Tip:** You'll get these naturally from [Hunter rumours](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hunter_rumours)

### Yellow Fin / Haddock Eye
- **Used for:** Extreme energy potion, Super fishing potion
- **Where:** Sailing activities, fishing
- **Tip:** These are niche - don't grind specifically unless you need the potions

### Marlin Scales
- **Used for:** Extended stamina potions
- **Where:** Sailing
- **Tip:** Late-game content, don't worry about it early

## Common Mistakes

- **Not farming snape grass early** - you'll need hundreds for prayer pots, start at 61 Farming
- **Ignoring bird house runs** - crushed nests stack up passively, start after Bone Voyage
- **Grinding wine of zamorak too early** - wait until you actually need ranging pots
- **Forgetting volcanic ash** - ultracompost is essential for farming, grab it when you're on Fossil Island
