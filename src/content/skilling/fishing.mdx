Fishing on an ironman is about resource efficiency. You need food for PvM. You need Agility levels. You need training methods that don't waste clicks.

**At a glance:**
- **Quest speedrun (1-27):** 30 minutes, zero XP grind
- **Early AFK (27-58):** Fly fishing for trout/salmon, collects food
- **Mid game (58-70):** Barbarian fishing gains 73 Agility XP as bonus
- **Late game (65+):** [Karambwan](/supplies/food/) fishing - the meta (AFK + food + decent XP)
- **Key unlock:** Fishing Barrel (Tempoross drop) = 3-5 min AFK vs 1.5 min
- **Endgame:** Sacred eels for Zulrah scales + [Anglerfish](/supplies/food/) for overheal raids

---

## Early Game: Quest Speedrun (Level 1-27)

Don't grind early fishing. Use quests.

| Quest | XP | Level After |
|-------|----|----|
| **Sea Slug** | 7,175 | 24 |
| **Fishing Contest** | 2,437 | 26 |
| **RfD (Pirate Pete)** | ~1,000 | 27 |
| **Total** | ~10,612 | **27** |

<Callout type="tip">
Sea Slug requires 30 Firemaking (easy early grind). Fishing Contest requires 10 Fishing (do it after Sea Slug). Total time: 30 minutes with existing firemaking XP.
</Callout>

## Early-Mid Game: Fly Fishing (Level 27-58)

### Basic Fly Fishing
- **Levels:** 27-58
- **Location:** Southeast of Prifddinas gate (bank nearby)
- **Equipment:** Fly fishing rod + feathers (~5,000 needed total)
- **XP Rates:** 30,000-42,000 XP/hour depending on level
- **AFK Duration:** 2-4 minutes per session
- **Food Output:** Trout (20 HP), Salmon (40 HP) - useful [cooking](/skilling/cooking/) materials (see [Food Guide](/supplies/food/) for usage)

**Why this stage:**
- Pure AFK - drop feathers, click fishing spot, do something else
- Collects tradeable food for later
- No quest requirements or unlocks needed

## Mid Game: Barbarian Fishing (Level 58-70+)

Barbarian Fishing is the most efficient path to 99 **because of Agility XP gains**, not raw fishing XP.

### The Math on Agility XP

At **Level 58 Barbarian Fishing:**
- Fishing XP: 37,000 XP/hour
- **Agility XP: 3,400 XP/hour** (gained simultaneously)
- Strength XP: 3,400 XP/hour (bonus)

**To 99 Fishing (58-99):** ~40-50 hours of Barbarian Fishing gives you:
- 99 Fishing ✓
- **73 Agility levels gained for free** (from 1 → 73+)
- 73 Strength levels gained (bonus)

This is the equivalent of skipping Agility entirely. Normally Agility 1-73 takes 30+ hours. You get it passively.

### Barbarian Fishing Setup

- **Minimum Level:** 15 Fishing (but start at 48+ for Leaping Sturgeon - better food)
- **Location:** Barbarian Village (south of Varrock)
- **Equipment:** Fly fishing rod + feathers
- **Sturdy Boots:** Required to fish Leaping Sturgeon

### Optimal Progression

| Level | Food Type | XP/hr | Agility XP/hr |
|-------|-----------|-------|---------------|
| 48-58 | Leaping Sturgeon | 23,000 | 2,300 |
| 58-70 | Leaping Sturgeon | 37,000 | 3,400 |
| 70-99 | Leaping Sturgeon | 57,000 | 5,200 |

### When to Stop Barbarian Fishing

**Optimal cutoff: Level 70-75 Agility**

At this point, Agility XP becomes less valuable relative to pure Fishing XP gains. You have two options:

1. **Continue Barbarian to 99** → Gain 73 Agility levels total (best for Agility)
2. **Switch to Karambwan at 65** → Better food, more AFK, slightly less Agility XP (best for Ironman efficiency)

<Callout type="efficiency">
Most efficient Ironman route: Barbarian 58-70 (gain 46 Agility levels), then Karambwan 70-99 (ultra-AFK food). This gives you significant Agility gains without grinding it separately.
</Callout>

## [Tempoross](/guides/tempoross/) & Fishing Barrel

The **Fishing Barrel** is the unlock that changes everything about late-game fishing.

### What is Tempoross?

[Tempoross](/guides/tempoross/) is a minigame where you fight the Spirit of the Sea (scales to solo/group). It's not about combat efficiency - it's about the **Fishing Barrel drop.**

- **Requirement:** 35+ Fishing
- **Location:** Sea Spirit Dock (south of Al Kharid)
- **Reward Rate:** 1 in 400 attempts (brutal RNG)

### Why You Need the Barrel

| Condition | AFK Duration | Inventory | Banking |
|-----------|--------------|-----------|---------|
| Without Barrel | 1.5 min | 28 fish | Every 1.5 min |
| With Barrel | 3-5 min | Unlimited | Every 3-5 min |

The barrel eliminates inventory management. **3-5x longer AFK sessions** = massively better for grinding.

### Other Tempoross Rewards

- **Spirit Angler's Outfit:** +2.5% Fishing XP on all fishing (permanent, highly recommended)
- **Fishing Supplies:** Various equipment drops
- **Crates:** Food and supplies crates

### The Grind

Expect 200-400 games for the barrel. This is soul-crushing RNG, but it's the only way to unlock late-game AFK.

<Callout type="warning">
The Fishing Barrel grind is painful. Many Ironmen skip it and deal with 1.5-min AFK sessions instead. But if you plan to fish Karambwan for 100+ hours, the barrel saves significant clicking time.
</Callout>

## Late Game: Karambwan Fishing (Level 65-99)

This is the meta. Karambwan fishing is **self-sustaining food production while training.** See [Food Guide](/supplies/food/) for detailed karambwan strategy.

### Setup

- **Level:** 65 Fishing + 30 Cooking
- **Quest:** Tai Bwo Wannai Trio (Fishing portion only)
- **Location:** North of Karamja Shipyard
- **Equipment:** Karambwan Vessel (quest reward)
- **Bait:** Karambwanji (caught at fairy ring CKR)

### XP Rates

| Setup | XP/hour |
|-------|---------|
| Base Karambwan | 35,000 |
| + Fishing Barrel | 38,000 |
| + Angler's Outfit | 40,000 |
| + Barrel + Outfit + 99 Fishing | 46,000 |

### Banking Methods

**Fastest route:**
1. Karamja Gloves 3 teleport → Shilo Village bank
2. Quest Point teleport → Fairy ring DKP
3. Back to Karambwan spot

**Total banking time:** 2-3 minutes (efficient enough with barrel)

### Why It's Meta

1. **Food Output:** 900 fish/hour × 18 HP healing = 16,200 HP/hour of food
2. **XP Rate:** 35-46k is respectable for AFK
3. **Self-Sustaining:** Catches bait (Karambwanji) while fishing
4. **Duration:** Viable from 65 all the way to 99
5. **Banking:** Karamja Gloves teleport is one of the fastest in the game

<Callout type="tip">
Karambwans are your Ironman food source for the next 100+ hours. By the time you're done fishing, you'll have thousands of cooked karambwans stockpiled for [Slayer](/skilling/slayer/), bossing, and PvM content. See [Food Guide](/supplies/food/) for combo-eating strategy.
</Callout>

## Alternative Methods (Monkfish, Anglerfish)

### Monkfish (Level 62-99)

- **Location:** Piscatoris Fishing Colony
- **XP Rate:** 15-20k XP/hour (slow but AFK)
- **Food Value:** 16 HP healing (worse than Karambwan)
- **Banking:** Fairy ring AKQ (fast)

**When to use:** Pre-65 Karambwan, or if you want variation. Not recommended as primary method.

### Anglerfish (Level 82-99) - Essential for Late-Game PvM

The most important late-game food for survival in raids and endgame PvM. Not about XP - it's about **overheal mechanics and endgame preparation.**

#### Setup & Rates

- **Location:** Port Piscarilius (Piscarilius House, requires 100% house favor)
- **Equipment:** Fishing rod + sandworms (buyable at spot)
- **XP Rate:** 15-23k XP/hour (very slow, not the focus)
- **Catch Rate:** 75-150 fish/hour with sandworms
- **AFK Duration:** Inconsistent (4-8+ minutes between clicks)
- **Banking:** Deposit box nearby, convenient for inventory management

#### Anglerfish Healing Properties

Unlike other foods, anglerfish scales with your HP level:

| HP Level | Heals | Use Case |
|----------|-------|----------|
| 80-89 | 16 HP | Mid-game (similar to monkfish) |
| 90-92 | 17 HP | Pre-99 |
| **93-99** | **22 HP** | Endgame raids/bosses |

**Unique Feature: Overheal**
- Can heal **above maximum HP** up to the amount restored
- At 99 HP level: Can reach 121 total HP (21 HP overheal buffer)
- **Critical for survivability:** Overheal acts as a safety cushion in long raids (ToB, CoX, Inferno)
- **Note:** Overheal does NOT apply in PvP areas

#### Why Anglerfish Over Karambwan for Late-Game?

**Anglerfish Strengths:**
- 22 HP heal (vs karambwan's 18 HP)
- Overheal mechanic provides survival buffer
- Better for endurance encounters (long bossing, raids)

**Karambwan Strengths:**
- Combo-eating: Eat shark (20 HP) + karambwan (18 HP) = 38 HP in one tick
- Better for DPS-critical content (Inferno, phase-based bosses)

**Optimal Late-Game Strategy:**
Use **both** - [Karambwan](/supplies/food/) for DPS phases, [Anglerfish](/supplies/food/) for overheal buffer and recovery. Most raiders bring 70% karambwan + 30% anglerfish. See [Food Guide](/supplies/food/) for detailed combo-eating and raid food strategy.

#### Yama's Diabolic Worms (New Drop)

Yama boss (accessed post-Kingdom Divided quest) drops **Diabolic Worms** which significantly boost anglerfish catch rates:

**Mechanics:**
- **Drop rate:** 7.4% from Yama
- **Quantity:** 90 worms per drop
- **Effect:** Next fishing catch occurs 50% faster
- **Usage:** One-time consumable per catch

**Impact on Rates:**
- **Without worms:** 75-150 fish/hour
- **With worms:** 300-450 fish/hour (3x improvement)
- **Trade-off:** XP drops from 120 → 79.2 per catch

**Ironman Value:**
Diabolic worms are economically negative for profit, but **excellent for quickly stockpiling anglerfish** when you need high quantities before raiding. Can combine sandworms + diabolic worms for flexibility.

#### Stockpiling for Raids

Most ironmen aim for:
- **Per raid tier:** 50-100+ cooked anglerfish
- **Before serious raiding:** 1,000+ total stockpile
- **Cooking requirement:** 84 Cooking (use Cooking Gauntlets to reduce burn rate)

<Callout type="warning">
Anglerfish fishing (82-99) is **NOT AFK** like karambwan. AFK duration is inconsistent and unreliable. Plan for semi-active fishing or treat it as downtime activity. However, the overheal mechanic makes it essential for raid preparation.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
Start anglerfish fishing at 82 as soon as possible. By the time you reach 99 Fishing, you'll have thousands stockpiled for endgame content. Post-Yama, use Diabolic Worms if you need to accelerate stockpiling before specific raid goals.
</Callout>

## Optimal Ironman Progression

### Fastest Route to 99 (Pure XP)

1. **Levels 1-27:** Quests (30 min)
2. **Levels 27-58:** Fly fishing (5 hours, AFK)
3. **Levels 58-99:** Barbarian fishing (45 hours, gains 73 Agility)
4. **Total:** 50 hours
5. **Bonus:** 73 free Agility levels (skip Agility training entirely)

### Best All-Around Route (Food + XP)

1. **Levels 1-27:** Quests (30 min)
2. **Levels 27-58:** Fly fishing (5 hours, AFK, collects food)
3. **Levels 58-70:** Barbarian fishing (10 hours, gains 46 Agility)
4. **Levels 70-99:** Karambwan fishing (40 hours, ultra-AFK with barrel, self-sustaining food)
5. **Total:** 60 hours
6. **Bonus:** 46 Agility levels + thousands of food for PvM

### Most AFK Route (Minimal Clicks)

1. **Levels 1-27:** Quests (30 min)
2. **Levels 27-62:** Fly fishing + Monkfish (10 hours, pure AFK)
3. **Levels 62-99:** Karambwan fishing with barrel (80 hours, 3-5 min AFK sessions)
4. **Total:** 90 hours
5. **Bonus:** Minimal attention, consistent food output

## Endgame: Sacred Eels for Zulrah Scales (Level 87+)

Not the fastest fishing method, but an essential **passive activity for scale generation.**

### Why Sacred Eels?

Late-game ironman requires scales to charge critical gear (used in endgame [food](/supplies/food/) strategy):
- **Serpentine Helmet:** 11,000 scales
- **Toxic Blowpipe:** 3,063,621 scales (full capacity)
- **Trident of the Swamp:** 1 scale per cast
- **Toxic Staff of the Dead:** Scales for charging

Rather than repeatedly killing Zulrah boss, sacred eels let you accumulate scales passively while training Fishing and Cooking.

### Sacred Eel Fishing Setup

- **Level:** 87 Fishing + 72 Cooking (dissecting requirement)
- **Location:** Zul-Andra (south of Chasm of Fire, requires Regicide quest)
- **Equipment:** Fishing rod + fishing bait
- **Processing:** Dissect eels with knife for Zulrah scales (3-9 scales per eel)

### Scale Yields

| Fishing Level | Cooking Level | Scales/Hour | XP/Hour (Fishing) | XP/Hour (Cooking) |
|---------------|---------------|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| 90 | 72 | ~1,200 | 21,300 | 16,000 |
| 97 | 99 | ~1,500 | 23,500 | 26,400 |

**With Chef's Delight boost (Cooking +8):** Can achieve 8+ scales per eel at 99 Cooking.

### AFK Strategy

Sacred eels are **not actively AFK while fishing**, but the process is very passive:

1. **Fish for 30-40 minutes** at Zul-Andra (decent attention required to avoid depleting the spot)
2. **Store eels** in bank or inventory for batch processing
3. **Dissect at bank** when convenient (multiple eels at once)
4. **Repeat** - total scales accumulate passively over weeks

**Click frequency:** Normal fishing clicks required, but dissecting can be done whenever convenient.

### When You Need It

- **Early:** Skip sacred eels, focus on Zulrah boss kills for scales (easier)
- **Mid-Game (88+ Fishing):** Start sacred eel fishing to passively accumulate scales
- **Late-Game:** Combine with boss kills - eels for steady passive income, boss for peaks when needed

### Cost-Benefit Analysis

| Method | Scales/Hour | XP Gain | Attention | Total Hours for 50k scales |
|--------|-------------|---------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Sacred Eels (87+ Fishing) | 1,200-1,500 | Dual (Fishing + Cooking) | Low-Medium | 33-42 hours |
| Zulrah Boss | 220 avg/kill | None | High | 227+ kills (~200+ hours) |

**Verdict:** Sacred eels are far superior for passive scale generation. Zulrah boss kills become worthwhile only for unique drops or if you want scales faster (at the cost of attention/difficulty).

<Callout type="tip">
Sacred eels are the ironman's secret weapon for scale management. You don't need to boss farm scales - catch eels during AFK fishing sessions, dissect when convenient, and let scales accumulate passively. By the time you've trained Fishing to 99, you'll have 50,000+ scales without touching Zulrah.
</Callout>

## FAQ

**How long to 99 Fishing?**
- Barbarian route (best Agility): 50-60 hours
- Karambwan route (best food): 60-80 hours
- AFK route: 90-120 hours

**Can I skip Barbarian Fishing?**
Yes, but you lose free Agility XP. Go Fly fish → Karambwan instead (still good, just less efficient overall).

**Is the Barrel grind worth it?**
For 100+ hours of Karambwan: Yes. You save 40+ hours of clicking. For shorter grinds: No, just deal with 1.5-min AFK.

**When do I stop fishing?**
When you reach 99 or when you have enough food stockpiled (typically 500+ cooked Karambwans).

<Callout type="efficiency">
By 99 Fishing, if you went Barbarian 58-70 then Karambwan 70-99, you'll have: 99 Fishing, 70+ Agility, 70+ Strength, and 5,000+ cooked Karambwans for the next 500 hours of PvM.
</Callout>
