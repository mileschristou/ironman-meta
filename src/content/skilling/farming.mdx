Farming is essential for ironmen. You can't buy seeds or herbs from the Grand Exchange, so efficient farming provides critical resources for herblore and consistent XP with minimal time investment.

**At a glance:**
- **Best early method:** Quest to 38 (10 hours, free XP)
- **Essential unlocks:** Magic secateurs (+10% yield), bottomless bucket
- **Recommended:** Tithe Farm for auto-weed, seed box, herb sack (optional but very helpful)
- **Core training:** Farming contracts + daily tree/herb runs to 99
- **Time to 99:** 3-4 months with 15-30 minutes daily effort

---

## Why Farming Matters

**Resource generation:**
- Herb runs provide ranarr (prayer potions), snapdragon (super restores), torstol (super combats)
- Consistent herb supply is essential for all PvM content
- Cannot be skipped on ironman accounts

**Efficient XP:**
- Tree runs: 150,000-250,000+ XP per day in 10-15 minutes
- Most time-efficient skill in OSRS (XP per active hour)
- Passive growth while you do other activities

**Quest/Diary requirements:**
- 70 Farming for Song of the Elves (cannot boost)
- 85 for Farming Guild advanced tier (fruit tree patch, contracts)
- 90 for Elite Ardougne diary (can boost with +6 garden pie)

## Tithe Farm (34→62): Essential Unlocks

After questing to 38, do Tithe Farm to unlock critical rewards and reach level 62.

<Callout type="tip">
**Tithe Farm is optional but highly recommended.** You can skip it and go straight to tree runs + farming contracts if you want. However, the rewards (especially auto-weed, seed box, herb sack) make farming significantly more convenient. Most players find the 10-12 hour investment worth it for permanent quality-of-life improvements.
</Callout>

### Location and Requirements

- **Location:** Hosidius, Zeah (requires 100% favour or Architectural Alliance miniquest)
- **Minimum level:** 34 Farming (Golovanova seeds)
- **Better seeds:** 54 (Bologano), 74 (Logavano)

### How It Works

Plant seeds in 20 available patches, water them before each growth stage (3 times over 3 minutes), harvest, and deposit fruits in sacks for points. Plants wilt if not watered on time.

### XP Rates

- **Golovanova** (34+): 6 XP per fruit
- **Bologano** (54+): 14 XP per fruit
- **Logavano** (74+): 23 XP per fruit
- **Overall:** 90,000-100,000 XP/hour at 74+

### Essential Rewards (Priority Order)

| Reward | Cost | Priority | Why You Need It |
|--------|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Auto-weed** | 50 | **1st** | **Prevents weeds on ALL patches forever** |
| **Seed box** | 250 | **2nd** | Stores 6 seed types, essential for runs |
| **Herb sack** | 250 | **3rd** | Stores 30 of each herb type |
| Farmer's outfit | 400 total | 4th | 2.5% Farming XP boost |
| Gricoller's can | 200 | Optional | Holds 1,000 water (convenience) |

**Total for essentials:** 950 points = ~10-12 hours at Tithe Farm

<Callout type="efficiency">
Auto-weed is game-changing. Once unlocked, you never need a rake again. Patches stay clear of weeds permanently. Get this first, even before the XP boost outfit.
</Callout>

## Farming Contracts (65→99): The Meta

Farming contracts are the core of efficient ironman farming training from 65-99.

### How They Work

1. Enter Farming Guild (65 Farming for intermediate tier, 85 for advanced)
2. Talk to Guildmaster Jane to receive a contract
3. Grow the assigned crop within the Farming Guild patches
4. Check health or harvest to complete
5. Receive seed pack reward + XP

**Key detail:** The same crop won't be assigned twice in a row.

### Difficulty Tiers

| Tier | Level | Patches Available | Can Downgrade? |
|------|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| Easy | 45 | Limited | No |
| Medium | 65 | More variety | Yes (to easy) |
| **Hard** | **85** | **All patches** | Yes (to medium) |

**Always do hard contracts** (85+) for the best seed rewards.

### Pre-Planting Strategy (Critical!)

Pre-plant slow-growing crops **before** accepting contracts. When assigned that crop, instantly complete it instead of waiting for growth.

**What to Pre-Plant:**
- **Magic trees** (8 hours growth) - Very common contract
- **Dragonfruit trees** (16 hours) - Common hard contract
- **Redwood trees** (4.5 days) - Highest XP contract
- **Mahogany hardwoods** (3 days) - Good hard contract
- **Calquat trees** (21 hours) - Medium frequency
- **Celastrus** (13 hours) - Occasional contract

**Don't Pre-Plant:**
- Herbs (too fast, 80 minutes)
- Allotments/flowers (fast growth)
- Bushes (already have designated patches)

**How It Works:** Plant a magic tree, DON'T check its health. When assigned "magic tree" contract, immediately check health for instant completion, then plant another magic tree for next time.

<Callout type="warning">
Contracts are self-sustaining. Seed pack rewards give you more seeds than you use. Pre-planting lets you complete 5-10 contracts per day instead of waiting for each crop to grow.
</Callout>

### Contract Rewards

- **Seed packs:** Contain tree seeds, fruit tree seeds, herb seeds, hespori seeds
- **XP:** 10,000-20,000+ depending on crop tier
- **Best seed source** for ironmen (better than birdhouses, slayer, Wintertodt combined)

### Protection vs Ultracompost

**Always protect trees if you can afford payment.**

- Protection guarantees 100% survival
- Trees have long growth times (4-96 hours)
- Time cost of replanting failed trees outweighs payment cost

**Use ultracompost** only if:
- You don't have payment items
- You're willing to risk failure
- It's a low-value tree (oak, willow)

<Callout type="tip">

</Callout>

## Ultracompost: Essential for Ironmen

Ultracompost reduces disease chance to ~10% per growth stage and increases herb/allotment yields.

### How to Make Ultracompost

**Method 1: Volcanic Ash on Supercompost**
- Use 2 volcanic ash on bucket of supercompost
- Grants 1.1 Farming XP per bucket

**Method 2: Big Compost Bin (Recommended)**
- Requires 45 Farming
- Fill bin with supercompost (or compost ingredients)
- Add 50 volcanic ash
- Collect 30 buckets of ultracompost
- **Most efficient method**

**Method 3: Fertile Soil Spell**
- Requires 83 Magic, Lunar spellbook, ash covered tome
- Cast on patch with 2 volcanic ash in inventory
- Applies ultracompost without needing buckets
- Costs ~500-600 gp per cast

### Volcanic Ash Sources

1. **Mining ash piles** (Fossil Island Volcano)
   - Requires 22 Mining
   - Yields: 1 ash at 22 Mining → 6 ash at 97+
   - Rates: 6,500+ ash per hour at high levels
2. **Volcanic Mine** rewards (for ash covered tome)
3. **Wyvern Cave monsters** (Fossil Island)

### Supercompost Sources

- **Pineapples** - Buy from charter ships (20 gp each)
- **Watermelons** - Grow at allotments (47 Farming)
- **White lilies** - Harvest from flower patches
- **Moons of Peril** - Reward chest gives supercompost

### Bottomless Compost Bucket

**How to Obtain:** 1/35 drop from Hespori boss (65 Farming required)

**Benefits:**
- Holds 10,000 uses of one compost type
- Each bucket added gives 2 charges (doubles efficiency)
- Saves massive inventory space during runs
- **Essential endgame item**

**Strategy:** Fill with 2,500 buckets of ultracompost = 5,000 uses. Tool leprechauns can store one.

## Hespori: Critical Ironman Boss

Hespori is a solo farming boss in the Farming Guild that drops the bottomless bucket and anima seeds.

### How to Fight

1. Plant hespori seed in Hespori patch (west wing of Farming Guild)
2. Wait 22-32 hours for growth
3. Use spade on patch to enter fight

### Combat Strategy

- **HP:** 300
- **Weakness:** Fire spells (100%), slash weapons
- **Minimum stats:** 60+ melee with dragon scimitar, OR 59+ Magic with Fire Blast
- **Mechanics:**
  - Spawns 4 invulnerability flowers at 100%, 66%, 33% HP - kill with ranged/magic
  - Vine entanglement - spam click to escape within 6 seconds or take 40+ damage

### Critical Drops

**Anima Seeds (guaranteed, 1/3 each):**
- **Attas seed** - Increases yield on all patches worldwide
- **Iasor seed** - Decreases disease chance on all patches worldwide
- **Kronos seed** - Chance to skip growth stages on all patches worldwide

**Important Drops:**
- **Bottomless compost bucket** (1/35) - Essential item, keep trying until you get it
- Various herb/tree seeds
- Tangleroot pet (1/5,375 to 1/4,525)

**Frequency:** No cooldown - fight as often as you get hespori seeds (from farming contracts)

<Callout type="warning">
Keep fighting Hespori until you get the bottomless bucket. It saves thousands of inventory slots over your account's lifetime. This is non-negotiable for efficient farming.
</Callout>

## Anima Seeds: Worldwide Farming Buffs

Plant anima seeds in the anima patch (Farming Guild, 76 Farming) for global benefits.

### The Three Seeds

| Seed | Effect | Best Use |
|------|--------|----------|
| **Attas** | Increases crop yield | **Best overall** - more herbs, more resources |
| **Iasor** | Reduces disease chance | Good early if no bottomless bucket |
| **Kronos** | Skip growth stages | Marginal benefit (speeds trees slightly) |

**Key Details:**
- Growth time: ~85 hours (3.5 days)
- **Effects activate immediately upon planting**
- Effects increase as plant grows
- Immune to disease
- 100 XP upon planting

**Priority:** Iasor first (if no ultracompost) → Attas (maximum yields) → Kronos (minimal benefit)

**Endgame Strategy:** Keep Attas planted permanently for increased yields on all herb/tree runs.

## Seed Sources for Ironmen

### 1. Farming Contracts (Primary Source)

**Best for:** Tree seeds, fruit tree seeds, hespori seeds, celastrus seeds

- Do hard contracts (85+) for best rewards
- Pre-plant extensively for instant completions
- Self-sustaining once established

### 2. Birdhouse Runs (Passive Tree Seeds)

**Best for:** All tree/fruit tree seeds passively

- Requires Hunter skill, Crafting to make birdhouses
- Place 4 birdhouses on Fossil Island with any seeds
- Collect after 50 minutes for seed nests
- **Excellent passive method** - do between farm runs

### 3. Master Farmer Pickpocketing (Herb Seeds)

**Best for:** Ranarr, snapdragon, torstol herb seeds

- Requires 38 Thieving (94+ for 100% success with Hard Ardougne Diary)
- **Cannot obtain tree/fruit tree seeds**
- Rates: ~60 high-level herb seeds per hour
- Locations: Farming Guild (3 spawns), Hosidius, Draynor

### 4. Slayer (Supplementary)

- Nechryaels, turoths, kurasks drop herb seeds
- Dagannoths drop various seeds
- Slayer bosses drop high-level tree seeds

### 5. Wintertodt (Early-Game Only)

- Supply crate rewards scale with Farming level
- Good for early herb/tree seeds
- Not efficient long-term (contracts are better)

### 6. Kingdom of Miscellania

- Allocate workers to herb gathering
- Passive seed accumulation
- Better for herbs than seeds

## Strategic Advice for Ironmen

## FAQ

### What's the fastest way to train farming on an ironman?

Quest to 38 (10 hours), Tithe Farm to 62 for rewards (15-20 hours), then farming contracts with pre-planting plus daily tree runs. Reach 99 in 3-4 months with 15-30 minutes daily.

### Should I protect my trees or use ultracompost?

Always protect trees if you can afford payment items. Protection guarantees 100% survival, trees have long growth times, and replanting failed trees wastes more time than the protection cost.

### What should I pre-plant for farming contracts?

Pre-plant slow-growing crops: magic trees, dragonfruit trees, redwoods, mahogany hardwoods, calquats, celastrus. Don't check health - when assigned that contract, instantly complete it.

### How many herb runs should I do per day?

Aim for 2-4 herb runs daily (herbs grow in 80 minutes). Each run takes 10-15 minutes and provides critical herbs for herblore. The herb stockpile is more valuable than the Farming XP.

### What Tithe Farm rewards should I prioritize?

1) Auto-weed (50 points), 2) Seed box (250 points), 3) Herb sack (250 points), 4) Farmer's outfit (400 points). Total 950 points = ~10-12 hours.

### How do I get seeds as an ironman?

Farming contracts (best for tree seeds), birdhouse runs (passive tree seeds), Master Farmer pickpocketing (herb seeds), slayer (supplementary). Contracts become self-sustaining with pre-planting.

### When should I use Attas vs Iasor vs Kronos seeds?

Use Iasor first if you don't have reliable ultracompost. Once you have bottomless bucket, switch to Attas permanently for maximum yields. Kronos has minimal benefit.

### Is the bottomless compost bucket worth it?

Absolutely essential. Fight Hespori repeatedly until you get it (1/35 drop). Saves thousands of inventory slots over your account's lifetime and makes ultracompost sustainable.
